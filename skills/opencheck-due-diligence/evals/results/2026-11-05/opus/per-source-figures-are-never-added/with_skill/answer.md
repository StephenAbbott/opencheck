**The Norwegian state's total stake is about 67%, not 201%.** The three rows are not three separate holders whose stakes add up. They are three sources each reporting the same state holding, and each names a different part of the state as the holder. OpenCheck works out each source's figure separately and says these figures are "listed side by side, never added." All three sources agree on 67%.

The sources disagree only on which part of the state holds the shares. The answer doesn't settle that. As far as I know, the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Fisheries is currently responsible for the state's Equinor shares. Wikidata matches that, but the answer above can't confirm it. To settle it, check Equinor's own shareholder register or the Norwegian government's ownership report.

### Declared beneficial owners
- **None.** OpenCheck read no register that names beneficial owners for Equinor. That isn't evidence that no beneficial owner exists. Also, an owner that is a government body isn't a natural-person beneficial owner anyway.

### How to read these figures
- These are integrated-ownership figures: OpenCheck's arithmetic on the shareholdings the sources filed. They count shareholdings only, not voting rights or control. OpenCheck calls them "context, not a finding". They don't establish beneficial ownership.
- The lookup went one layer up the ownership chain, with no hop limit reached.
