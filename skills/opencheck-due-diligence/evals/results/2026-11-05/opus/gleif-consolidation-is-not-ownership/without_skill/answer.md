### How to read this

- **No percentages, by design.** The result describes itself as *"GLEIF Level 2 accounting consolidation: entities that hold an LEI and report this entity as their direct or ultimate consolidating parent."* Consolidation is not equity ownership.
- **The list is not complete.** Subsidiaries without an LEI, and those that report no parent to GLEIF, are not included. Shell has far more Dutch and Norwegian group companies than these 32.
- **Lapsed LEIs (9 in the Netherlands)** mean the entity hasn't renewed its LEI. The parent link may be out of date. It does not mean the company has been dissolved, since all 32 are still shown with entity status ACTIVE.
- **The "Ultimate only" rows** are consolidated into Shell further down the chain. NOORDZEEWIND C.V. is a limited partnership. Partnerships like this are often joint ventures, so I wouldn't assume Shell holds 100% of it.

### Getting actual percentages

The shareholdings have to come from an ownership source rather than GLEIF Level 2:
- **Netherlands:** the KvK Handelsregister and the Dutch UBO register. UBO access has been restricted since the 2022 CJEU ruling, so expect limited public detail. Shell's annual report also lists significant subsidiaries with the group's interest.
- **Norway:** Brønnøysundregistrene, and the shareholder register (*aksjonærregisteret*) via Skatteetaten.
- In OpenCheck, `opencheck_owners` or `opencheck_lookup` on an individual subsidiary's LEI shows any per-source ownership figures that the connected registers publish. Run `opencheck_knowability` for NL and NO first to see what those registers disclose.
