The MTender Black List ticket (13 Nov 2026) asked for Moldova's list of economic operators barred from public procurement as a `DEBARMENT` signal, joined on the IDNO. Measured that day: the current list is the Public Procurement Agency's table at tender.gov.md (23 bans, decisions 2023 to 2 Oct 2026, three-year terms), which answers 403 to every datacentre address and publishes no IDNO, only name, address, administrator and founders, applicant, decision, reason, entry date and end date. MTender's own `api/banned-operators` is a copy loaded on 27 May 2021 (41 rows) whose every ban had ended by April 2024. OpenSanctions crawls the tender.gov.md list daily as `md_interdictie`, in its `default` and `debarment` collections, with the `debarment` topic and one `Sanction` per ban carrying authority, decision number, reason and start and end dates, so OpenCheck already raised `DEBARMENT` / `RELATED_DEBARMENT` for these companies by name. What it did not say was any of the terms. New `opencheck/debarment.py` reads the `Sanction` entities nested in the record OpenSanctions serves at `/entities/{id}`: the subject-level signal from the deepen pass gains one sentence ("Barred by Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Moldova (decision 1/26) from 2 October 2026 until 2 October 2029; reason given: \"…\"") and `evidence.debarment_terms`; the related-party and register-anchored name-screen matches, which carry no nested sanctions, have their record read once through the adapter's cached `fetch` (at most 25 per lookup, after the PEP consolidation in all three pipelines). A ban whose every term has ended reads "Was barred … the ban has ended" with `debarment_ended: true`; code and confidence never change, so `SIGNAL_RULES` is not bumped. On a record also on a sanctions list only `Sanction`s from OpenSanctions' 61 debarment datasets count. Reasons are quoted in the publisher's language, never translated. No new source: building our own reader would have needed an off-datacentre index, name-only matching and an unconfirmed licence, to duplicate OpenSanctions. Check after deploy: a register lookup for a company on the list (when `asp_moldova` answers) shows the ban's terms; the curated cards that carry `DEBARMENT` / `RELATED_DEBARMENT` keep their codes and confidence, but the narratives quote signal summaries, so regenerate the curated narratives once.
