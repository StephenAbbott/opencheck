---
title: The ownership graph's zoom, Fit and Add next layer controls move under the canvas.
readme: The ownership graph's zoom, Fit and Add next layer controls now sit in a tinted bar directly under the diagram, so they stay next to the network on a phone.
---
On NVIDIA's FullCheck the controls sat in the toolbar above the canvas, with the signal legend (open by default since Phase 243) between them and the network; with many signals that put them a screen away from the graph on a phone. They now sit in a bar under the canvas, on the `oo.soft` tint the FullCheck run box wears, so they read as part of the diagram. Zoom out, Zoom in and Fit are one bordered white segmented group (`role="group"`, "Zoom") with 36px targets and stroke icons (`minus` joins `ui/Icon`) instead of bare grey glyphs that did not look pressable. Expand all became a `ui/Button` ghost (raw `<button>` count in `BODSGraph.tsx` 8 → 7). The single-layer control keeps its Phase 245 rule (secondary, never primary) and is pushed right; its visible label is "Add next layer" with a plus icon and the frontier count as a pill, where it read "+1 layer". Search and the legend stay above the canvas; "Skip to text version" still skips every canvas control. Checked against production data at 1280px and 390px.
