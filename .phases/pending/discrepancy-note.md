The "Discrepancy report export for registers" ticket (opportunity 10 of the Open Ownership usable-data check). `opencheck/discrepancy_note.py` builds, from a run's `RECORD_CONSISTENCY` evidence, one note per register that holds a disputed value — every `officialRegister` source and GLEIF, whose public data challenge facility is the route — each naming the subject (name, LEI, that register's identifier and record link), every disagreement (the field, the register's own value, the conflicting value with its source and licence, the dates each side was stated and retrieved, all read off the run's frozen BODS), where to send it, blank reporter lines and a footer. Stephen's decisions (3 Nov 2026): only the disagreements the Phase 337 allowlist already shows qualify — nothing is compared at export time, the counted-but-unshown fields stay out, and name/address comparisons are their own consistency ticket with the two-week gate first; a lapsed LEI (GLEIF's record of the LEI, not the company) and a GLEIF parent (consolidation, not a shareholder) are not register discrepancies; the note describes and never alleges, its fixed sentences pinned against the knowability banned vocabulary, and it says in its own words that it is a register-record note and not an AMLR / PSC beneficial-ownership discrepancy report; the footer carries the saved-report link and SHA-256 only when the run is saved (saving is a deliberate, quota-bound act), otherwise one line says how to get one; the reporter is blank lines and nothing about the reader is stored. The reporting routes live on the Notion table *Beneficial ownership access status* — three new properties, Discrepancy reporting route, Discrepancy reporting note and Company register discrepancy route, synced into `jurisdictions.json` by `sync_jurisdictions.py` as every other legal fact there — drafted from the registers' own pages for GB (the Companies House PSC discrepancy report, MLR 2017 reg. 30A), EE (the e-Business Register's lahknevusteade), LV (kludas@ur.gov.lv), DK (the virk.dk self-service) and NO (Altinn RRH-0120), left unverified for Stephen to check. Surfaces: `POST /export/discrepancy-note` (`lei`, or `scheme` + `id` for a company with no LEI; Markdown or PDF through the report toolchain; `saved_report_id` renders a saved report's own run and never runs the check; 404 when nothing qualifies), the "Discrepancy note" item in the Export menu's Report group (aria-disabled with its reason when the run found no disagreement — the three Report items are now one `ReportItem`, which also brought the design-system raw-button ratchet down by one), a button under the "Records compared" table, and the MCP tool `opencheck_discrepancy_note` (heavy tier, Markdown plus a routing summary). `docs/record-consistency.md` gains "The discrepancy note"; `docs/mcp-server.md` the tool. Check in production: a lookup whose "Records compared" chip names a disagreement (a register status the register records as dissolved beside a GLEIF ACTIVE is the shape) offers the item, and its Markdown opens with "**To:**" the register and that register's recorded route; a lookup with no disagreement shows the item greyed with its reason. No file in the findings golden set asserts a `RECORD_CONSISTENCY` disagreement, so the test pins the shape on a fixture (Companies House dissolved since 3 Apr 2019, GLEIF ACTIVE) rather than a named LEI.
