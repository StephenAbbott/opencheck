`oece_rnp` reads one SQLite index built from OECE's "Personas declaradas en la conformación jurídica de proveedores" file (ODC-By, ~227 MB, ~1.5M rows, 368,732 suppliers on the 2 Nov 2026 cut), downloaded from OECE's Confluence page by `refresh-oece-rnp-index.yml` early each month (skipped when the attachment is unchanged) and shipped as the `oece-rnp-index` release asset (~30 MB gzipped), downloaded at boot. Stephen's decisions on the Notion ticket: accept ODC-By from the publisher's metadata document; SOCIO_ACCIONISTA_TITULAR → `shareholding` with no share value (`otherInfluenceOrControl` for forms with no shares), REPRESENTANTE_LEGAL → `seniorManagingOfficial`, ORGANO_ADMINISTRACION → `boardMember`; never `beneficialOwnershipOrControl`; every statement annotated as a supplier's self-declaration to a procurement regulator. No person's document number is stored (Ley 29733), nor a natural person's `10…` RUC; a company member keeps its RUC as `PE-RUC`; person-or-company is decided from the document's shape and legal-form words, estates and marital partnerships are `arrangement`s and "OTROS ACCIONISTAS" an unspecified party. The file names no supplier and gives no status: the company's name is GLEIF's. GLEIF files the RUC bare under SUNAT (RA000689), SUNARP (RA000482), the SMV (RA000979) and the SBS (RA000980); `PE-RUC` is value-schemed on the check digit and is a FullCheck register hop. 84 of 296 ISSUED RUC-filing LEIs are RNP suppliers. OECE's OCDS procurement API still answers 403 to datacentres; procurement from the Open Contracting Partnership's registry mirror is the ticket's next step. After merge, run `refresh-oece-rnp-index` once by hand: until the asset exists the source is never announced.
