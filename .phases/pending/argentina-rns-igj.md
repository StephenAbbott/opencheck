New source `registro_sociedades_argentina` (Notion: Argentina IGJ entities and authorities; Stephen's decisions A to D, Nov 2026). `scripts/build_ar_sociedades_index.py` reads the two ZIPs datos.jus.gob.ar publishes under CC BY 4.0, the RNS (one row per CUIT and activity: 1,270,806 CUITs on the 18 Sept 2026 cut) and the IGJ's newest complete month (1,021,998 entities once natural persons go), and writes one SQLite index (417 MB, 129 MB gzipped). `refresh-ar-sociedades-index.yml` checks CKAN weekly and rebuilds only when either file changed (exit 3 otherwise), shipping the `ar-sociedades-index` asset that `warm_index` downloads at boot. Run it once by hand after merge: until the asset exists the source is never announced.

Decision A: one source. A CUIT reads the RNS row and the IGJ entry carrying it. Decision D: an IGJ foreign entry or a contract on the same CUIT (art. 123) is set aside, and a removed or duplicate entry ranks last. Decision B: the IGJ's autoridades file is read only to count rows per entity; no name, document number or gender reaches the index, the bundle (the schema refuses the keys) or BODS, and the count is worded as everyone named since registration, not who serves today. Decision C: natural-person forms are never indexed. Nothing is called active, because the RNS publishes no status and the IGJ marks only removed entries. A removal is terminal only on a cancellation, dissolution without liquidation or extinction, and a move of jurisdiction is said not to be an ending. No date is invented.

GLEIF files the CUIT under RA000009 (ARCA), RA000010 (RNS) and RA000800 (CNV), bare or hyphenated. `AR-CUIT` (hyphenated) is written only when the check digit agrees, the Chile and Colombia rule, and is a FullCheck register hop. About 30 RA000010 records hold an IGJ entry number instead. That number is read as `IGJ:<n>` and name-gated, and the GLEIF hit never asserts it. The resolver and the picker search all three authorities in both spellings. Measured against the GLEIF AR records on 11 Nov 2026: 313 of 319 ISSUED CUIT filings resolve. The six misses are two public banks, the central bank, the deposit-guarantee fund, a trust and a personal CUIT, each a note card. Six of eight IGJ-number filings resolve, and two name another entity (Bayer's `001672`).
