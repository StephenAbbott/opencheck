readme: Israeli companies' filings with the Corporations Authority now appear on the History tab and re-run a watched Israeli company, saying what was filed and never more than the register publishes.

The `ica-changes` file on data.gov.il (CC BY, 564,796 rows, 97 request codes on 11 Nov 2026) lists every request the Authority registered for a company or partnership in the past year — the request type and the day, never who or what it changed to. `sources/ica_israel_changes.py` is the one reading of a request code (English label beside the register's Hebrew, thirteen groups, the material set); the index builder gains a `change` table keyed (number, code, day, charge) and, with `--previous`, carries the previous build's rows older than the window forward, so OpenCheck's copy grows past the register's year (Stephen). The eighth History emitter, `timeline/ica_israel.py`: winding-up, liquidation, revival and strike-off requests are Tier-2 `STATUS_CHANGED`, mergers `SUCCESSION`, a change of name `LEGAL_NAME_CHANGE`, each with the filing as `value_new` and never a status reached; share transfers, allotments and shareholder updates are the new Tier-1 `SHARES_FILED`, worded "filed; parties not published" (Stephen: the ownership tier, History and the watchlist only, never QuickCheck); directors added or removed Tier 4 with no person; everything else Tier 3. `/history` gains `registers_kept_since`, and the tab's register note names the day OpenCheck's copy starts. The watchlist's register tier gets a second feed: `scripts/diff_ica_israel_index.py` lists each corporation's new filings in the material groups (shares, capital, directors, name, status, merger, attachment, classification — charges are not material, Stephen) and its name, status, sub-status and violating-mark changes into `ica-israel-delta.json`; `ica_israel_tick` is the Estonian tick generalised (`_register_delta_tick`, `_RegisterFeed`), with its own watermark, gaps, `il_*` counters and an hourly index refresh; the trigger is graded by filing group and field, and worded on the feed and the page. After merge, run `refresh-ica-israel-index` once by hand: the first run builds the change log and publishes an empty delta day.
