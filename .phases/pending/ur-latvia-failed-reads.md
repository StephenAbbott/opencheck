---
title: The Latvian register's failed reads are said, never cached as "no record".
readme: When data.gov.lv does not answer, the Latvian register now reads as not answering instead of caching an empty answer that showed owners as "none filed".
tests: backend +10, vitest +1
---
On 19 Nov 2026 data.gov.lv answered 502/503 and AS "Remars-Rīga" (LEI 984500ADQ50FFDAB6260) read "none filed" for its owners: `UrLatviaAdapter.fetch` read every failed CKAN table as empty and cached the bundle under `ur_latvia/entity/<regcode>`, breaking the Phase 262 rule. Now `_fetch_entity` answers `None` (not `{}`) and `_fetch_rows` — which replaces `_fetch_resource` on the lookup path too — answers `None` (not `[]`) for a refusal or an exception. A company record that did not answer is a stub with a `source_fetch` degradation, so the card reads "Did not answer" with Try again; a beneficial-owner, officer, member or former-name table that did not answer is left empty, named in the bundle's `unread`, and recorded as a `source_read` degradation, so the disclosure status reads "Not read" rather than "none filed". Neither bundle is cached; a full answer is cached as before. On the History tab `fetch_timeline_data` raises `UrLatviaUnavailable` when the company's record did not answer, and `timeline/service.py` puts `ur_latvia` in `registers_unavailable` (the reason, `upstream_error` for any other failure, or the new `partial` when some tables answered and others did not), which `unavailableRegisterLines` words; until now the register was dropped from the tab silently. The daily watch of the register's 9 Nov 2026 shareholder ruling is a separate task. After deploy: an LV lookup during a data.gov.lv outage should show the register as not answering, and a later lookup should read it fresh.
