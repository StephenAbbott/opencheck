Part D of the "Watchlist as pKYC: materiality, context and review record" ticket (Notion) — Moody's defensibility argument that a monitoring programme must be able to say why A was reviewed and B was not. Stages A–C made the log say what changed, how much it mattered, what the watcher expected and when OpenCheck last looked; this stage records what someone decided, and what they decided on. New module `opencheck/watchlist_decisions.py` and migration 6: an `entry_decisions` table (append-only — a change of mind is a later row, the entry's current decision is the latest and the history is kept) and `entries.run_completed_at` / `deepen_top`, the replay-cache key of the run that produced the entry, written by `_write_rerun` (every watchlist re-run now runs at `RERUN_DEEPEN_TOP` = 5, the stream's depth). Stephen's decisions (18 Oct 2026): three closed statuses — "Reviewed, no action" (Looked at; nothing to do), "Re-checked" (Looked at and the company was re-run), "Escalated" (Routed to whoever decides; what they decided is not recorded here) — the reader's statement, never set automatically; a *decision*, not a *disposition* (`dispositions.py` signs off narrative claims, a different object); the optional note reuses stage B's rationale rule as it stands; evidence is opt-in and never automatic, as saved reports always were — `evidence: "entry_run"` saves the entry's own re-run through `save_from_replay` while the server still holds it (15 minutes), a run no longer held is a 409 `run_not_held` ("That re-run is no longer held …; re-check and keep instead.") that writes no decision, and `evidence: "recheck"` runs a fresh re-run and saves that, worded as evidence of now; every save sits behind the per-client saved-report quota and a refused save records nothing; no reviewer identity (no accounts, so none). Routes: `POST /watch/{token}/entries/{id}/decisions` (default tier; a plain decision or the entry's held run) and `…/decisions/recheck` (heavy tier, like `/recheck` — it runs a pipeline), `GET /watch/{token}/record.md` — the list's review record as Markdown, one line per decision with its entry's headline and materiality, the note, and the pinned report's id and SHA-256, reading no clock and running no pipeline so it renders byte-identical on any day. The payload carries `decision` / `decision_count` / `decision_history` / `run_held` per entry, `review_history` per watch and a list-level `decisions` block (`undecided_material`, `evidence_available`); the Atom feed adds the decision as the entry's last line and a `category` in the `…/watchlist/decision` scheme; `/watchstats` adds `decisions_by_status`, `decisions_with_evidence` and `material_undecided`, counts only. On `/watchlist`: a chip (ok / context / warn — never risk; a decision is the reader's act, not a finding) and the decision line on a decided entry with earlier decisions behind a disclosure and "Change decision"; the three buttons on an undecided one, opening a form with the status, its meaning, a note field and a "… and keep the evidence (a saved report)" checkbox worded for this re-run when the server still holds it or a fresh re-check otherwise — switching to the re-check wording when a save answers `run_not_held` — hidden when the instance has no saved-reports store; a "Needs review" filter (`?show=needs_review`, material and undecided) beside All / Material only; a line over the log counting material entries with no decision; and a "Download the review record (Markdown)" link. `docs/watchlist.md` gains "The review record: change → review → decision" and the three routes; `docs/saved-reports.md` closes its deferred "save what this re-run found" item; the /features clause says so. `STATUS_LABELS` / `STATUS_MEANINGS` / `EVIDENCE_WORDS` are mirrored in `lib/watchlist.ts` and pinned by `test_watchlist_decisions.py`, which also runs both evidence paths against a stubbed `save_from_replay`, the 409 against the real one, the quota refusal, the list-notice entry (a decision, no evidence) and the record's second-read identity. Not done, on purpose: no automatic status, no reviewer field, no deletion of a decision, no e-mail (stage E is a scoping note).
