---
title: FollowTheMoney citizenship reaches BODS nationalities on OpenSanctions, OpenAleph and EveryPolitician person statements.
readme: OpenSanctions, OpenAleph and EveryPolitician person statements now list a person's FollowTheMoney citizenship among their nationalities, saying which entries came from it.
tests: backend +8
---
`bods/mappers/ftm.py::_ftm_person_statement` read only the FtM `nationality` property; OpenSanctions fills `citizenship` for many sanctioned people and PEPs, often instead of nationality (Arkadij Abramovič, Q4791679: citizenship `ru`, no nationality). `ftm_person_nationalities` now reads both, nationality first, deduplicated on the ISO code and, for a name-only entry, on the name. FtM `country` on a Person stays out: it is unscoped. Stephen's decisions (4 Nov 2026): an entry that came from citizenship alone is said in one `commenting` annotation on `/recordDetails/nationalities` ("Uzbekistan (UZ), … are read from the record's FollowTheMoney citizenship property, not its nationality property"); a value in both lists is not annotated. A former state is kept by name with no code — FtM writes the Soviet Union as `suhh`, its ISO 3166-3 code, on Prigozhin, Usmanov, Abramovich and Sechin; `_ftm_resolve_nationality` takes the name from pycountry's historic list ("USSR, Union of Soviet Socialist Republics") instead of the literal `suhh` it wrote before, and Kosovo (`xk`) by name. Matching: `cross_check` compares codes on OpenCheck's side against the hit's nationality, citizenship and country, so the hit side is unchanged and a name-only entry is never compared; the only effect is that an FtM person who is a related-party target can now corroborate on nationality where its record had only citizenship. Golden set measured on production lookups of all fifteen subjects (4 Nov 2026): two FtM-mapped people in total — Igor Sechin (Rosneft; gains only the name-only Soviet Union) and Mohsen Seifi Kafshgari (Bank Saderat; no citizenship) — so no RELATED_* confidence moves; Sechin's BackgroundCheck card lists the Soviet Union after Russia, and BackgroundCheck's "possibly same" scoring gives a shared nationality its usual +0.05. Tests on the two real OpenSanctions records (`tests/fixtures/opensanctions/`). No `SIGNAL_RULES` bump: no rule changed.
