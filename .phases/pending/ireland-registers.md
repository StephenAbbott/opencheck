The Ireland row of the jurisdictions table was re-synced from the Notion "Beneficial ownership access status" database after Stephen accepted five proposed updates (15 Nov 2026): the RBO's full name (Central Register of Beneficial Ownership of Companies and Industrial and Provident Societies), its coverage (companies and IPSs), the legitimate-interest procedure under S.I. 335/2026 (a decision in 12 working days, extendable to 43, appeal to the District Court), notes on Ireland's three registers (RBO, Revenue's CRBOT for trusts, the Central Bank's CFV for funds) and official sources only. Only the IE row was taken from the Notion query; the query's text output drops page and date mentions, so the other rows were left as generated. The "not public" note on a source card (`bo_access.notice_for`) now carries the register's name and home page from the table and is set apart under a rule with a "Beneficial ownership register" heading, so the CRO card no longer reads as if the CRO restricts its own data (`lib/boAccess.ts`). The CRO record link `core.cro.ie/company/<number>` answered 404: CORE addresses a company by an internal id that neither the open data nor Open Services publishes, and company numbers repeat across companies and business names, so the `cro` register-link entry is removed (no link rather than a wrong one) and the statements' `source.url` is the CRO open-data record for the company number.
