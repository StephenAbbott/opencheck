title: FullCheck no longer pushes the page sideways on a phone — the "Read as text" tree is the containing block of its own screen-reader text.
readme: The FullCheck "Read as text" tree no longer makes the report scroll sideways on a phone.
tests: vitest +1
---
Found by the post-deploy tester on 12 Nov 2026: Rosneft's FullCheck at 390×844 scrolled the page to 1,050px. The ticket blamed the graph's overlay marks, but the overlay already clips them (`overflow: hidden`); hiding elements one by one put the overflow in `BodsTree`. On a phone the tree is open first, and Rosneft's rows (signal chips, integrated-ownership cell, kind column) are up to 1,209px wide, so the tree scrolls sideways inside itself. Its `sr-only` spans are `position: absolute`, and with no positioned ancestor inside the tree their containing block lay outside its scroll box: the "Integrated ownership:" label at a row's far end was laid out there and escaped the clip. The tree is now `relative`, which makes it their containing block. The same one-class fix went on the other sideways-scrolling tables that hold `sr-only` text (the integrated-ownership list, EITI Zambia's mining rights, the batch results table). A jsdom test pins the class; a new 390px e2e case opens Rosneft's FullCheck, widens the rows and ends one with a screen-reader label as production does (offline, no screen or integrated figure makes them that wide), and asserts no sideways scroll; it fails without the fix. After deploy, re-measure `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` on https://opencheck.world/?lei=253400JT3MQWNDKMJE44&mode=full at 390px: it should be 390.
