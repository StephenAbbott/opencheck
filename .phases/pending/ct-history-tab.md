title: Connecticut joins the History tab, from the Secretary of the State's Filing History and Name Change History.
readme: Connecticut businesses now have a History tab built from the Secretary of the State's filings and recorded name changes, with annual reports counted on one row.
tests: backend +30, vitest +1
---
The Connecticut History ticket. `CtSotsAdapter.fetch_timeline_data` returns the lookup's cached bundle (Business Master, Principals, Name Change History) plus the **Filing History** (`ah3s-bes7`, 10.1M rows, replaced on the first of each month), read for the History tab only and never by the lookup, on `account` = the Master row's 18-character id. The `$select` is an allowlist: the organizer's and incorporator's names, titles and types (97,015 rows name an organizer, 82,125 of them a person) are never asked for, and `CtSotsTimelineBundle` refuses them. Filing History carries no licence field on data.ct.gov; Stephen decided on 20 Nov 2026 to treat it as Public Domain like its three siblings without asking the Secretary of the State again, and the `CT-Open-Data` row, attribution and ATTRIBUTIONS.md say so. The filings are cached a day under `ct_sots/filings-v1/<id>` only when they answered; a failure is `filings_unavailable` and reaches `/history` as `registers_unavailable["ct_sots"]`, while the recorded renames still show.

`timeline/ct_sots.py` maps the filings, measured over the 686 ISSUED `RA000600` LEIs (median 19 filings a business, 3 without annual reports, at most 195; 73% are annual reports). Renames come from Name Change History as `LEGAL_NAME_CHANGE`; an amendment filed or effective on a rename day is the rename and is folded in (150 of 172 rename days carry one; Eder-Goodman's was a plain Amendment / Certificate of Amendment). Dissolution, administrative dissolution or revocation, withdrawal, cancellation, reinstatement, revocation of dissolution and forfeiture are Tier 2 status changes, worded for a foreign business as ending only its Connecticut registration; a Notice of Intent to Dissolve/Revoke is a warning and stays Tier 3. A merger or consolidation certificate never names the other business: it is a `SUCCESSION` only on a domestic business the Master records as Merged or Consolidation (the latest such certificate), and an administrative row on a survivor. Conversions are `LEGAL_FORM_CHANGE`, domestications `JURISDICTION_CHANGE`. **Annual reports are one grouped row per business** (Stephen's choice), "N annual reports filed, for 2003 to 2026", dated by the latest. Everything else is an administrative row keyed by its filing number. Filing types are mapped on the (category, filing type) pair with the category as fallback; the two categories published with a broken dash and the literal "None" are cleaned. Dates are the effective date where given, else the filing date.

Wiring: `_registration_numbers` carries the zero-padded business ID tenth for `RA000600`, whatever the jurisdiction (UBS AG is Swiss), and a new `_unpack` pads the shorter tuples older test doubles return. `registry_numbers["ct_sots"]` links the register chip to the Business Master query for the business ID (`recordUrl` case in `historyMode.ts`; not a register-link entry, so no "Register record" appears on lookups). `HISTORY_SOURCES`, the label "Secretary of the State (CT)" and a register note were added, and the change-log register lists on the API page, /features and how-it-works name Connecticut. Tests are on real Filing History answers captured 20 Nov 2026 (`tests/data/ct_sots_filings_live.json`), including WEB ACQUISITION CO., a domestic corporation recorded as Merged. After deploy, open the History tab for a Connecticut LEI with a recorded rename and one with a dissolution, and check the grouped annual-report row.
