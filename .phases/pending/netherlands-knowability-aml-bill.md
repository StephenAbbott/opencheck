The Ministry of Finance published the Implementatiewet ter voorkoming van witwassen en terrorismefinanciering (KetenID WGK027204) on 20 Nov 2026 and sent it to the Tweede Kamer. It implements Directive 2024/1640 (AMLD6), gives effect to Regulation 2024/1624 (AMLR) and repeals the Wwft. For the UBO register it amends the Handelsregisterwet 2007 and the trusts Implementatiewet: AMLR beneficial-owner data, KvK verification and discrepancy duties, historical data for AMLD6 Art. 12(2)(a), (b) and (e), and notification of the UBO only on request, which answers the Raad van State's objection to the legitimate-interest decree WGK015141. Five proposals in "EU legitimate interest — proposed updates" were accepted by Stephen and applied to the Netherlands row of the Notion access-status table: AMLR alignment watch, 6AMLD details, Sources, Pending changes and Last verified (20 Nov 2026). jurisdictions.json was re-synced for the Netherlands with sync_jurisdictions.py --rows-json and docs/knowability.md regenerated. The beneficial-ownership access status stays restricted with no legitimate-interest route yet: the bill does not open access, which still waits for decree WGK015141.
