From the "Companies House officer-by-id lookups never carry a nationality" ticket (found on the PSC country-of-residence ticket, 3 Nov 2026). Two defects. `_map_companies_house_officer` read `nationality` from the `/officers/{id}/appointments` envelope, which never carries one (the live keys are active_count, date_of_birth, etag, inactive_count, is_corporate_officer, items_per_page, kind, links, name, resigned_count, start_index, total_results); it now reads every appointment (`_ch_appointments_nationalities`: an envelope value first should the register add one, then current appointments latest first, then resigned latest first, the residence order) and keeps every distinct value (Stephen, 4 Nov 2026). And no Companies House path wrote a `code`, while `cross_check` compares `nationalities[].code` only, so nationality never corroborated a match for anyone Companies House names. New `bods/nationality.py` (`ch_nationalities`, `DEMONYMS`) splits a filed value on commas, "and", `/`, `&` (and on spaces only when every word is a demonym: "Irish Canadian"), resolves each part through a 268-entry demonym table (from the `countryinfo` package's pairs, cut to unambiguous ones, sovereign country for French / American / Chinese / Indian / Norwegian, plus CH spellings English, Scottish, Hong Konger, Hksar, Kazakh) and then `statements._country_code`, after removing "Citizen of" / "… citizen". A part it cannot resolve ("Other", "Kosovan", "Congolese") keeps its name with no code. Each part keeps the register's spelling as `name`; entries dedupe on the code. Applied to the officers list, PSCs (and so the PSC graph) and officer-by-id. Fixtures in `test_bods_ch_officer.py` and `test_bods_libcovebods.py` moved nationality off the envelope. Golden-set effect, measured by running the real related-party screen before and after on production's exported BODS for all fifteen subjects (4 Nov 2026): one change — Shell's director Jane Holl Lute, American, now corroborates on nationality, so her Russian MFA counter-sanction listing (`RELATED_COUNTER_SANCTIONED`) moves medium → high and her two PEP matches add nationality beside birth year; Birtley, Persia International Bank, Regulatory DataCorp and ASDA have Companies House people and no change; the other nine have none. Bank Saderat's Companies House people come from Open Ownership's stored bundle, which already carries codes. Content-addressed `statementId`s of Companies House person statements change (the code is new content); `recordId`s do not. OpenCorporates' officer nationalities are still name-only (a separate decision). After deploy: Shell's QuickCheck shows the Lute counter-sanction chip at high.
