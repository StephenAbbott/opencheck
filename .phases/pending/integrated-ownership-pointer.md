---
title: The integrated-ownership annotation targets /recordDetails, not the statement root.
readme: The integrated-ownership note on a subject's BODS statement now points at its record details, so libcove no longer rejects it.
tests: backend +1
---
Found by the BODS quality sweep of 19 Oct 2026, the first since the integrated-ownership band shipped: eleven `bods_additional` warnings (`gleif` ×9, `GLEIF` ×2), all libcove's `annotation_statement_pointer_target_invalid` with pointer `/` — on ASDA Stores, BP, Rosneft, Birtley Investment, Eesti Energia and the other lookup subjects with an integrated figure. `integrated_ownership.apply_to_bods` wrote `statementPointerTarget: "/"`, and the Phase 339 test pinned it. A statement-wide annotation targets `/recordDetails` (the Phase 255 rule); the test now asserts that, and a new test fails on `/` and on any pointer that does not resolve inside the statement. No other annotation in the backend targets `/`. Saved reports keep the annotation they were folded with; a new run, or a replay after the cache window, carries the fixed pointer. Left open on the ticket, not part of this fix: on BP and Rosneft the annotation is written onto a copy of Open Ownership's verbatim GLEIF statement (the PermID `publicListing` precedent), which sits awkwardly with Phase 317's rule against patching another publisher's statements. Check the next weekly BODS quality sweep reports no `annotation_statement_pointer_target_invalid`.
