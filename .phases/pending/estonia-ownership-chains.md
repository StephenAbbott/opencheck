Phase 330's index answered one company at a time: an Estonian OÜ's corporate shareholder was a name and a registry code, and its owners — one more read of the same index — arrived only when FullCheck hopped. This phase makes the index tier walk the chain itself, the Companies House shape: `walk_chain` follows every corporate holder (OÜ shareholder rows, the AS shareholder rows of the off-card phase, partner officer rows) with an Estonian registry code, breadth-first, cycle-safe, to six hops and twenty-five companies, and `bundle["chain"]` reports what it did not follow and why — a foreign code (the index is Estonia only), a code the index lacks, the depth, the cap. `map_ariregister` emits each related company's full entity statement and its parties on one `isikukood_hash` node where a person recurs (a related company's founders are not the subject's history), so integrated ownership computes the chain figure in the first lookup; the finding says "N more companies followed up the chain; M owners not followed". The Subsidiaries tab gains a fifth list, what an Estonian company holds, from the index's reverse side (`holdings_of`, over the builder's new partial indexes; keyed from the LEI through the GLEIF mirror's `registeredAt`, mirror first so a non-Estonian subject is "not covered" with or without the index); rows carry the held company's registry code as a register-lookup key, never an LEI. And a foreign owner's `valis_kood` — stated with no scheme — becomes an identifier on its country's register only in shape: one register hop for the country, the scheme in `_EE_FOREIGN_CODE_SHAPES`, the hop-normalised code matching that register's number (the hops' own normalisers mostly tidy), a leading country prefix removed, the annotation saying it is derived — which is what lets a FullCheck register hop follow the owner across the border. `opencheck_owners`' grow still follows LEIs only; a downward register hop in FullCheck is a separate ticket. Stacked on the off-card phase; the index needs the daily rebuild for the reverse indexes, and answers by scan until then. Notion: Estonia: follow ownership chains inside the index.
