pKYC stage E, Route 2 (Notion "pKYC stage E, Route 2: `ownership_changed` — a statement-level ownership diff on every watchlist re-run"; the scoping page's Q3 and Q4, the latter confirmed 19 Oct 2026). `CHANGE_KINDS` had no ownership kind: an owner replaced by another owner with the same risk profile moved no signal, status or coverage, so the re-run wrote nothing — in any register. New module `opencheck/watchlist_ownership.py`. `snapshot_from_response` now keeps `ownership_digest` on the baseline, frozen with the run's day: one row per ownership-or-control relationship with the subject, per source, keyed on the party's record — an entity's `recordId`; a person's **register key only**, read back from the Phase 255 `identifying` annotation (a Companies House officer id or PSC item id — the key the phase before this gave it, so an edited PSC filing is one party whose band moved, not one ended and one added — an Estonian `isikukood_hash`, a Wikidata Q-id, a Slovak `kuv_id`); a person with none (OpenCorporates officers, Norway's name-derived hash) is not a row, is counted per source, and the entry says "N parties could not be compared (source publishes no person key)". Never a name, a date of birth, or a hash of either (Stephen, Q3). Each row carries the interest types, the bands of its shareholding and voting-rights interests (BODS v0.4's `exclusiveMinimum` is the bound itself, so Companies House's "25-to-50" reads "over 25% to 50%"), the beneficial-ownership flag, direct / indirect, and whether every relationship for the pair has ended (`bods.lifecycle`); a current relationship beside an ended one for the same pair makes the row current. Left out, as the Phase 339 lists do: GLEIF's reporting-exception bridges (a filing, not a party — `gleif_reporting_exception` and an unspecified party both read), MEIP (group membership) and officer-only roles (`seniorManagingOfficial` / `boardMember` / `boardChair`, or any interest carrying `opencheckOfficerPosition`), so the digest is the 2–10 rows the scoping page measured, not 6–60. `diff_snapshots` compares the two digests when the baseline carries one — a baseline from before this phase compares nothing and gains a digest on this update (the Phase 301 baseline-shape rule: widening the snapshot never writes a change for every watched LEI) — and names each row added, ended or re-sized as one `ownership_changed` change (`change`, `source_id`, `party`, the key, `interests`, `bands_before` / `bands_after`, `bo`, `crossed_25`); a row gone or ended while its source is degraded is `ownership_unchecked` (one per source, counted), never ended; a row that first appears already ended is history newly read, not news. Materiality (Q4): `ownership_changed` is **recheck**, and **escalate** when a shareholding or voting-rights band at or above 25 % was added or ended — a party becoming or ceasing to be a registrable controller (25 % is the PSC and AMLD threshold; registers publish bands, so crossing is a ≥25 % band appearing or disappearing, and an exclusive floor at 25 counts, as the PSC register's own band does) — while a move between bands above 25 % (50→75) stays recheck; the one place a kind's tier reads the change's contents, said in `docs/watchlist.md`. `ownership_unchecked` is note. No `MATERIALITY_RULES` bump: two new rows, nothing moved. Words on both sides (`INTEREST_WORDS` / `PARTY_WORDS` / `CHANGE_WORDS`, `change_sentence`, `band_words`; `ownershipChangeSentence`, `bandWords`, `uncomparedLines` in `lib/watchlist.ts`, parsed by the backend test with the `CHANGE_KINDS` mirror): "A registered beneficial owner's shareholding changed from 25–50% to 50–75% (e-Business Register)." / "A person's shareholding of over 25% to 50% added (UK Companies House)." / "2 parties' holdings could not be re-checked — OpenSanctions did not answer." The entry headlines "ownership changed" after a register-status move and a new signal; the page lists the uncompared parties under the changes. `/watchstats` adds `ownership_changes_by_change`, `ownership_changes_escalated`, `ownership_unchecked`, `ownership_uncompared_by_source` and `baselines_with_ownership_digest` — counts only. It catches nothing on its own: it runs inside a re-run some delta caused, and complements the PSC tier, which says that the register moved; this says what. Per source, never across sources. Tests pin the digest's rows and exclusions on a hand-built bundle and on the real Companies House mapper under two etags, the Q4 rule on every side of the line, the degraded and already-ended cases, the no-baseline case through `snapshot_from_response` / `diff_snapshots`, the words, the headline, the counters and the two docs tables.
