The second step of the Companies House country-of-residence ticket: its mapper sweep found four registers that publish a person's residence country and OpenCheck dropped it. All four go through the shared `bods/residence.py` — **a country only, never a street** — and a filed value that is itself the ISO code (Latvia "LV", New Zealand "NZ") no longer gets a "files the country as…" note. **Latvia:** the BO resource's `residence` column (ISO-2), on beneficial owners only; the officers and members resources carry none. **New Zealand:** `roleAddress[].countryCode` and the shareholder's address, from a current residential block only (`PHYSICAL`, or untyped — the pre-Act spelling); **never an `ALTERNATIVE` block**, which under the Address Act (in force 18 Nov 2026) is typically an agent's office, and never `roleAsicAddress`. The NZ bundle cache never expires, so its key moved to `company-v2`. **Czechia (ARES):** a natural person's address in the commercial register is their bydliště; `_extract_person` now keeps its `kodStatu` and both person paths (owners and directors) publish it. The ARES bundle cache moved to `bundle-v3`. **EITI Armenia:** the v0.2 person addresses the register files are residential; the street stays out as before (data-minimisation) and only the country is published. Same effect on signals as the Companies House change: the ICIJ jurisdiction gate now has a country for these persons. Person `statementId`s move (content-addressed); `recordId`s do not.
