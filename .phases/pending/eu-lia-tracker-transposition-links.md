Found while writing the two-month progress report on the 25 Sept 2026 6AMLD infringement letters (Notion "Write progress report two months after 6AMLD infringement notice"): the Notion table "Beneficial ownership access status" lacked the EUR-Lex national transposition records. Stephen approved five rows in "EU legitimate interest — proposed updates" on 24 Nov 2026, and they were applied. Greece gains the Hellenic Parliament text of Law 5354/2026, NIM:202608836 and its Greek PDF; the Greek journalism links stay. Spain gains Real Decreto 813/2026 (BOE-A-2026-20915, in force 28 Oct 2026), NIM:202608061 and its Spanish PDF, plus 6AMLD details: a partial transposition that leaves the legitimate-interest rules unregulated. Its access status stays `restricted_no_lia_route_yet`, by Stephen's decision: the law exists but the regulations that would open a route have not been published. Austria gains the RIS text of BGBl. I Nr. 94/2026 and NIM:202608400 / NIM:202608614. Italy gains NIM:202605266. Austria's and Italy's `access_url` follow the first source, as `sync_jurisdictions.py` derives it. The sync was made from the Notion SQL export, which drops date mentions inside text fields, so only these four rows were taken from it. Every other row is as it was, apart from the broken "Untitled" page mention dropped from two source notes.
