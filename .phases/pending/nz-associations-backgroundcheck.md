readme: A New Zealand director's or shareholder's other companies on the register now show on their BackgroundCheck card, beside the Estonian register's list, instead of in a company-wide panel on QuickCheck.

From the Notion ticket "Transfer NZ Director & shareholder associations functionality to BackgroundCheck". The Companies Entity Role Search enrichment was a company-wide "Director & shareholder associations" panel on QuickCheck's New Zealand source card; it is now a "Companies on the New Zealand register" control on each person's BackgroundCheck card, the shape of the Estonian register's "Companies on the Estonian register" (fetched when asked for, listed, never drawn, absent from a saved report). What stays different: Estonia keys a person on the register's own person key, while the NZ register searches by name, so every row keeps its address-graded match strength (MatchConfidenceChip), the name-only / address-matched split and the alternative-address wording. New `GET /nz-associations/person?company_number=&name=` (`nz_associations.person_associations`): one Role Search call for one role holder, who must be a director or shareholder of that company as filed (the filing grades the matches and keeps it from being a free name search); cached per company and name, and answered from a cached company-wide result with no call. `lib/backgroundCheck.ts` reads `nzRoleHolder` (the company's NZ-COH, else NZ-NZBN, and the name as the NZ person statement files it) off the nz_companies relationship statements, so a person merged from another source's spelling still asks the register about the filed name. QuickCheck's NZ card says where the list went; `NzAssociations.tsx` is deleted. Kept: `GET /nz-associations` (documented API); corporate shareholders' other companies are reachable only there now, since BackgroundCheck lists people. Check in production on Meridian Energy (254900IFKXIGNCY3A092): the BackgroundCheck cards of its directors carry the control, it lists companies with match chips, and the QuickCheck NZ card shows the pointer sentence instead of the panel.
