---
title: The MCP ownership map no longer draws GLEIF reporting exceptions or company secretaries as controllers.
readme: The MCP ownership map states GLEIF reporting exceptions in words and leaves company secretaries and other officers off the picture.
tests: backend +15, vitest +4
---
Road-testing `opencheck_ownership_map` on UNILEVER PLC (15 Oct 2026) drew three "Controls" edges into a widely held listed company and none was an owner: GLEIF's direct- and ultimate-parent reporting exceptions (NON_CONSOLIDATING), each as its own "Non-consolidating parent" node, and the company secretary from OpenCorporates (position "secretary", typed `otherInfluenceOrControl` since Phase 197). A GLEIF reporting exception is a filing that names no parent, so the map now lists it under a new `reporting_exceptions` field — direct and ultimate folded into one entry — and opens its JSON with a one-sentence `summary` ("No source names a current owner or controller of UNILEVER PLC. GLEIF records a reporting exception for the direct and ultimate parents …"); an undisclosed parent (NON_PUBLIC and its deprecated family) is still drawn, by Stephen's decision, and the BODS bridges themselves are unchanged. An officer appointment with no BODS role code now carries the extension field `opencheckOfficerPosition` on its interest (`bods/officer_roles.py`, written by the OpenCorporates officer path and the Companies House officer-appointments path); the PDF/MCP diagram and the FullCheck canvas read it as a role labelled by the position ("Secretary"), while a shareholding the same party holds still decides the edge — Stephen chose marking the BODS over a map-only filter so every reader agrees. The OpenCorporates position fallbacks now match the exact table (a director is `seniorManagingOfficial`, never `appointmentOfBoard`; a secretary `otherInfluenceOrControl`). Node names wrap onto two lines of 26 characters with the full name as `<title>`; one 52-character label had run off the left edge. On seven production bundles (Shell, BP, Maersk, ASDA, Equinor, Persia International Bank, Unilever) the only edges that left the map were the five exception bridges and the secretary; every owner stayed. Other registers that type officers `otherInfluenceOrControl` (Norway, Latvia, France, Czechia) are listed on the Notion ticket "OpenCheck MCP ownership map fixes" for a follow-up.
