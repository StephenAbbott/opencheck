Two sentences: the report from the Export menu as a tagged PDF or Markdown, with the saved report's link and SHA-256; and the discrepancy note per register where two registers' records disagree on status or jurisdiction, describing the difference without saying which is right and without claiming to be a beneficial ownership discrepancy report. Accent `oo.mark.checkBlue` (#2563eb), the logo's "Check" wordmark — a shipped token no other feature wears; glyph `oo.graph.ownershipTintBorder`. New icon `report` in `ui/Icon.tsx` (a page with a folded corner and a download arrow). It names no company and shows no value — on 4 Nov 2026 no production lookup produced a shown disagreement, so a real company on a note would be an invented finding.
