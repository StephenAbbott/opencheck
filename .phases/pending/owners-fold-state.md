Found by the MCP testing of 7 Nov 2026 (Notion "MCP testing ahead of Connected Data London 2026", issue 4): Equinor's `opencheck_owners` listed FINANSDEPARTEMENTET (OpenSanctions), Government of Norway (GEM) and the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Fisheries (Wikidata) as three 67% owners and the map drew three nodes; Ørsted, Eesti Energia and Rosneft did the same with their states. Stephen's decision: fold states only, by country — what `STATE_CONTROLLED` has done since Phase 240 — never companies by name. `owners.state_groups` maps every entity statement typed `state` / `stateBody` with a country code to `state:<ISO>` and "<Country> (state)" (a body with no country stays its own party). The fold is presentation only: integrated figures are still computed per body (folding before the arithmetic could add one stake a source files under two bodies — the Phase 338 double count), and `fold_states` then gives one `calculated_owners` row per state, `kind: "state"`, with `bodies` and every source's figure carrying `as` (the body it was filed under), never added; `grow`, `chain_stops` and `in_both` still read the bodies. The summary reads "Norway (state) (67% OpenSanctions as FINANSDEPARTEMENTET, 67% Global Energy Monitor / Climate TRACE as Government of Norway, 67% Wikidata as Ministry of Trade, Industry and Fisheries)". The map draws one node per state; its JSON rows keep the exact body as `owner_as`; a node's figures are gathered, not overwritten. Parallel edges between one owner and the company (a folded state, or a party two sources name) are drawn as one line labelled once per source (`_bundle_parallel`) — the style is ownership if any source says ownership — where they used to draw two or three lines with their labels stacked (Eesti Energia's map in production); the "as …" note is left off the drawing when the body is just "Government of <country>", which the node already says. After deploy: `opencheck_owners` for OW6OFBNCKXC4US5C7523 shows one "Norway (state)" row with three figures, and its map one Norway node.
