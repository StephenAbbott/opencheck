Found while investigating the BODS quality sweep of 26 Oct 2026 (`declaration_subject_not_subject|openaleph` on Regulatory DataCorp and A.P. Møller - Mærsk): some OpenAleph records of the subject carried their registration number under the bare scheme `REG`, so the 24 Oct `REG-<country>` → `JUR:<country>:` bridge could not join them to the subject's referent group and `subject_identity`, the profile and `network_subjects` treated them as other companies. Read from OpenAleph on 26 Oct 2026, neither record fills `jurisdiction` or `country` — the two properties the mapper already read — so qualifying from those alone would change nothing: Regulatory DataCorp Limited's UK Find a Tender party record (`ocds-party-gb-fts-88512…`, `REG 08739364`) gives its country only in its structured address (`addressEntity.country: gb`); Mærsk's overseas-entity PSC record (`gb-coh-psc-OE030334…`, `REG 22756214`) only in free text ("…1263 København K, Denmark") and in `legalForm`. Stephen's decisions (26 Oct 2026): the structured address may qualify the number, free text may not; the scheme only — the statement's `jurisdiction` stays empty, so an address country never reaches the FATF / EU high-risk / non-EU checks. `bods/mappers/ftm.py::ftm_registration_country` is the one rule: `jurisdiction`, then `country`, must name exactly one country (two different ones now leave the number bare rather than take the first); for an entity that fills neither, every expanded `addressEntity` must carry a country and all the same one; an unexpanded address reference says nothing; the collection (`gb_coh_psc`, `gb_ocds_find_a_tender_service` — the filing register, not the company's) and name matching are never read. A number qualified from the address carries a `transformation` annotation on its scheme quoting what the source published. The rule lives in the shared FtM mapper, so OpenSanctions records follow it too. Regulatory DataCorp's Find a Tender record now joins GLEIF's record of the subject through `JUR:GB:08739364`; Mærsk's OE030334 record stays bare (its 24 sibling PSC records carry `REG-DK` and already join). After deploy: look up Regulatory DataCorp (`894500LD30X1VN839203`) and check the Find a Tender record sits in the subject's group and names the subject's `declarationSubject`.
