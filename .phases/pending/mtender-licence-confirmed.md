The Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Moldova answered OpenCheck's licence request in letter No. 13-09/820 (13 Nov 2026, received 25 Nov): MTender's OCDS data are open data, re-usable by anyone including for commercial purposes, and "not subject to a specific licence" — re-use is governed by the national legal framework for open data and the re-use of public-sector information. It confirmed the attribution "Source: MTender, Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Moldova (public procurement data, in OCDS format)" as appropriate, which `mtender_moldova` now carries verbatim (`ATTRIBUTION`); the previous line was a longer wording of OpenCheck's own. `MTender-Open-Data` keeps its id and moves from amber to green (commercial use and redistribution yes, attribution required), its name and summary saying there is no specific licence. The letter also accepted the weekly API read at no more than four simultaneous requests, said MTender offers no bulk download, and that procedures older than a year are read by giving the period in the API's query parameters; the adapter docstring records all of it. ATTRIBUTIONS.md, docs/sources.md and the OKF bundle follow.
