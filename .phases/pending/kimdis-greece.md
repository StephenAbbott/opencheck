`kimdis_greece` reads the KIMDIS open-data API (`cerpp.eprocurement.gov.gr/khmdhs-opendata/contract`, keyless, CC BY 4.0) live, per Stephen's decisions on the Notion ticket: the API rather than the Diavgis file (A), contracts only, no payment orders (B), the ΑΦΜ from the `gemi_greece` record and no crosswalk (C), shown beside `ted_eu` and never added to it (D). A lookup whose GLEIF record files a twelve-digit ΓΕΜΗ number under RA000685 or RA000376 reads the company through the ΓΕΜΗ adapter (its own cache, now one request per number in flight, in its own provenance scope) and queries KIMDIS by the ΑΦΜ, whose check digit is verified first. The API answers at most 180 days a call, 50 records a page, and a 404 for a window with no contracts (an answer, not a failure); the adapter reads windows newest first back to 2017, three at a time under a 300-a-minute bucket, at most 60 calls a lookup (a large supplier such as OTE reads back to the day the cap was reached, and the card and finding say so). A window the server never answers (one of ΑΚΤΩΡ's 2018 windows hangs) is not retried: it becomes a stated gap and a `source_read` degradation, and the read carries on; a dropped connection or 5xx is retried once; nothing answering is `source_fetch`. A ΓΕΜΗ refusal is a degradation, never "no contracts". Amendments are folded through `prevReferenceNo`, so a contract counts once at its latest version. Activity, not ownership: one entity statement under `GR-AFM`, no relationship, no signal; the hit asserts no identifier; a company with no contracts gets no card. Personal data is never kept (`authorEmail`, signers, the units' operators and other contractors' names or numbers), and the bundle schema refuses those keys. Values are summed as the authorities published them, without VAT — one published record (ΑΔΑΜ 24SYMV014747658) reads €7.75bn, and the card says values are as posted. Measured live 12–13 Nov 2026: ΑΚΤΩΡ 127 contracts in 15 s with one gap; OTE 1,524 contracts in 25 s, truncated at 2021. Not measured: whether ΓΕΜΗ's name and KIMDIS's agree, because no ΓΕΜΗ key was available in the build environment — check one Greek LEI in production.
