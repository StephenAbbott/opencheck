Found by the MCP testing of 7 Nov 2026 (Notion "MCP testing ahead of Connected Data London 2026", issue 7): Rosneft's `opencheck_owners` answered `sanctions_ownership_test: null`. Traced on the production lookup: the test ran and was not met — the only designated party holding shares in any source is National Settlement Depository (10.7% in GEM's data), a name-only match and so not counted; the OpenSanctions record matched to Wikidata's "Rosneftegaz" is on no OFAC, EU or UK list; and Rosneft is itself designated under all three. A not-met result raises no signal, and the owners answer only echoed a signal, so none of this was said. `sanctions_ownership.outcome` reports the no-signal case: `status` `not_met` (with the per-regime sentence) or `no_designated_holder`, `not_counted` (name-only designated holders with their direct stake per source), `subject_designated` (the regimes from the company's own `SANCTIONED` signal) and a sentence saying the ownership test asks about companies not listed themselves. `owners.sanctions_ownership_block` returns it when there is no signal (it now takes the network), and adds `status` and `subject_designated` to the signal case. The test's rules, the signals, the lookup and the reports are unchanged. After deploy: `opencheck_owners` for 253400JT3MQWNDKMJE44 gives `status: not_met`, NSD under `not_counted`, and `subject_designated` OFAC, EU and UK.
