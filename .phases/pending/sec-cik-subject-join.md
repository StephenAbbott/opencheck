title: A zero-padded SEC CIK joins the unpadded one, so a US issuer's 13D/13G holders reach the owners list, the ownership map and integrated ownership.
readme: SEC EDGAR's record of a US issuer now joins the subject when another source spells the same CIK with leading zeros, so 13D/13G holders reach the MCP owners and map answers.
tests: backend +12, vitest +10
---
Found by the MCP testing of 7 Nov 2026 (Notion "MCP testing ahead of Connected Data London 2026", issue 1): Eli Lilly's `opencheck_ownership_map` said "No source names a current owner or controller" while the same lookup's `disclosure_status` said SEC EDGAR declares three shareholders. SEC EDGAR's own statement of the issuer carries `US-SEC-CIK 59478` and no LEI (it does not assert one); Wikirate's carries the LEI and `US-SEC-CIK 0000059478`. The merge keys kept the zeros, so `consistency.referent_groups` never joined the two, `subject_statements` and `lookup_canon` left the SEC EDGAR record out, and Lilly Endowment 9.6%, Vanguard Capital Management 6.48% and PNC 5.39% reached no owners list, map or integrated figure. `disclosure_status` found them through the source's `declarationSubject`, which is why one answer contradicted itself. Fix (Stephen: keys only, published identifiers unchanged): `identifiers.match_value(scheme, value)` strips leading zeros from an all-digit value under a `ZERO_PADDED_SCHEMES` scheme (`US-SEC-CIK` only — a Companies House number's leading zeros carry meaning) and `reconcile._identifier_keys` builds its scheme-scoped key from it; `matchValue` / `ZERO_PADDED_SCHEMES` in `frontend/src/lib/reconcile.ts` do the same for FullCheck's `identKeys`. The case table `frontend/src/lib/identifierMatchCases.json` is run by both sides and a backend test parses the TypeScript set. Saved reports join too, since the key is computed on read. Side finding, not fixed here: Wikirate's Eli Lilly record also carries `OPENCORPORATES-COMPANY-NUMBER U24239HR1993PTC034844`, Eli Lilly India's MCA number — no wrong merge (different scheme), worth reporting to Wikirate. After deploy: `opencheck_ownership_map` and `opencheck_owners` for FRDRIPF3EKNDJ2CQJL29 should show Lilly Endowment, Vanguard Capital Management and PNC from SEC EDGAR, and `integrated_ownership.holders` should no longer be empty.
