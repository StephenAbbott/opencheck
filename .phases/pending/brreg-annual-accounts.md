Brønnøysundregistrene opened the Regnskapsregisteret API to everyone on 1 October 2026: three filed years of company and group accounts, no key (until then only the latest year's key figures were open; the rest was for public authorities). `sources/brreg_accounts.py` reads it inside `BrregAdapter.fetch` into the bundle's `accounts`, with the register's list of years it holds a PDF copy for. Stephen's decisions (26 Oct 2026, the "Brreg annual accounts" ticket): part of the brreg bundle, not a new source; facts about the latest period only — period, journal number, group accounts filed, audit opt-out or unaudited, liquidation accounts, currency, accounting rules, small-company rules, parent company — and no turnover, result or assets (`summarise` reads no amount, and a test fails if one reaches the summary); a link to the latest filed PDF, never fetched; nothing in BODS (`map_brreg` ignores `accounts`, pinned); no risk signal or verdict clause. Four answers: 200 is `filed` (cached a day); 404 is `none`, worded "no annual accounts in the last three filed years" and never "overdue", because the API says nothing about the filing obligation (cached as an absence); a 500 naming an unsupported statement format — `BANK`, `LIV`, `SKADE` (DNB, Storebrand Livsforsikring, Gjensidige) — is `unsupported`, worded "annual accounts in a format the open API does not serve (bank / life insurance / non-life insurance)", an answer and not an outage (cached, no degradation); anything else is `unavailable`, a `source_read` degradation, never cached. A failed PDF list keeps the facts and drops the link. Brreg gains its first finding sentence (`finding_brreg`): a pending or final register status (deleted, bankrupt, compulsory liquidation, liquidation) and then the accounts clauses — "Latest annual accounts filed for 2025, with group accounts." for Equinor. The card lists the details under the sentence with the PDF link (`BrregAccountsFacts`, `lib/brregAccounts.ts`); read from the hit, so a saved report shows what the run recorded and fetches nothing. Fixtures are real answers of 26 Oct 2026. The API spells the field `regnkapsprinsipper`; Equinor's company accounts are simplified IFRS, its group's full IFRS. Licence: Brreg publishes its open datasets under NLOD; the notice states none for this API, so confirm with Brreg.
