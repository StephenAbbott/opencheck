The post-deploy re-test of the six curated examples (7 Nov 2026, MCP testing ticket) found two drawing leftovers. On Rosneft, the label of a return edge (the company holding part of one of its own owners, drawn as a curve) printed "Owns 100% OpenSanctions" over RN-NeftKapitalInvest's "Owns 9.6% Global Energy Monitor / Climate TRACE": the label tried five points on a short chord at the curve's apex and, finding none clear, took the first. A return edge's label now tries points along the curve itself, then steps below and above the apex, and failing all of those takes the spot that covers least; straight edges are placed first so the curve's label works around theirs. On Taqa Bratani, a wrapped label rounded each line's baseline on its own, so lines alternated 12 and 14 units apart and at 12 an 11-unit face touched the line above; the first baseline is now rounded and every later line is a whole 13 units below it. Re-rendered from the six production exports: no two labels overlap on any of the six maps. The PDF figure's layout is unchanged apart from the even line spacing.
