The "ur_latvia name search stubs" ticket, defect 1 from the scoping of the Estonia and Latvia Time Machine emitters. data.gov.lv dropped CKAN's `datastore_search_sql` action by 3 Nov 2026 (re-checked 5 Nov: HTTP 400 "Action name not known"), and `UrLatviaAdapter.search` fell back silently to its stub, so `/search?q=Latvenergo` carried no Latvian register candidate while the weekly sweep, which exercises `fetch`, read ok. The search is now `datastore_search` on the business register with a full-text `q` scoped to the `name` column (`search_url`, one request, 20 s timeout, under a second on 5 Nov). CKAN ranks a company's branches and trade-union locals above the company itself (34 rows match "Latvenergo"; AS "Latvenergo" is eighth), so a page of 100 rows is read and `rank_records` keeps the best ten: `search_rank.match_tier` on the better of the full name and the register's own `name_in_quotes` (the name without its legal form, which `names` does not know for Latvian forms), then live before closed, then a legal person before its branch, foreign branch, representative office or union local unit, then CKAN's order. A refused or failed search (an HTTP error, a body that is not JSON, `success: false`, no records list) raises `UrLatviaSearchError`, which `/search` records in `errors` as a source that did not answer; nothing is cached, and the message carries no URL. An empty answer is still no match. Offline without a cache the labelled stub is unchanged. The cache key moved to `ur_latvia/search-v2/`. The `register` resource used here was reloaded on 4 Nov 2026 (daily), unlike the stale historical-names copy. `tests/test_ur_latvia_search.py` pins the request shape against the portal's real answer of 5 Nov (`tests/fixtures/ur_latvia/search_latvenergo.json`). Not done: a sweep probe for `search()` — probes are one per source and ur_latvia's exercises `fetch`, so it is a follow-up.
