The posts are Markdown files in data/blog/posts with YAML frontmatter (title, subtitle, published, medium_url, topics, hero image), imported from Medium's "Download your information" export by backend/scripts/import_medium.py: a post is imported when it carries Medium's opencheck tag (Stephen tagged all twelve on 20 Nov 2026 and will tag new ones), its images are fetched from Medium's CDN and stored as WebP (33 images, 12.8 MB as PNG, 2.2 MB as WebP), link cards become plain links, and a link to another imported post is rewritten to /blog/<slug>. The author RSS feed was ruled out as the source because it holds only the latest ten posts and Medium's story pages answer 403 to scripts. opencheck/blog.py renders the files with markdown-it-py (CommonMark, raw HTML escaped) and routers/blog.py serves /blog (posts by month on a dated rail, topic chips that filter with ?topic= and are noindex), /blog/<slug> (an "About this post" panel with the date, reading time, the Medium origin, topics and a contents list; the date again in words and as publicationDate under the title), /blog/feed.xml (RSS 2.0 with each post's full text), /blog/sitemap.xml (named in robots.txt) and /blog/media/<slug>/<file>. The look is option B, "Ledger", chosen by Stephen from three mockups on the Notion ticket. Every post page names its own opencheck.world URL as rel=canonical and carries og:type article, article:published_time and a schema.org BlogPosting; the Medium story is credited, not canonical. The site header and footer link to /blog with a plain href (BLOG_PATH in views.ts) because the pages are server-rendered, and the Vite dev server proxies /blog. markdown-it-py and PyYAML are named as dependencies (both were already in the lock). After deploy: set each Medium story's canonical link to its opencheck.world URL in Medium's story settings, and check /blog, one post, /blog/feed.xml and /blog/sitemap.xml in production. Not fixed here: the Nigeria post's tenth example links Nestle Nigeria to MTN Nigeria's LEI, as it does on Medium.
