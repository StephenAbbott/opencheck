`sources/austender.py`, `scripts/build_austender_index.py`, `.github/workflows/refresh-austender-index.yml` (weekly, Tuesdays, release `austender-index`), `bods/mappers/australia.py::map_austender`, `finding_austender`, `_bh_austender` and `AusTenderContractsList` on the QuickCheck card. Measured on the registry's full file of 12 Nov 2026 (publication 19, CC BY 3.0 AU): 876,389 compiled releases, one contract each; 93.9% of the 880,821 supplier parties carry a valid eleven-digit ABN (53,793 suppliers, a 32 MB index, 15 MB gzipped). Contracts are activity, never ownership: no BODS relationship, no signal, no verdict clause — one entity statement with the ABN under `AU-ABN` and the totals in `entityType.details`. The join is GLEIF's filed ABN (RA000013) or the ABN implied by a filed ACN (RA000014: two check digits plus the nine-digit ACN) — the ACN route is 82% of the LEI matches in a 10,000-record sample; an ACN that takes two valid prefixes is refused when both are suppliers. The registry's flags shape the card: a supplier renamed under one ABN is one supplier (named as its latest notice names it), a notice naming two ABNs is flagged as shared, and because an amendment can overwrite a contract's status the card shows no status, only a neutral "marked cancelled in the data" chip with a caveat; the summed published values are said as OpenCheck's sum, never as money paid. The buying agency's contact point is never read. Each contract links to its notice on tenders.gov.au (`/Cn/Show/<uuid>`, from the award id).
