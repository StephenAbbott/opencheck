Synced from Stephen's Notion row (*Beneficial ownership access status*, Argentina, last verified 11 Nov 2026) ahead of the Argentina adapter: ARCA's Registro de Beneficiarios Finales is open to authorities and obliged entities only, and the company registers are the Registro Nacional de Sociedades and the Inspección General de Justicia's entity file on datos.jus.gob.ar. Until the adapter merges the sentence says OpenCheck reads no Argentine register; it then names `registro_sociedades_argentina` with no further edit, because `opencheck_reads` is derived from the REGISTRY. `test_knowability` and `test_entity_pages` take Uruguay as their example of a jurisdiction with no row.
