Third of the Estonia REST tickets, on the shared client. RIK's `GET /company/{code}` with `onlyValid=false` returns the rows the SOAP `detailandmed_v2` returns with history on, under the same `recordId` (Eesti Energia AS: 163 rows, 147 ended, on both; Bolt Operations OÜ: 15 and 11). New `rest_person_rows` reads the REST rows into SOAP's field names, so the full-history phase's join and strip run unchanged: a personal code joins an ended holding to the same person's current row, groups a former-only person's rows, and is then dropped; the stripped history is cached a day under a new key. Comparing the two services on four real companies found every person, date, role and share the same, and one difference: REST names a company shareholder by its name today ("Bolt Holdings OÜ") where SOAP names it as filed ("Taxify Shareholder SPV OÜ", "Lihtkonto: Rahandusministeerium"), and gives place names in English. Stephen's decision (17 Oct 2026): the ownership graph's history reads REST when `ARIREGISTER_REST_API_KEY` is set — its current names match the rest of the graph — and the History tab keeps SOAP, reading REST only when the SOAP credentials are not set. The beneficial-owner history stays on SOAP (REST's requires a named viewer and a stated reason); with the key and no SOAP credentials the holdings are shown and the missing owner history is said as not configured. A failed REST read is a degradation and is never cached. Fixtures are real REST and SOAP answers for Eesti Energia AS and Bolt Operations OÜ read within a minute of each other, every personal code replaced by the same fake in both; the parity test pins identical rows, identical ended holdings and identical statements apart from provenance and a company's spelling. The two deleted-company fixtures were re-read with the same scrubber. Production check: open an Estonian company with former shareholders and confirm `/signalstats` → `ariregister_rik.outcomes` counts `company|answered` and `soap_beneficial_owners|answered` per lookup, and `soap_detail` only when the History tab opens.
