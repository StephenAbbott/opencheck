---
title: The Senzing export types each identifier and names its country from a fixed table of schemes.
readme: The Senzing export now sends register numbers, tax numbers, passports and other databases' keys as their own Senzing features, with the issuing country read from the scheme, and leaves out industry and securities codes.
tests: backend +52
---
Prompted by OpenSanctions changelog #54 (13 Nov 2026), which moved their own Senzing export to typed identifiers: Senzing keeps two records apart when the identifier type and country match and the numbers differ, and treats a number with a different or missing type or country as supporting evidence only. OpenCheck never used that export, but its own exporter (`bods/senzing.py`) sent every non-LEI identifier as `NATIONAL_ID` with a country read from any two-letter prefix. That covered VAT and tax numbers (`PL-NIP`, `CZ-DIC`, `EE-KMKR`, `CA-BN`), CRO's NACE activity code, ISINs, BICs, MICs, Wikidata and OpenSanctions ids and the Estonian person key. `XI-VAT` got the country `XI`, and alpha-3 person schemes, GLEIF RA codes and `REG-<country>` got none. The test for an LEI read "LEI" anywhere in the scheme name, so a Leipzig register number would have gone out as an LEI. The new `bods/identifier_kinds.py` decides each scheme's kind from a fixed table, never from `schemeName`. The kinds are `lei`, `register` (`NATIONAL_ID`), `tax` (`TAX_ID`), `passport`, `other` (`OTHER_ID`: other databases' keys and register person keys) and `drop` (industry, securities and market codes). A register scheme is one the GLEIF mapper writes for an RA, a bare RA code, `REG` / `REG-<country>`, a US state subdivision or an adapter's own register scheme. The country comes from a two-letter ISO prefix, `REG-<country>` or a person document's alpha-3 prefix, and is left blank for `XI-` schemes, RA codes and unprefixed database keys. An unknown scheme is exported as `OTHER_ID`, which never splits two records, and the test suite's mapper guard fails any mapper that writes a scheme the table does not know. The table was built from every scheme the whole suite's mappers produce, person annotations included. Notion: Senzing export identifier enhancements - November 2026.
