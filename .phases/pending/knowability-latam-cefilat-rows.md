Thirteen rows drafted from CEFILAT and the Financial Transparency Coalition's 'The Invisible Owners' (Aug 2026; legislation to March 2026) on the Notion ticket 'Inspect CEFILAT report on South American beneficial ownership registers' and verified by Stephen on 23 Nov 2026. Seven have a BO register that only authorities and obliged entities can see: BZ, CR, EC, NI, PA, PY, UY (authorities_and_obliged_entities_only). The report finds no register in the region that is public or open to a legitimate interest. Six have no register: BO, CL, SV, GT, HN, MX (no_register). Chile and Guatemala have bills, and Mexico's Código Fiscal requires companies to keep BO information without filing it; each is given in pending_changes or notes. Only those thirteen rows and Japan's Last verified date (23 Nov 2026) were taken from the Notion table; every other row is as on main. A statement for one of these codes now reads from its row instead of the stated-absence sentence. test_knowability's unknown-jurisdiction case moved from Uruguay to Zambia, which has no row. No source was added: none of these registers can be read.
