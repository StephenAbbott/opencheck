Found by the post-deploy tester on 19 Nov 2026 (ticket "Bulgarian company missing from the open data reads 'Beneficial owners: Not public'"): СДРУЖЕНИЕ БЪЛГАРСКА СТОПАНСКА КАМАРА (254900GJOBKSEBN2NA75, EIK 831391124) has no entry in the Commercial Register's files since 2021, and its `bg_tr` disclosure status read `none_filed` for shareholders and `restricted` ("Not public") for beneficial owners — although the Bulgarian open data publishes owners. Cause: the note card's `raw` dropped the adapter's `not_found` flag, so `coverage._has_record` counted the card as a source with data, the disclosure status skipped its `no_record` branch, and the generic fill added `restricted` because Bulgaria's register access is restricted. The same omission was in eleven other index-tier hit builders (`ariregister`, `onrc_romania`, `jar_lithuania`, `edr_ukraine`, `cyprus_drcor`, `ica_israel`, `si_prs`, `nta_japan`, `oece_rnp`, `bce_belgium`, `corporations_canada`): each now keeps `not_found: True` on its note card, as INPI, Sirene, KvK and the name-gated registers already did. Effects: coverage names these sources under "Answered with no record" instead of counting them as with data, the MCP `sources` row reads `found: false` with the note, and the disclosure status is `no_record` (or `unreachable` when the read failed). A parametrized test pins the flag on all twelve builders, and a lookup-shaped test pins the Bulgarian case. After deploy, re-run the lookup for 254900GJOBKSEBN2NA75 with `refresh=true` and check `disclosure_status.sources.bg_tr` is `no_record`.
