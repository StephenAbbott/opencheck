tests: backend +2
---
MBIE's go-live notice for the Companies (Address Information) Amendment Act 2025 (18 Nov 2026) matched what the earlier alternative-address work had built for: NZBN v5 `addressType` ALTERNATIVE / PHYSICAL / null on directors' `roleAddress` and shareholders' `shareholderAddress`, chosen deliberately by `_role_address` and never used for a residence country; Role Search's empty `physicalAddress` read as withheld and followed up on the company's own NZBN record. OpenCheck does not call the Companies v2 API, so its `Alternative` spelling needs nothing. The new part of the notice is an update planned for 25 Nov 2026 that makes Role Search return the alternative address itself, untyped. The sandbox already does (checked 18 Nov 2026: MBIE's test director reads 25 The Terrace, the NZBN record's ALTERNATIVE block), and the opt-in smoke test that pinned the empty block would have reported it as a residential-address leak. It now accepts an empty block or the address the NZBN record files as ALTERNATIVE for that person, and still fails on anything else. Two unit tests pin the 25 Nov shape: an untyped block on the subject's alternative address is graded without a follow-up and stays at most medium, with or without a pafId. The BackgroundCheck caveat no longer says the name search always publishes no address. `docs/nz-associations.md` records the notice and the sandbox reading. After 25 Nov, the scheduled post-go-live check should confirm production matches the sandbox.
