title: OpenCorporates refuses a company number that names another company, and its card no longer reads "None".
readme: OpenCorporates' record is now checked against GLEIF's legal name, so a wrong company number in GLEIF's mapping shows a note card instead of another company's record.
tests: backend +17
---
The lookup reaches OpenCorporates through GLEIF's LEI-to-OpenCorporates mapping (`ctx.ocid`), and nothing checked that the record it names is the subject's. American Foreign Policy Council (`549300W96W2VKSMVDF81`, a lapsed LEI) is linked to `us_dc/L21249`, which OpenCorporates holds for CONNIE-19 STREET LLC — the collision `dlcp_dc` already name-gates — so the report carried CONNIE-19's status, founding date and two officers against AFPC, the hit asserted AFPC's LEI and `RECORD_CONSISTENCY` counted the wrong company as agreement (post-deploy tester, 16 Nov 2026). `OpenCorporatesAdapter` is now `lookup_pass_legal_name`: `fetch(ocid, legal_name=)` compares GLEIF's legal name with OpenCorporates' current, previous and alternative names (`names_agree`, the `dlcp_dc` rule: comparable name, despaced, distinctive tokens, similarity ≥ 0.88) and on a mismatch returns `name_mismatch` with the name OpenCorporates holds and asks for no officers or network. The dispatch, `/deepen` and the deepen and count passes all pass the name (`_legal_name_kwargs`), so a refused number is never mapped. A name in a script that cannot be compared letter by letter (CJK) never refuses. A mismatch is a note card (`_bh_opencorporates_mismatch`): no identifier, `not_found`, the finding names the company OpenCorporates holds and says GLEIF's link may be wrong. The OpenCorporates hit now asserts the LEI only where OpenCorporates' own record carries it (`publishes_lei`), the identifier corroboration rule. Separately, the card's summary dropped `· None` when OpenCorporates sends `current_status: null` (Equinor ASA read "OC no/923609016 · None", Moody's "OC us_de/2882059 · None"). After deploy: AFPC's report shows the OpenCorporates note card and no CONNIE-19 statements; Equinor's row reads "OC no/923609016".
