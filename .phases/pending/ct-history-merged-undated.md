title: A Connecticut business the register records as merged says so on its History tab, even when the merger was filed on the other business.
readme: A Connecticut business recorded as merged or consolidated now shows that ending on its History tab even when the merger certificate sits on the surviving business's record.
tests: backend +5
---
The post-deploy check of the Connecticut History tab found merged businesses (Accurate Wire, Tabs Group) with no ending row: the merger certificate is filed on the survivor's Filing History, so nothing on their own record said they had ended. `timeline/ct_sots.py` now adds one Tier 2 `SUCCESSION` row from the Business Master when a domestic business's status is Merged or Consolidation and no merger or consolidation certificate is on its own record. It is worded from the register ("Merged into another business, as the register records; no merger filing is on this business's record, and the register does not name the other business"), dated by the Master's `dissolution_date` where given and otherwise undated (sorted last, shown as "date unknown"). A business with a certificate still gets exactly one row; a foreign business marked merged gets none, since its Connecticut registration ending is not a succession. After deploy, check the History tab for Accurate Wire (213800EQOHV27NLR7927) and Tabs Group (549300U050DGZYWKPK21).
