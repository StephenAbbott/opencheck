`gcis_taiwan` reads 公司登記基本資料-應用一 and 公司登記董監事資料集 live on the Unified Business Number GLEIF files under RA000551 (500 of the 504 ISSUED LEIs resolve; RA001009 files funds' tax numbers and is not dispatched). A number with no company record reads 統編查是否為公司、分公司及商業 for the note card (a branch, a sole-trader business, a limited partnership on its own register); a malformed number is never sent (check digit). The platform's Chinese refusals on HTTP 200 are degradations, never cached; requests are paced at 60 a minute; the TWCA chain is accepted under Python 3.13 by a per-host SSL context (`build_client(verify=)`, used by this source only). Directors' and supervisors' holdings (Company Act art. 393) are `shareholding` interests — shares for a 股份有限公司, NT$ capital for a 有限公司 — with a percentage OpenCheck computes and annotates where a denominator exists; never beneficial ownership. A legal person holding a seat is an entity with one interest per seat and one holding (never summed across its representatives); supervisors and a foreign company's representatives are officer-marked `otherInfluenceOrControl`. `TW-CR` joins `_GLEIF_RA_TO_ORG_ID`, so FullCheck can hop to the register. Resolving legal-person holders to their own UBN is a v2 ticket.
