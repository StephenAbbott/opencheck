Second of the Estonia REST tickets, on the shared client. Stephen's decisions (17 Oct 2026): every code the open-data index does not hold is read from `GET /company/{code}` (every section, `onlyValid=false`) when `ARIREGISTER_REST_API_KEY` is set — a deleted company, which the files never carry, or one entered after the cut; a code the index holds never calls REST; a deleted company says "Deleted" in the verdict. New `sources/ariregister_rest_company.py` translates the answer by allowlist into the index's row vocabulary: a natural person's personal code groups their rows into one key and is dropped, a legal person keeps its Estonian registry code, free texts are scrubbed with a copy of the builder's `scrub_text` (pinned equal), and the bundle is refused if any personal data survives. Current rows are the current lists; every ended shareholding is history in the full-history phase's shape, so the graph draws one closed record per former holder; founders are kept as ownership history. REST publishes no beneficial owners, so the card says they were not read rather than "none filed". A deleted company carries its `dissolutionDate` (REST's `deletionDate`), its former names as `alternateNames` with dated annotations, and its merger or division partners (never itself). Outcomes match the page fallback: RIK's 404 `code: 4043` is a note card with no degradation; a refusal (spent budget, busy slot, 5xx, timeout) is a note card and a degradation and is never cached; a success is cached a day. Without a key the page answers as before. The verdict gains an opening sentence for any register's terminal status — "Deleted on 10 January 2017, according to the Estonian e-Business Register." where the register's word is "deleted", "Dissolved on …" otherwise (`VERDICT_TEMPLATE` 6); the status chip says "Deleted" by the same rule. The finding leads "Deleted from the register on 18 August 2017; 15 ended ownership records; beneficial owners not read; …"; a live company newer than the cut leads "Entered on <day>, after the open data's cut". Fixtures are real REST answers of 17 Oct 2026 with every personal code replaced.
