The Registre National des Entreprises that `inpi` reads holds only units with a business activity, so an association or foundation answered 404 there: of the 3,070 ISSUED French LEIs with an association or foundation legal form (11 Nov 2026) INPI holds 25 and Sirene 3,069. Stephen's decisions A–G on the Notion ticket "France / Sirene + RNA for associations and foundations": a separate `sirene` source (A), dispatched on every French SIREN through the `siren` key INPI's deriver already builds from RA000189 / RA000192 / RA001129 (B); INSEE's API Sirene 3.11 with the key `INSEE_SIRENE_API_KEY` in the `X-INSEE-Api-Key-Integration` header, never the URL, paced at 30 requests a minute by `host_throttle` (C) — `GET /siren` for the legal unit and its current period, then `GET /siret` for the head office's address; the RNA from a monthly index (D), joined on the RNA number Sirene carries (`identifiantAssociationUniteLegale`), written under the scheme `FR-RNA` (E); the `FR-INSEE` register hop asks INPI first and Sirene when INPI answers not_found, in the register-number pipeline and the FullCheck hop (F, `RegisterHop.fallback`); Sirene history is a follow-up (G). The index (`scripts/build_rna_index.py`, `.github/workflows/refresh-rna-index.yml`, monthly on the 5th, release `rna-index`, downloaded at boot by `rna_index.warm_index`) packs the waldec file kept to the associations a fully public Sirene unit names — read from INSEE's StockUniteLegale parquet — which takes it from 1.6 GB (415 MB gzipped) to about 540 MB (176 MB gzipped), and keeps an allowlist of columns: the management address (`adrg_*`, usually the declarant's home), the director's title, `observation` and `publiweb` are dropped by the builder and refused by the bundle schema. Traps: W numbers carry a letter in the third place for Paris (`W9G2…`); `0001-01-01` and `1900-01-01` are no date, and a Sirene creation date of 1900-01-01 takes the RNA's creation date with a transformation annotation, or none; Windows-1252 punctuation arrives as C1 controls and is repaired. A unit INSEE does not publish (`statutDiffusionUniteLegale` `P`, or a 403 "non diffusable") is a note card, never cached or mapped; a 404 is a cached absence; a 401 or other 403 is a `not_configured` degradation, a 429 `rate_limited`, a failed head-office read a `source_read` degradation and the unit is not cached. Entity statements only — Sirene and the RNA name no officers, members or owners; catégories juridiques 71–73 are `stateBody`.
