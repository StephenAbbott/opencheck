title: INPI officers carry the country of their home address.
readme: Officers from France's Registre National des Entreprises now carry their country of residence, read from the country of their home address.
tests: backend +5
---
The third step of the Companies House country-of-residence ticket, from its live payload check of 3 Nov 2026. The RNE publishes, on every non-BO officer (`pouvoirs[].individu`), the country of their home address — `adresseDomicile.codePays` (ISO 3166-1 alpha-3) and `adresseDomicile.pays` (the French name) — with the street withheld (only `voiePresent` / `numVoiePresent` flags). Danone SA carried a country on 11 of 11 officers, a second large issuer on 14 of 14; `descriptionPersonne.nationalite`, the only country the mapper read, was empty on every officer checked, so these people had none. `map_inpi` now publishes it as a `residence` address through `bods/residence.py`: **a country only** — not the commune, not the postcode — the code where the RNE gives one (no note, since the filed value is the ISO code), else the French name as filed with a `commenting` note saying no ISO country could be read from it. Beneficial-owner rows are still never read, here or anywhere (Loi Sapin II). The same check found OpenCorporates publishes no residence field: its officer `address` is a single service-address string and `nationality` a demonym already mapped by name. Same effect on signals as the earlier steps: the ICIJ jurisdiction gate now has a country for these officers. Person `statementId`s move (content-addressed); `recordId`s do not.
