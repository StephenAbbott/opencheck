`docs/identifiers.md` is the identifier counterpart to `docs/dates.md`: what each of the three identifiers on an OpenCheck statement answers, what is stable across runs (the `recordId`, the key everything joins on) and what changes (the content-addressed `statementId`, Phase 398), what is outside the digest (annotations, dates, `retrievedAt`, `publicListing`), how `declarationSubject` names each source's own record of the looked-up company (Phase 396) and how a source's several records of the subject fold onto one, that relationships reference parties by `recordId`, how another publisher's statements are carried, how to read two exports, and the checks that hold the rules (the BODS quality sweep's `stale_statement_id` and `declaration_subject_not_subject`, the test suite, `/signalstats.declaration_subject`). It links the standard's record-identifier and information-update guidance at the pinned 0.4.0 URLs. The /about BODS spine card used to say statement ids were "SHA-256 of source + type + local key" and never changed between runs — true of the `recordId`, and false of the `statementId` since Phase 398; it now describes the three identifiers and links the page, which also joins the reading list beside the BODS record-identifier guidance. `docs/dates.md` and `docs/bods-quality.md` cross-link it. `BehindTheScenesPage.test.tsx` pins the two links, the new wording and the absence of the old sentence.
