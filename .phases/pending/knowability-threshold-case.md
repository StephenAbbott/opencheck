Found by the post-deploy tester on 27 Nov 2026 (Notion: "Indonesia's What can be known sentence reads holders of More than 25%"): `knowability._recorded_sentences` set the Notion table's *Threshold wording* verbatim after "it records holders of", and Indonesia's row opens with a capital. The same sentence reaches QuickCheck's band, the entity pages, the PDF/Markdown report and the MCP `opencheck_knowability` tool. Fixed in the code rather than the row, because the table is copied from statutes and assessments and five more rows already open with a capital (IN, KE, MH, MV, RW) — they show no sentence today only because their access status is not yet recorded. `knowability.threshold_clause` lower-cases the first letter (after an opening quotation mark), keeps an acronym or a figure as written, and gives a wording that names the person rather than the holding (Mauritius: "A natural person who holds…") as "it records a natural person who…", never "holders of a natural person". A test runs every row of the synced table through it. `docs/knowability.md` regenerated (Indonesia's sentence only).
