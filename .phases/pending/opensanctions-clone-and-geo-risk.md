Found on 6 Nov 2026 in model.json (30 target topics, two more than the 5 August list) and researched on 10 Nov 2026 against the OpenSanctions crawlers, FollowTheMoney and the published data. corp.clone ("Clone of real entity", FtM 30 Aug) is set by one dataset, gb_fca_notices (the FCA Warning List): 3,235 of its 18,422 targets, every one LegalEntity, every one also reg.warn, none with an LEI, and each named with the real firm's name ("Citibank UK Ltd / CitiGold Wealth Management", "Rothschild Financial"). The dataset is not in the default collection OpenCheck searches yet, so nothing reaches a lookup today. geo.risk ("High-risk area", FtM 2 Sept) belongs to the new Territory schema for designations of a place (FATF lists, FinCEN 311, OFAC Crimea), which no crawler sets yet and which a LegalEntity or Person search never returns. Both join _RISK_TOPICS and _PUBLISHED_TARGET_TOPICS (the search cache re-keys through _TOPIC_FINGERPRINT) and risk._INFORMATIONAL_TOPICS, with labels "clone of a real firm" and "high-risk area" in topics.py and vocab.ts. No signal for a clone on the firm it copies, because it would rest on a name match alone and assert the impersonation against its victim; finding_opensanctions instead opens a clone record's row with "A firm reported as copying a real firm's name; the record is the copy, not that firm", ahead of the topic list the 140-character cap drops first. New @pytest.mark.live test test_risk_topics_match_the_live_published_target_topics reads model.json (schema metadata, key-free) in the weekly live-smoke workflow, so the next unannounced topic fails a check instead of being found by chance. After deploy: once gb_fca_notices joins the default collection, look up a large UK bank and check its clone records read as copies.
