---
title: Entity pages load their fonts from the site, not Google Fonts.
readme: The server-rendered entity pages now take their fonts from the site's own stylesheet, the same files the app uses, instead of Google Fonts.
tests: backend +1
---
The last follow-up of the "Speed up first load" ticket. The SEO entity pages (`entity_pages.py`) are rendered by the backend, which cannot know the build's hashed font filenames, so they still opened fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. The frontend build now writes `fonts.css` beside `index.html` (`fontsStylesheet` in `vite.config.ts`): the `@font-face` rules of `src/fonts.css` pointing at the same hashed woff2 files the app's stylesheet uses, so a reader who has opened the app already has them cached; a font missing from the bundle fails the build. The entity page head links `/fonts.css` (`FONTS_STYLESHEET`), served under the unhashed-file rule (an hour, stale-while-revalidate a day), and `TEMPLATE_VERSION` is 3 so crawlers re-fetch the pages. Without a built frontend the link misses and the page draws in the fallback faces its CSS already names. Also removes a fallback `.woff` URL, mislabelled `format('woff2')` and never shipped, from the four DM Mono rules in `src/fonts.css`. After deploy: an entity page requests `/fonts.css` and three or four `/assets/*.woff2` files and nothing from Google; `/fonts.css` answers `text/css` with `public, max-age=3600, stale-while-revalidate=86400`.
