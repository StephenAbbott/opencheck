Follow-up to Phase 120, which scoped `names.distinctive_token_agreement` to the ICIJ screen and sized the extension from data. Above the unchanged 0.88 score, `cross_check._signals_from_os` and `openaleph_check` now require an organisation pair to agree on its distinctive tokens (legal forms and filler stripped). Which pairs count as organisations is one classifier, `names.screens_as_organisation`, now shared by all three screens: an entity target always, a person target only when either name carries a legal form (a corporate officer filed as a person). Person names with no legal form bypass and keep the corroboration ladder; BackgroundCheck (`/person-check`) is untouched; `name_similarity` and `eval_name_matching.py` are unchanged. On OpenAleph the gate subsumes the Phase 119 empty-residue guard. Re-measured before merging on the live pool, 16 Oct 2026: 24 production `/lookup`s (the findings golden set plus the 14 ICIJ-evaluation subjects), 122 entity-type OpenSanctions/OpenAleph signals; the gate drops none. The Rosneft «Общество с ограниченной ответственностью» `RELATED_SANCTIONS_LINKED` collision measured in August no longer appears in production (Rosneft's «Общество…» signals are now exact-name matches), so this lands as a guard against the next collision rather than a removal, and no curated card or narrative changes. `watchlist.SIGNAL_RULES` is 5, naming the related-party listing codes and the 50% test, so a re-run never reports a gated-out match as a company change. `docs/risk-signals.md` states the gate and its measured cost; `docs/icij-distinctive-token-evaluation.md` records the re-measurement; `eval_icij_distinctive.py surfaces` uses the shared classifier. Check in production: the findings regression and `/signalstats` show no RELATED_* movement on the next weekly run.
