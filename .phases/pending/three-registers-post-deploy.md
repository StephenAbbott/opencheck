The production tests of the three sources wired on 29 Oct 2026 (Phases 419–421) passed the checks their tickets asked for — each card answers with a dated snapshot chip, each register's identifier corroborates GLEIF's (`UA-EDR 20077720`, the dotted `BE-BCE_KBO 0202.239.951`, the Greek `CY-DRCOR ΗΕ 449149`), `opencheck_register_lookup` works for all three schemes — and found three defects. (1) The ЄДР writes a SIGNER string as `NAME[; powers] - керівник|представник`, and `parse_role_holder` split on `;` alone, so a signer with no stated powers became the person "КОМАРОВ ОЛЕКСАНДР ВАЛЕРІЙОВИЧ - керівник" — 398,929 of 1,094,376 signer rows on the 27 Oct export, every one of them reaching the related-party screens and BackgroundCheck under that name; `split_role_tail` moves the two role words into the role, and `strip_role_tail` applies the same rule when the adapter reads a row, so the index already published is right without a rebuild. (2) The National ID picker asked for a Belgian enterprise number as ten digits, but GLEIF files it dotted under RA000025 and `registeredAs` is an exact-string filter, so no Belgian company could be found; `enterprise_number_spellings` searches both spellings, the French SIREN arrangement, and the picker accepts either. (3) The picker's own Cypriot placeholder `HE 449149` found nothing because GLEIF stores `ΗΕ 449149` in Greek letters; `he_number_spellings` searches Greek, Latin and the bare number. Check in production: `/resolve-national-id?country=BE&number=0202239951` → Proximus, `?country=CY&number=HE 449149` → BERN INVESTMENTS, and Kyivstar's register lookup names its head without the role word.
