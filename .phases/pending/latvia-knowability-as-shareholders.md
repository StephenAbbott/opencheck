---
title: Latvia's knowability row records that AS shareholders who are natural persons are no longer public.
readme: Latvia's knowability statement now records that joint-stock company shareholders who are natural persons moved to the non-public part of the register on 19 November 2026.
---
The Register of Enterprises moved information on joint-stock company (AS) shareholders who are natural persons, and the shareholders' register section, into the non-public part of the registration file on 19 Nov 2026, following the Constitutional Court (Satversmes tiesa) judgment of 9 Nov 2026 in case 2024-01-01. Three proposals in "EU legitimate interest — proposed updates" were accepted by Stephen and applied to the Latvia row of the Notion access-status table: Notes (the change, with the data.gov.lv stockholders dataset answering 403 from 19 Nov 2026), Company register publishes (officers, shareholders, exact shareholdings, annual accounts; previously empty) and Sources (UR's notice). jurisdictions.json was re-synced for Latvia with sync_jurisdictions.py --rows-json and docs/knowability.md regenerated. The beneficial-ownership access status stays public: the notice does not touch beneficial owners or SIA members. Check in production: /knowability?jurisdictions=LV.
