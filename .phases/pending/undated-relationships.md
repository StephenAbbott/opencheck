Prompted by Rosneft's FullCheck of 11 Nov 2026. BP Russian Investments read as a current owner of about 20% four years after BP announced its exit. All 120 of the lookup's ownership relationships were undated, and the BP holding arrived twice: once from Global Energy Monitor (19.75%), and once through OpenSanctions' republished copy of the same Global Energy Ownership Tracker rows ("at least 21.9%"). Stephen's decisions are recorded on the Notion ticket "How to deal with data sources where relationships have no start date or end date": keep undated relationships and label them; fold OpenSanctions' copy into GEM's own read, keeping it only as a labelled fallback; keep integrated-ownership figures with a note; no editorial overlay for known events, report upstream instead.

`bods/undated.py` (mirrored in `lib/undated.ts`) holds the one rule. A current relationship with an ownership or control interest and no `startDate` or `endDate` is undated; roles never are. Each such statement gets a `commenting` annotation as it leaves a `@content_addressed` mapper. The graph label, legend and tree, and the PDF diagram, all say "no date given". Integrated ownership marks each such figure `undated` and prints `undated_note` under the table, in the reports, in the annotation and in the MCP block. Its route no longer calls the subject's own subsidiary a route: the give-back now reads "directly, with its share of the company's own shares held by X", told apart by reachability that does not pass through the subject (T-Rank 3.4 keeps "through C").

FtM edges carried only by `gem_energy_ownership` get `source.assertedBy` Global Energy Monitor, a note and GEM's citation. An undated FtM edge also gets the day its publisher first recorded it. `bods/republished.py` drops those edges about the subject, and any OpenSanctions party only they named, when GEM's own read answered for the subject. This rides on a new `republished_folded` event, which `fold_lookup_events` applies, so the response, the exports and saved reports fold the same way. `/deepen` and FullCheck hops keep the labelled edge.

This builds on the FtM ownership-date fix (its own pull request), so EGRUL holdings that carry an end date close rather than reading as undated. After deploy, check Rosneft's FullCheck: one BP node, from GEM, labelled "no date given", and integrated ownership showing the undated note. The curated card's signals should be unchanged. Upstream notes to GEM and OpenSanctions are drafted on the ticket for Stephen to send.
