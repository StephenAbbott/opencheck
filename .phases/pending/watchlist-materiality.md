Part A of the "Watchlist as pKYC: materiality, context and review record" ticket (Notion), prompted by Moody's "KYC continuum part 2": the watchlist answered *what changed?* and not *does it matter?*. A `coverage_changed` from a briefly degraded register sat beside `signal_new: SANCTIONED` with equal weight. New module `opencheck/watchlist_materiality.py` grades every change the diff can name into one of three tiers from a closed table: `KIND_TIER` per `CHANGE_KIND`, `GLEIF_FIELD_TIER` per material GLEIF field (pinned to `FIELD_WORDS`), `event_tier` per Legal Entity Event type and status, `SIGNAL_TIER` per emittable code (pinned to the `test_signal_label_coverage` enumeration plus `RECORD_CONSISTENCY`; its own table, deliberately not the graph badge severity, which inverts `COUNTER_SANCTIONED` on purpose), and `REGISTER_TRIGGER_TIER` for the Estonian register tier's own trigger, since a register entry is written even when the snapshot moved nothing. The entry's tier is the worst of its changes'; an unknown kind, field, code, type or list is recheck, never silent. Stephen's decisions (17 Oct 2026): `signal_unchecked` and every `context_*` kind are note; `jurisdiction` is escalate; `direct_children`, `OFFSHORE_LEAKS` and `STATE_CONTROLLED` are note; `IN_PROGRESS` events sit one tier below `COMPLETED` and `WITHDRAWN_CANCELLED` is note; the labels are "Note only / Recheck the source / Escalate" — source, not register, because OpenSanctions or ICIJ can prompt a recheck. Graded once at write time and kept: migration 3 adds `materiality` and `materiality_rules` to `entries`, `add_entry` grades, and a pre-grading row is graded on read with `materiality_rules` null so the page can say "graded under today's table". Shown on `/watch/{token}` (entry and change), in the Atom feed as a second `<category scheme="https://opencheck.world/watchlist/materiality">` beside the unchanged tier category with the tier word prefixed to the title for the two material tiers, on `/watchlist` as a chip with a "Show: All · Material only" filter (`?show=material`, remembered per browser, default All so a stale list stays visible) and a per-change tag, and on `/watchstats` as `entries_by_materiality` — the distribution that says whether the lines are drawn right. The three tables are stated in `docs/watchlist.md` and pinned to the module by `test_watchlist_materiality.py`, as are the frontend's labels and sentences. No new detection, no per-list override, no automatic re-run: triage as data, not workflow. Post-deploy: read `entries_by_materiality` after a week and record it on the stage A ticket; if note is not the large majority, revisit the tables with a `MATERIALITY_RULES` bump.
