Found in the post-deploy test of the failed-reads fix (19 Nov 2026). From that day, data.gov.lv answers HTTP 403 "Pieeja liegta: Lietotājs nav tiesīgs lasīt resursu" to anonymous reads of the AS stockholders resource (`6adabd83-…`). The other ten UR resources still answer. This follows the Constitutional Court's judgment of 9 Nov 2026 (case 2024-01-01), which moves natural-person AS shareholders into the non-public part of the file. Production History therefore read every Latvian company as "answered only in part … try again later". Now a 403 on that one table (`_WITHHOLDABLE`) is named in `fetch_timeline_data`'s new `withheld` list and reaches `/history` as `registers_withheld: {"ur_latvia": ["stockholders"]}`; it is not listed in `registers_unavailable`. The tab says it with `withheldRegisterLines`, in muted note text and not the warning box, as the register's restriction and not a finding about the company. Any other status on that table, and a 403 on any other table, is still a failure. If UR reopens the table to the public, nothing needs to change. After deploy: `/history?lei=984500ADQ50FFDAB6260` (AS "Remars-Rīga") should show `registers_withheld` and an empty `registers_unavailable`.
