Step C of the "Daily deltas for OpenCheck" ticket, after the two-week shadow (14–27 Nov 2026: ownership and sanctions 44–124 a weekday, ceased 10–55, no errors or gaps), with Stephen's decisions on the mock (13 Nov). The page (`components/ChangesPage.tsx`, `lib/dailyChanges.ts`) reads `/daily-changes` and shows one day (`?day=`, else the newest complete day): ownership and sanctions open and cut at five, ceased, ceased on an already-lapsed LEI and record changes folded, new LEIs and note-only changes counted and not listed, an OpenSanctions listing in the risk chip, "Run a check" the reader's choice. "Changes" joins the top nav as an experiment. The file (`opencheck/daily_changes_file.py`) is rebuilt whole after each GLEIF delta from every GLEIF row the day holds, every band, each LEI mapped from the Golden Copy mirror the way a lookup's anchor is (`_snapshot_bundle(children=False)` + `map_gleif` in `mapping_provenance`), children dropped, parents' entity statements kept so references resolve, a removed parent published as a closed relationship under the open statement's recordId with no endDate. It lives on the persistent disk beside the store, pruned with it, and is served at `/changes/<day>.jsonl.gz` (a day-long cache once the day is over), `/changes/latest.jsonl.gz` (302 to the newest) and `/changes/<day>.csv`; OpenSanctions is never in the BODS file. After deploy: open opencheck.world/changes, then check that the first GLEIF delta wrote `/var/data/changes/<day>.jsonl.gz` and that `/changes/latest.jsonl.gz` redirects to it.
