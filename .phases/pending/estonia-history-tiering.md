The follow-up to the Estonia–Latvia emitters role re-tiering (Notion "Estonian History tiering: what Phase 442 left on Tier 1"). Checking every person-role code in the register's vocabulary (47, daily index of 25 Oct 2026) and the real SOAP and REST history of Eesti Energia AS and Bolt Technology OÜ against `timeline/ariregister.py::role_kind` found `O` — the OÜ shareholder off the card, in both the SOAP and the REST history — in no role table, so every private limited company's shareholder history sat on Tier 3 (Bolt Technology OÜ: 643 rows); and `YHLLV` (cooperative member with additional liability) on the board stream while `YHL` was ownership. Both are now in `OWNERSHIP_ROLES`; a test pins the stream of all 47 codes, and another that every role `ariregister_history.SHAREHOLDER_ROLES` counts and every role the mapper types `otherInfluenceOrControl` is ownership. Stephen's decisions (4 Nov 2026): a beneficial-owner-list row coded `J` / `C` (a board or council member named because no owner reached the threshold — the mapper's `kasusaaja_fallback`) is the Tier-4 board stream, worded "named on the beneficial-owner list because no owner was identified", in `_bo_events` for both the SOAP and REST card paths (Eesti Energia: 36 of 42 BO history rows); partners (`TOSAN`, `UOSAN`, `EUSOS`, `EUSOS2`) stay on the board stream as the mapper types them. After deploy, check `/history` for Eesti Energia (`5493005044RTLQ5RZU70`): Tier 1 holds the stockholders and the few real BO rows, the J rows are Tier-4 "named on the beneficial-owner list…"; and an OÜ with SOAP history (Bolt Technology OÜ) shows its shareholders on Tier 1.
