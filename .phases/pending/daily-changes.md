From the Notion ticket "Daily deltas for OpenCheck" (13 Nov 2026). Measured on 6–13 Nov 2026 a weekday's GLEIF delta names 7,000–24,000 LEIs, 55–80% of them renewal churn, and the watchlist's own grading would put 1,500 reporting-exception moves a day in front of a reader, so the list has its own stricter bands: ownership and sanctions head it (Stephen's choice), endings on an LEI that had already lapsed are set apart, and exceptions, register identifiers and registration status are notes. `opencheck/daily_changes.py` reads the Golden Copy mirror before and after each delta in `mirror_refresh.apply_delta` and reads OpenSanctions' entity deltas against a local table of each LEI-bearing organisation's topics and datasets, seeded once from the full export; it never runs a lookup. OpenSanctions rows are references only (entity id, topic codes, dataset names), matched on `leiCode`, organisations only. Sole proprietors are left out. Served as JSON at `/daily-changes` and `/daily-changes/{day}.json`, counted on `/signalstats`, thirty days kept, off unless `OPENCHECK_DAILY_CHANGES_DB_FILE` is set. Shadow mode for two weeks: no page and no BODS file; the watchlist's materiality table is unchanged. To check in production: `/signalstats` → `daily_changes` shows a baseline of about 10,800 OpenSanctions organisations after the first tick and a day's counts after the next GLEIF delta.
