Three fixes from the first production check of Prozorro on Epicentr K (24 Nov 2026). Prozorro gives a contract the status `terminated` when it ends, whether it was carried out (it carries `amountPaid` and an empty `terminationDetails`) or broken off, so the card, the finding and the BODS details say "ended" and the footnote says the two are not told apart; the finding now puts the ended count second, so the 140-character cap drops the buyers first (Epicentr K's 3,311 had fallen off). A contract often changes long after its tender last did, so after two runs 43% of the harvest's contracts had no procedure ("Procedure not read" on 24% of Epicentr K's): `harvest_methods_back` reads the tenders feed backwards (`descending=1`) from the harvest's start to two years before it, and `fill_missing_methods` reads any tender still missing one at a time (`GET /tenders/{id}`, ~37 a second, at most 60,000 a run), in the last fifth of each run's budget; a tender Prozorro no longer has is kept with an empty procedure. A signing day before 2015 or after the build (2004-03-16 on one contract) is not a date: the contract is counted without it and `signed_date_implausible` counts it. After merge, the next refresh runs fill the procedures; nothing to dispatch by hand beyond the backfill runs already under way.
