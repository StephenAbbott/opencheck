The weekly BODS quality sweep had failed every week since 26 Oct 2026 on `declaration_subject_not_subject|openaleph`. OpenAleph's name steps (FtM match, percolation, `q=`) returned procurement records of the subject — two OCDS parties for Regulatory DataCorp Limited on 16 Nov, one for Equinor ASA from 2 Nov — that carry nothing but their own OpenAleph id. Nothing joins them to the subject's referent group, so each was deepened as its own network and OpenAleph named two subjects. Stephen chose option 3 on the ticket "OpenAleph records of Equinor that join no referent group" (17 Nov 2026): `_openaleph_strategies` flags a hit a name step found that has no identifier beyond its OpenAleph id and no identifier corroboration (`raw["name_only_match"]`), and the pipeline keeps its row on the card but neither deepens nor counts it. A record found by the LEI, OpenCorporates URL or registration-number filters, or one carrying a number, is mapped as before; the country-less `REG` join (option 1) is still open. The sweep's other failure that night, SEC EDGAR's `/deepen` for Moody's, was an upstream timeout; the error read "SEC EDGAR request failed: " with nothing after it because httpx timeouts carry an empty message, and now names the exception (`describe_exception`). After deploy: `/lookup?lei=894500LD30X1VN839203&refresh=true` should carry no OpenAleph entity statement without an identifier beyond the OpenAleph id, and the next sweep should have no `declaration_subject_not_subject|openaleph`.
