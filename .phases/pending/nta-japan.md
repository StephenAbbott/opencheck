`nta_japan` (`sources/nta_japan.py`, `bods/mappers/japan.py`, `scripts/build_nta_japan_index.py`, `.github/workflows/refresh-nta-japan-index.yml`). The NTA publishes the whole register monthly as one zip behind a token-carrying form POST, with an OpenPGP signature; the builder fetches it, checks the signature against the NTA key committed beside it (fingerprint F4109E82…4D615230) and refuses repeated numbers, more than ten malformed rows or fewer than five million rows. On the 30 Oct 2026 file: 5,850,621 bodies (790,701 closed), 118 s, 1.3 GB of SQLite, 425 MB gzipped, kept on the persistent disk (`NTA_JAPAN_DB_FILE`). Search is a trigram FTS5 table over open records' Japanese and English names. GLEIF's `RA000412` files the 12-digit 会社法人等番号, which becomes the 13-digit corporate number by its check digit (`identifiers.japanese_corporate_number`, a `transformation` note on the GLEIF identifier); `RA001075` files the corporate number itself; both map to `JP-JCN` by value, so the register hop and `REG-JP` follow, and `RA000413` (FSA fund codes) is never dispatched. The register publishes identity and standing only — no officers, shareholders or owners. Kinds 101/201 are `stateBody`, 499 `legalEntity`, the rest `registeredEntity`; no ELF or ISO 5009 code is written, because the kind does not name a form precisely enough. A closed record is terminal, dated by its close date with the cause; a merger's successor is an entity statement with notes on both sides and no relationship (the ClimateTRACE rule); 検索対象除外 (the address no longer exists) is a note, never a status; the 2015 assignment date is never a founding date. The Web-API path (`NTA_JAPAN_APP_ID`, applied for) reads a number the index lacks: success cached a day, a definitive none `put_absent`, a failure a degradation and never cached, the NTA's required Japanese notice on the attribution. Licence `PDL-1.0` (CC BY 4.0 compatible) added to the licensing table. Japan gains a row in the knowability table, drafted in Notion as unverified (no public BO register; the voluntary 実質的支配者リスト is issued to the company only) for review. After merge, run `refresh-nta-japan-index` once by hand: until the asset exists the source is never announced; when the NTA issues the application ID, set `NTA_JAPAN_APP_ID` on Render.
