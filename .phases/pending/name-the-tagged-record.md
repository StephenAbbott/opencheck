title: A subject-record signal names the company it read, so an added FullCheck layer's crime tag no longer reads as the looked-up company's.

tests: backend +1

Found on NVIDIA's FullCheck (13 Nov 2026): after "Add layer", the network carried a `CRIME_RECORD` whose summary read "OpenSanctions tags its record of this company, which carries the company's LEI, as financial crime" — which reads as NVIDIA. The tag is on The Vanguard Group's own OpenSanctions record (NK-TJ2nJdhENfzugPiHGLfHdS, LEI 5493002789CX3L0CJP65): `/expand-layer` runs a full lookup on each owner and folds that lookup's subject-level signals into the network's list, where "this company" names nobody. The attribution itself was right (the evidence's `statement_id` is remapped onto Vanguard's node); only the words were wrong. `assess_subject_record` now names the company — GLEIF's legal name, else OpenSanctions' caption — with its LEI in all three summaries (`CRIME_RECORD`, `REGULATORY_ACTION`, `REGULATORY_WARNING`): "OpenSanctions tags its record of The Vanguard Group, Inc. (LEI 5493002789CX3L0CJP65), which carries that LEI, as financial crime." The same template problem sat in the nine subject-level OpenSanctions topic summaries ("OpenSanctions lists this record as sanctioned", debarment, counter-sanctions, sanction control and adjacency, the three export families, PEP), which reach the network the same way; they now read "its record of <caption>" (`risk._record_phrase`; "this record" only when the record has no name). Codes, confidence, evidence and `SIGNAL_RULES` are unchanged, so the watchlist and findings regression see no change; replayed runs and saved reports keep the words they were recorded with. After deploy: open NVIDIA's FullCheck, add a layer, and check the Crime record chip names The Vanguard Group; regenerate the curated narratives once, as their packets quote summaries.
