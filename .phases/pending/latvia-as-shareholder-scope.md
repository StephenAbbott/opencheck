Two defects the post-deploy tester found on 20 Nov 2026 after the Latvia withdrawal work. The disclosure status counted `ur_latvia` as a shareholder source for every company, but the lookup reads only the SIA members table, so an AS (Remars-Rīga, 40003197429) read "Shareholders: None on record" while its History tab said the register no longer shows AS shareholders. `disclosure._ur_latvia_hints` now gives an AS `restricted` shareholders with the register's reason (`LV_AS_SHAREHOLDERS`: the register stopped publishing a joint-stock company's shareholders on 19 November 2026, after a Constitutional Court judgment); an SIA is unchanged. And `fetch_timeline_data` put `stockholders` in `withheld` on any 403, so SIA UDEA's History tab told it joint-stock shareholders were hidden; the withdrawal is now said only when the register's own record types the company `AS` (`ur_latvia.is_joint_stock`), and for any other company the refusal is neither withheld nor unavailable. After deploy, check Remars-Rīga's QuickCheck reads "Shareholders: Not public" and SIA UDEA's History tab carries no withholding note.
