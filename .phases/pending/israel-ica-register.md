`ica_israel` dispatches on GLEIF's RA000406 (`il_roc`, the bare 9-digit corporate number; all 1,378 ISSUED records file it that way, 1,372 resolve, the six misses are associations and cooperatives on other registers). `scripts/build_ica_israel_index.py` pages the CKAN `datastore_search` API 32,000 rows at a time — the CSV download answers 403 to datacentre addresses — checks the field set and that rows read equal the API's total, and writes one `entity` table (733,463 companies + 29,301 partnerships, ~180 MB, ~45 MB gzipped); `refresh-ica-israel-index.yml` rebuilds it daily and ships the `ica-israel-index` asset, downloaded at boot. Stephen's decisions (10 Nov 2026): companies and partnerships both in v1; an index tier, not live reads; the care-of field `אצל` is never read (about 230,000 rows hold what look like people's names); the violating-company mark `מפרה` is a finding sentence and a `commenting` annotation, never a signal; no name gate (1,194 of 1,230 company LEIs agree on a name, the rest are renames). `~` in every text field is the Hebrew quotation mark and is written back as `"`. Entity data only: the open data carries no officers, shareholders or partners. The register publishes no dissolution date, so a terminal status is carried without one. The last annual report is said for private companies only (a public company reports to the Securities Authority; Bezeq's register year is 2002). A foreign company gets no jurisdiction (its home country is not published). Hebrew country names joined `bods/countries.ALIASES`. History and the watchlist (the one-year `ica-changes` log) are a separate follow-up ticket.
