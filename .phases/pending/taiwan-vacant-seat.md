title: A seat Taiwan's register files as vacant (缺額) is no longer mapped as a person.
readme: A board seat Taiwan's company register files as vacant is now noted on the company and counted in the finding instead of appearing as a director called "缺額".
tests: backend +20
---
The register of directors and supervisors writes 缺額 ("vacancy") in `Person_Name` when a seat is unfilled; `map_gcis_taiwan` made it a person statement, so the FullCheck hop to 鴻海精密工業 (04541302) drew a director called "缺額", the name reached the OpenSanctions, EveryPolitician, OpenAleph and ICIJ screens, and the finding counted it as a director (26 rows on 22 of the 503 ISSUED RA000551 companies, 4 Nov 2026). `tw_vacant_seat` is the one place that decides it: 缺額 with or without brackets (ASCII, full-width, 【】), plus 出缺 / 懸缺 / 從缺 / 空缺 in case the register uses them (the dataset's filter cannot search by name, so they could not be measured). A vacant seat on its own row draws no party and no relationship; the company statement carries one `commenting` annotation listing the vacant seats by position, and the finding says "N seats vacant" and no longer counts them as directors. On a legal person's row with no representative named, the seat stays the legal person's and its details say "the register names no representative"; no representative person is drawn. A vacancy carries no holding. Tested on the register's real answer for 鴻海 of 11 Nov 2026 (`hon_hai.json`: a director's and an independent director's seat vacant). After deploy: the 鴻海 hop shows no "缺額" person and its finding reads "7 directors, 2 seats vacant".
