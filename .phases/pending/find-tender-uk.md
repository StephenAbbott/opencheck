`sources/find_tender_uk.py`, `scripts/build_find_tender_index.py`, `.github/workflows/refresh-find-tender-index.yml`, `bods/mappers/find_tender.py`, `finding_find_tender_uk`, `_bh_find_tender_uk`, `lib/findTender.ts` and `FindTenderAwardsList` on the source card. Stephen's decisions A–F are on the Notion ticket. The Find a Tender API has no supplier filter, so the source is an index: the OCP Data Registry's compiled `full.jsonl.gz` (publication 41, OGL v3, refreshed weekly on Saturdays) is read in two passes and published as the `find-tender-index` release asset, downloaded at boot by `warm_index`; without it `covers_lei` is False and the source is never announced. Awarded contracts only (B): a cancelled or unsuccessful award is skipped, so a re-awarded contract names the new supplier. Pre-Act notices are included with a caveat (C): under the old regulations few notices named a supplier's number (13% GB-COH against 49% under the Procurement Act), and the finding says so when every award predates the Act. A PPON is joined to a Companies House number only where the file pairs them one to one (D; 10,317 of 26,520 PPON-only parties); a scheme-less CH-shaped number is ignored (F). `parties[].control` (connected persons with addresses and birth months) is never read (E), and no contact, address or person reaches the index. The 7 Nov 2026 file built in 42 s: 215,655 releases, 117,797 awards to 30,950 Companies House-numbered suppliers from 25,231 buyers, 21.6 MB (6.4 MB gzipped). Dispatched on a GB Companies House RA (585/586/587) and on a register-anchored `GB-COH` lookup; the hit asserts no identifier (the number is the lookup key), the BODS is one entity statement with `GB-COH` and no relationship, and nothing reaches a signal, the verdict or a screen. The card lists the latest five notices (one row per contract, lots counted) and the three largest sole-supplier awards; values are never summed, and a shared framework value says "shared by N suppliers". After merge, run `refresh-find-tender-index` once by hand. Contracts Finder (publication 128) is a follow-up.
