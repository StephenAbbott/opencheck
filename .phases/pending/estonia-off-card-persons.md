Phase 330 read four of RIK's daily open-data files and left `ettevotja_rekvisiidid__kandevalised_isikud.json.zip` — the persons the register knows about who are *not* on the registry card — for later. Read on 16 Oct 2026 it held 434,083 rows on 296,427 entities: founders (A 370,119, B 35,074), supervisory board members and chairs (N 10,691, E 2,252), **the shareholders of a public limited company the register itself records** (S 3,323 — 1,780 legal persons, 1,543 natural, every one with a percentage, 3,296 from Nasdaq CSD), audit firms and auditors (D 9,217, J 1,368, M 698), bankruptcy committee members, authorised persons, audit committees and congregations. The rows have the OÜ shareholder row's shape plus the role; no row carries an end date. So an AS lookup — Eesti Energia AS read "no shareholders on file, 4 on the registry card" — was missing its supervisory board and, where the register records one, its shareholder. `scripts/build_ariregister_index.py` streams the fifth file into an `off_card_person` table (index schema 2, and the adapter still reads a schema-1 index with an empty off-card list so a deploy before the next daily rebuild changes nothing; floor 300,000 rows; the workflow's sanity check reads the count), `entity_record` hands it over as `off_card_persons` (empty on the page path, which has no such list) and `map_ariregister` maps it by Stephen's decisions of 16 Oct 2026: N/E are `boardMember`/`boardChair` labelled "supervisory board (nõukogu)", serving-only like every officer; S is a `shareholding` by the OÜ row's rule — `_ee_shareholding_interest` is now the one builder for the three shareholder-shaped rows — with "Shareholder of a public limited company (aktsionär), as recorded by the register" leading its details, and the finding counts it as a shareholder; founders are ownership history, never a current share: a founder still on a current shareholding (hash, registry code or foreign key; an ended holding does not count) gets a `commenting` annotation on that holding naming the founding contribution and date, and one no longer holding gets a `shareholding` record with `startDate`, `recordStatus: closed` and no `endDate` because the register publishes none (the CAC rule) — closed, so ended to the risk engine and to integrated ownership, and a founding contribution never inflates a figure. On the real files 263,123 founders are current holders and about 133,000 are former; 9,138 foreign founders carry no hash and become closed records the name-keyed merge can later fold. Auditors and the other roles are no interest in the company and are not mapped; they stay in the raw bundle. The finding says "N on the supervisory board" only where there is one. Measured against the real file: every row passes the builder's value checks. After deploy: run the Refresh Estonian e-Business Register index workflow by hand so a schema-2 asset exists (until it does, Estonian cards read exactly as before), then check Eesti Energia's card — the finding gains the supervisory board — and run the curated-narrative regen checklist if its signals moved.
