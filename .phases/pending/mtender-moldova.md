`sources/mtender_moldova.py`, `scripts/build_mtender_index.py`, `.github/workflows/refresh-mtender-index.yml` (weekly, Tuesdays, release `mtender-index`), `bods/mappers/moldova.py::map_mtender_moldova`, `finding_mtender_moldova`, `_bh_mtender_moldova` and `MTenderAwardsList` on the QuickCheck card; a register lookup on `MD-IDNO` asks it too (`_register_procurement`). Measured on the registry's full file (publication 34, retrieved 21 Oct 2026): 125,774 compiled releases and no contracts — MTender publishes awards only, every award's `status` is `pending` and the winner is `statusDetails == "active"` (68,105), so the card says "awarded", never "contract". Awards on a cancelled tender or lot (332) and MTender's test procedures (by method or OCID, never title — "teste de laborator" is a real purchase) are skipped. The key is a legal person's IDNO (13 digits, 7-3-1 check, leading 1): natural persons' IDNPs and sole traders (Î.I., G.Ț., A.I., by name) are dropped at build time (Stephen, 12 Nov 2026), and the `contactPoint` with a named person, e-mail and phone on every party is never read. 7,442 suppliers, a 4 MB index, 1.4 MB gzipped. Three quarters of the awards are `directAward` — reported low-value purchases — kept and labelled by method (Stephen). **Coverage**: winning awards dated November 2022 to 3 March 2026, almost all from October 2025; every release the registry compiled since May 2026 is a planning record with no award, so the card states the period and our own MTender harvest is the follow-up (decision D). **Licence**: none is named by the registry or mtender.gov.md; recorded as `MTender-Open-Data`, amber (verify before commercial re-use), while the Ministry of Finance is asked (decision A). Awards are activity, never ownership: one entity statement with the IDNO under `MD-IDNO`, no relationship, no signal, no verdict clause. Each award links to `https://mtender.gov.md/tenders/<ocid>` (checked in a browser for an open tender and a direct award).
