Found by the weekly production check of 23 Nov 2026 on EIDE SEAFOOD AS and PEPSICO DE ARGENTINA SRL (Notion ticket "History tab shows GLEIF changes as raw codes, and dates a current consolidation as ended"). (1) `timeline/gleif.py::relationship_interest_dates` took the earliest start and latest end across every `RelationshipPeriod`, so Eide's ultimate parent, current since 30 Dec 2022, read "to 2023-12-31" — the end of the ACCOUNTING_PERIOD — and PepsiCo de Argentina's "1959-04-07 to 2025-12-31". The new `relationship_periods` rebuilds each period from the unindexed log (dates paired with the `PeriodType` that follows them in document order; an update finds its period by the value it replaces) and only the RELATIONSHIP_PERIOD dates the row; a log that cannot be paired gives no dates rather than a guess. RR modifications are grouped by relationship type and parent, so a direct and an ultimate consolidation by one parent keep their own periods. (2) GLEIF relationship rows read "Consolidating parent recorded / removed" and "Consolidated by its ultimate (direct) parent" instead of "Owner / parent added" and IS_ULTIMATELY_CONSOLIDATED_BY (`ChangeEvent.label`), and the parent is named from the Golden Copy mirror beside its LEI (`counterparty_name` on `/history`; a local read, never a GLEIF call). (3) An XML attribute (`@type`, `@xml:lang`) is never a change, and GLEIF's transliterated or other-language addresses are not address changes: Eide's two "Address changed" rows showing PREFERRED_ASCII_TRANSLITERATED_*_ADDRESS are gone, and a `LegalName/@xml:lang` update can no longer read as a name change. (4) Row dates, interest periods and board rows are in words ("30 Dec 2022"). Fixtures: both companies' GLEIF logs as served on 23 Nov 2026. After deploy, check the History tab for both LEIs.
