---
title: The homepage's sections share one type scale, one eyebrow, one card padding and one icon stroke.
readme: The homepage's curated examples, new features and How it works sections now share one type scale, eyebrow, card padding and icon stroke, and the page title is the largest heading on phones.
---
A design-system audit of opencheck.world (17 Nov 2026, after the curated-examples and How it works redesigns) measured the live page at 1280px and 390px and found five drifts, all fixed here with existing tokens. The heading order: the How it works panel heading was 26px on desktop (the size of the page's H1) and 22px on phones, above the hero H1's arbitrary 20px; the hero is now `oo-title` on phones and `oo-display` on desktop, the panel heading `oo-stat` / `oo-title`, and the "How it works" eyebrow a `p` so the section has one `h2`. The eyebrows: `HomePanels`' local `SectionLabel` rendered Bitter 11px semibold while `ui/SectionLabel` and the feature cards' "New · …" eyebrows are DM Sans 12px bold; it now uses `sectionLabelClasses` with the hairline rule, which takes HomePanels to zero arbitrary `text-[NNpx]` in the design lint. Body copy: card descriptions are `oo-small` (13px) in all three sections, and the hero subline `oo-body` instead of Tailwind's `text-sm`. Spacing: every top-level card is padded 16px on phones and 20px from `sm`, cards are 20px apart, and card titles keep the type scale's own line height. Corners and icons: the search inputs and submit buttons use `rounded-oo` and `buttonClasses("primary")`, the search tabs' icons are all 16px at stroke 1.75 (the Company name tab's GLEIF search-bar-and-cursor mark became the `ui/Icon` search glyph), and the How it works step icons are drawn at stroke 1.75 like `ui/Icon`. The New features cards keep their shadow on purpose (Phase 344). Before/after screenshots are in the project's homepage-design-audit folder.
