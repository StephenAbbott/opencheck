`guernsey_registry` reads the Guernsey Registry's public search API (`portal.guernseyregistry.com/api`, keyless, undocumented, 60 requests a minute from its own `X-Rate-Limit-*` headers) on the number GLEIF files under the registry's five RA codes — `RA000383` companies, `RA000667` LPs, `RA000668` LLPs, `RA000666` foundations, `RA001134` charities and NPOs — and under `RA000777` (Alderney) where a Guernsey number is misfiled there; `RA000886` (GFSC licence numbers) and `RA999999` are not dispatched (Stephen, 30 Oct 2026). 4,498 ISSUED LEIs have jurisdiction GG. GLEIF files bare digits and the registry numbers each register separately (`CMP2820` is a wound-up company, `LP2820` a live partnership), and the RA code is often wrong (half of `RA000668`'s records are companies), so the prefix is chosen from the ELF code, then the RA code, then every register, and each candidate is accepted only when the registry's current or former name agrees with GLEIF's — a wrong prefix returns another company (`CMP5309` Olympus Sportswear, `LP5309` Hedosophia). A sample of 190 records resolved in full on the registry's five codes. A new generic hook, `lookup_pass_anchor`, hands an adapter the anchor's RA and ELF codes; the derived key stays GLEIF's number as filed. **Only corporate officials are named** (Stephen): the registry sells its officials list as a "Statement of the Register", so an individual is kept as a role and an appointment date — name, honorific and the registry's id for the person are dropped in the adapter before cache or bundle, the schema refuses one, and the BODS carries a `commenting` annotation counting individuals by role. A corporate official registered in Guernsey is an entity statement with its own `GG-RCE` number (a FullCheck hop); one registered elsewhere (the registry answers "not publicly available") is named with no jurisdiction or identifier (Phase 295). Directors are `seniorManagingOfficial`, councillors `boardMember`, general partners and LLP members `otherInfluenceOrControl`, resident agents and secretaries officer-marked (Phase 340); at most 10 official reads per record. Alderney registrations (`NDC3`, `C/…`) are a note card, as are a number naming another entity and bare digits with no name. GLEIF's statement writes `GG-RCE` with a prefix only where the RA and ELF codes agree, otherwise the RA code (decision 4). **The pull request is held until the Guernsey Registry answers about reuse terms** (none are published; licence `Guernsey-Registry-Terms-Unstated`, amber). After deploy: Sirius Real Estate (`213800NURUF5W8QSK566`) should show a Guernsey Registry card with eight directors counted and unnamed and a former name, and Davos Investments a corporate director with its own number.
