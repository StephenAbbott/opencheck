readme: A refused GLEIF anchor keeps its cross-reference identifiers from the cache or says it lost them, the anchor's other calls stop at the first refusal, and exports reuse the lookup the reader just ran.

From the "GLEIF rate limiter refusing a third of stream/export calls" ticket, whose investigation (15 Oct 2026) found that a refusal is never sent and never reaches the reader as a failure: one refused call sends the whole anchor to the stale cache or the Golden Copy snapshot, which carries parents, exceptions and children intact. What it does not carry are the identifiers GLEIF inlines from its mapping files — `ocid`, `spglobal`, `bic`, `mic`, `qcc` — and OpenCorporates is dispatched only on `ocid`, so under load a lookup silently lost OpenCorporates: not in `sources_applicable`, no degradation, no notice. The fallback now copies those ids from the cached Level 1 record at any age (it is often answered in the same gather before a sibling is refused; `_merge_crossrefs` is shared with the Phase 179 mirror top-up), and when there is none the bundle says `crossrefs_available: false` with a reason and the pipeline records a `gleif` `source_read` degradation ("could not supply its cross-reference identifiers … so OpenCorporates was not consulted"), so the verdict reads "one source answered only in part". The same record is made when the mirror's top-up cannot run, ready for mirror-first, and when the curated-bundle path's live identifier call fails, which until now only logged. The anchor's four calls are all-or-nothing, so `_gather_cancelling` cancels the rest at the first failure instead of leaving them to queue for, and spend, budget whose answers were discarded. Separately, `/export` defaulted to `deepen_top=3` while `/lookup-stream` defaults to 5 and the replay cache is keyed `LEI:deepen_top`, so every download after a QuickCheck ran a second full pipeline (a gate slot, the lookup budget, every source again) and exported a shallower graph than the report on screen; an LEI export and `opencheck_export_bods` now default to 5, a `q` export keeps 3, and the API page's `/lookup` default is corrected to 5. To check in production: `/signalstats` `degraded` shows `gleif|source_read|rate_limited` only when a refusal met an uncached record, and `pipelines.by_caller.export.started` falls well below `stream` for exports made from the report.
