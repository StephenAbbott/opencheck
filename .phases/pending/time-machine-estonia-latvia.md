readme: The History tab now reads the Latvian Register of Enterprises' dated open data (additions only, since the register publishes no departures) and puts Estonia's board members, auditors and re-registered holdings on the right tiers.

The "More Time Machine emitters: Estonia and Latvia historic entries" ticket, from the Open Ownership usable-data check (feature 22, historical information). Scoping found the ticket's premise half wrong: the timeline had six emitters, not three, and Estonia has fed it since the registry-card phases — production shows Eesti Energia AS with 383 Tier-1 events back to 1998 — while UR's open data has no end date on any people table (measured 3 Nov 2026: beneficial owners, SIA members, AS stockholders and officers are current rows only, edited in place, a removed party simply leaving the next day's file). Stephen's decisions: re-tier Estonia here; build the Latvian emitter in the New Zealand shape, additions only; the tiers as proposed. Estonia (`timeline/ariregister.py`): person rows are tiered by role code through the mapper's own tables — shareholders (`OSAN`, `S`), founders and beneficial owners stay Tier-1 owner events; board members, procurists, liquidators, trustees, representatives and the supervisory board (`N`, `E`) become Tier-4 `OFFICER_APPOINTED` / `OFFICER_RESIGNED` on the board stream; auditors, the audit committee, the share registrar and the contact person are Tier-3 rows. Until now every one of them was a Tier-1 `OWNER_ADDED`. A row ending the day the next row of the same party and role begins is one re-registration, matched regardless of case: `CONTROL_BAND_CHANGED` naming both sizes where the holding changed (the Ministry of Economic Affairs' stake in Eesti Energia, re-registered eight times 2005–2013), an administrative row where it did not (a Nasdaq CSD account moved from a simple to an owner account), `OFFICER_DETAILS_CHANGED` for an office renewed — never a remove + add pair; and the Nasdaq account prefix ("Lihtkonto:", "Omanikukonto:") is stripped from the holder's name as the mapper strips it from the statement. Latvia (`timeline/ur_latvia.py`, `UrLatviaAdapter.fetch_timeline_data`): the lookup's cached tables plus six dated event tables read on the `/history` path only (AS stockholders, liquidations, reorganisations, insolvency proceedings, suspensions, securing measures; all CC0, no key, no index, nothing on the persistent disk). Current SIA members and AS stockholders are `OWNER_ADDED` at `date_from` (effective), beneficial owners at `registered_on` (recorded), officers `OFFICER_APPOINTED` at `registered_on` (recorded); termination, former names (each to the next by `date_to`), liquidations with their grounds, reorganisations naming the other company on either side, insolvency opened and ended with the resolution, and suspensions are Tier 2; securing measures and a depository's dematerialised-shares row Tier 3; the registration itself administrative, as a Companies House incorporation filing is. No removal is ever emitted, and the tab says so: `REGISTER_READ_NOTES.ur_latvia` states that a shareholder, beneficial owner or officer who has left does not appear because the register does not publish it, and `basisLabel` reads a `recorded` row with no GLEIF source as "as entered by the register" rather than crediting GLEIF. `HISTORY_SOURCES` gains `ur_latvia` (the parity test pins the three frontend tables), `registry_numbers` carries the Latvian regcode from GLEIF's `RA000423`, the service's registration tuple grows to seven. Two defects found on the way: data.gov.lv has dropped `datastore_search_sql`, so `ur_latvia`'s name search falls back silently to a stub (ticketed separately); and the CKAN datastore's copy of the historical-names table stopped being reloaded on 24 March 2025 (90,974 rows against 94,241 in the daily raw file), so both the lookup and the timeline now read the names from `dati.ur.gov.lv/register/register_name_history.csv`, cached a day, with the CKAN copy as the fallback. `docs/time-machine.md` gains the Latvian section and the re-tiering. Production check: Eesti Energia AS's History tab shows the ministry's stake as band changes, the supervisory board on the board stream and the audit firm in the administrative stream; a Latvian LEI (`RA000423`) shows a Register of Enterprises source with the "current records only" note.
