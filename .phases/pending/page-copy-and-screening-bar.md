title: One description of OpenCheck across the main pages, and the screening-incomplete bar moved into the verdict card.
readme: The main pages now describe OpenCheck the same way — who owns and controls a company, from open data — and the screening-incomplete bar sits at the foot of the verdict card instead of between the mode tabs and their panel.
---
A UX copy review of the homepage, /sources, /api, /features, /changelog, /watchlist and /about found six different descriptions of what OpenCheck is ("customer due diligence risk checks", "one lookup", "a proof-of-concept"…), none of which said what a reader gets. One description now leads everywhere: OpenCheck is a free, open-source tool that shows who owns and controls a company, and the risk signals around it, by bringing together open registers and datasets through its Legal Entity Identifier (LEI). Stephen's decisions (5 Nov 2026): "proof-of-concept" becomes "an open-source demonstration of"; "starting from a single ID" stays in the headline for simplicity; "company", never "legal entity"; acronyms spelled out at first use on each page; British spelling. Changed: the meta / Open Graph / Twitter description and title, the FastAPI description, the hero headline ("See who owns and controls 3.4 million companies, starting from a single ID") and a shorter subline, How it works step 1 (3.4 million companies, matching the headline), the footer tagline, and the lead paragraph of /sources (LEI spelled out, "licence"), /api, /features (`ledeSentence`), /changelog ("What's new in OpenCheck"), /watchlist and /about. One name per page: /sources is "Sources" in the title, the tab and the heading (was "Data Sources", "The sources OpenCheck queries", "About the sources"); /about is "About OpenCheck" (was "Behind the Scenes"). The MIT licence line is spelled the British way. The "Screening incomplete" bar (`DegradedScreensNotice`) and "Part of this report could not be loaded" (`PanelErrorsNotice`) gained an `attached` form and render as the bottom strip of the subject-and-verdict card, directly under the verdict's "see which" link, instead of as boxes between the mode tabs and the panel — which cut the active tab off from its content (the Ørsted FullCheck screenshot). On a phone the strip rides at the foot of the verdict card. Checked on a saved report of Ørsted at 1280px and 390px, collapsed and expanded. Not changed: the MCP server instructions and the entity pages' header line.
