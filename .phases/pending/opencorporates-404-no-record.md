Found by the weekly source-health check of 20 Oct 2026 reproducing `/lookup?lei=529900IQDQ6X3VTMWN63` ("Kaufland" S.R.L., Moldova): `errors.opencorporates` held `HTTPStatusError: HTTP 404 Not Found` and a `source_fetch` degradation said OpenCorporates "did not answer, so its records were not consulted". `OpenCorporatesAdapter._get` raised on every non-2xx, so the one answer that means "nothing here" reached the reader as an outage — a false degradation, the inverse of a silent green — while `_get_optional` beside it already treated 404 as definitive. `_get` now caches a 404 as absent (`put_absent`, the seven-day absence TTL) and returns `None`; `fetch` turns that into a `not_found` bundle with `COVERAGE_404` and skips the network call; `_build_result_hit` builds no hit from it, so the source is counted in coverage as answered with no record and nothing enters `errors` or `degraded_sources`. 402/403 (a tier the key lacks), 429, 5xx and network errors still raise and still degrade. The offline guard reads `get_payload` rather than `has`, so an expired cached absence with live calls off is a stub rather than an assertion. The ticket's open question answered itself: GLEIF's OpenCorporates mapping gives that LEI the ocid `md/1.0166E+12` — IDNO 1016600004811 cut to five significant figures by a spreadsheet — so the 404 was guaranteed. `repair_ocid` (in `sources/opencorporates.py`, called by `_resolve_ctx`) replaces a scientific-notation company number with the subject's own GLEIF `registeredAs` only when that is all digits and rounds to exactly the mangled value; otherwise the ocid is dropped and OpenCorporates is not asked; ordinary ocids pass through untouched. Both outcomes are logged. Check in production: the Kaufland lookup shows no OpenCorporates error, and either an OpenCorporates card for md/1016600004811 or OpenCorporates under "Answered with no record". How many other GLEIF ocids carry the scientific-notation shape was not measured.
