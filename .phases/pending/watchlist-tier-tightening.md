From the Notion ticket "Daily deltas for OpenCheck" (Stephen, 13 Nov 2026). A week of GLEIF's daily deltas (6–13 Nov 2026) showed that about half of everything the watchlist's materiality table graded "recheck" was two kinds of record-keeping: around 1,500 reporting-exception reasons moving a day (NON_CONSOLIDATING to NATURAL_PERSONS and the like) and issuers re-keying register numbers in bulk (1,700 records on 13 Nov). `GLEIF_FIELD_TIER` in `opencheck/watchlist_materiality.py` now grades `registered_at`, `registered_as`, `direct_exception` and `ultimate_exception` as note; `MATERIALITY_RULES` is 2 and `MATERIALITY_RULES_CHANGED` says what moved. A parent that appears or goes is still escalated by the parent fields, and an ending is still escalated whether or not the LEI had lapsed. The four fields still trigger a re-run (`GLEIF_MATERIAL_FIELDS` is unchanged), so a re-run that finds a signal is graded by the signal. An entry graded under rules 1 keeps its tier. `docs/watchlist.md` states the new rows. To check in production: a new watchlist entry carries `materiality_rules: 2`.
