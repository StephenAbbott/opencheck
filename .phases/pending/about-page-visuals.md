Stephen chose option A of three mockups (20 Nov 2026, the "Make the About page more visual" thread; mockups in the project's about-visual-mockups folder). "How a lookup works" moves out of the card grid into a full-width panel: a diagram of the LEI resolved through GLEIF, the bridge IDs, a selection of sources with their flags, BODS and the risk signals, then the same five steps as a numbered list, which is all a phone shows (the diagram is hidden under sm). The four checks each carry their mode badge (the mode glyph on the brand-mark navy, ringed in the mode accent). The BODS spine's statement types are drawn with the BOVS icons the graph uses, followed by a small person, company and subsidiary graph; the coloured monospace chips went, which removed the file's nine raw palette classes from the design-lint baseline. The LEI card shows the four parts of Eesti Energia AS's LEI, and the GODIN card a network joined through one LEI. The pictures live in components/about/AboutVisuals.tsx and use token classes on the SVG, never hex. No third-party logo is used: GLEIF's logo needs a licence its IP policy grants to LEI issuers, agents and partners, Neo4j's needs written permission, and Open Ownership's terms do not cover theirs (checked 20 Nov 2026); a test fails if an image on the page is anything but a BOVS data URI or a flag served by the site.
