---
title: A slow OpenAleph name search no longer turns the whole source into "did not answer".
readme: OpenAleph's free-text name fallback now reports a timeout or gateway error as a partial answer instead of failing the whole source.
tests: backend +5
---
777 PARTNERS LLC (213800KSO6IZRZPW3K52) showed OpenAleph as "did not answer" on every try (23 Nov 2026). The LEI, registration, FtM match and percolation steps all answered with nothing, and the cascade fell through to the free-text q= search. DARC.ZONE answers some free-text queries, mostly a number followed by words ("777 PARTNERS LLC", "777 Holdings", "321 Holdings LLC"), with `503 backend unavailable` after about 30 s. Other names answer in about a second. Our 15 s read timeout fired first, and `_get_tolerant` caught only status errors, so the `ReadTimeout` escaped and discarded the steps that had answered. The lookup's name fallback (`fetch_by_name`, via `_get_tolerant(degrade_unanswered=True)`) now treats a timeout, a network error or a 502/503/504 as no results and records a `source_read` degradation (`NAME_SEARCH_UNANSWERED_DETAIL`). The card therefore reads "answered with no record" and the verdict says one source answered only in part, never clean. The parser's 500 on an awkward name stays a quiet empty answer, and `/search` still raises on a timeout. The upstream slowness is DARC.ZONE's to fix. After deploy, retry OpenAleph on 777 PARTNERS LLC: it should answer, with the partial-answer note.
