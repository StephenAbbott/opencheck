---
title: A related-party person match is refused when each name carries a given name the other lacks.
readme: The related-party screens no longer match a person to a record that shares their other names but not their given name.
tests: backend +26
---
Found on АКВАХИМ (BG-EIK 200984964) after the Bulgarian Commercial Register went live: the director "Елица Петрова Рускова" raised a medium RELATED_PEP on OpenSanctions' "НЕЛИ РУСКОВА ПЕТРОВА" at 0.905, because the token-sort score ignores order and both names hold Петрова and Рускова. In Bulgarian order they are two people with different given names and fathers; a Bulgarian patronymic (-ова) is shaped like a surname, so the patronymic gate could not see it, and Bulgarian register persons carry no birth year. `names.person_names_conflict` is order-free: two names conflict when each holds a name token with no near partner in the other (rigour's edit budget, the Ukrainian/Russian father fold, a 0.75 whole-token ratio, or a prefix such as Alex / Alexander). A subset ("Anna Ivanova" beside "Anna Petrova Ivanova") never conflicts, a single-token name never conflicts, and one record name that does not conflict keeps the match. `cross_check._given_names_compatible` applies it to person targets on the OpenSanctions, EveryPolitician and OpenAleph screens beside the birth-year and patronymic gates; an identifier match or an agreeing birth year skips it. Every agreeing pair of the patronymic gate's tests still matches. After deploy: АКВАХИМ's lookup should carry no RELATED_PEP for Елица Петрова Рускова, and Борислав Любенов Великов's high-confidence match should stand.
