---
title: The table of what each country's registers publish about owners grows from 75 to 184 jurisdictions.
readme: The beneficial ownership access table now covers 184 jurisdictions, adding Africa, Asia and Oceania from FATF, regional-body and OECD assessment reports.
---
`backend/opencheck/data/jurisdictions.json` and `docs/knowability.md` are re-synced from Stephen's Notion database "Beneficial ownership access status" after the first rounds of the worldwide extension (Notion ticket "Update using FATF, UNODC, World Bank and OECD reports", 26 Nov 2026): 109 rows are new (Africa 52, Asia 43, Oceania 14), so every African state and the Asia-Pacific members of the APG now have a row, 184 in all (Africa 55, Asia 51, Europe 38, Americas 24, Oceania 16), 68 verified. The new rows were filled from FATF, ESAAMLG, GIABA, GABAC, MENAFATF, APG and OECD Global Forum reports through 159 proposals Stephen accepted one by one. The citation rule holds: an intergovernmental report fills the register's name, holder, legal basis, threshold and pending changes, and never the access status, which changes only on a primary source. So most new rows carry no access status yet and their sentence says who may look up beneficial owners "is not yet recorded here", instead of the stated-absence sentence a country with no row gets. No sync column was added: the Notion columns added for this work (region, legal basis, register holder, status evidence, FATF-style body membership) are not read by `sync_jurisdictions.py` yet. The table was read through the Notion connector's view mode and passed to the script as `--rows-json`, with the connector's markup (links, mentions, line breaks, escapes) removed first; for the 75 existing rows the only differences are India's and Peru's threshold wording, Peru's assessment body, and the stray word "Untitled" gone from the source notes. Two tests used Zambia as the country with no row; they now use Antarctica. To check in production: an entity page or QuickCheck for a Kenyan or Vietnamese company shows the "not yet recorded here" sentence, and `opencheck_knowability(["KE"])` answers with a row.
