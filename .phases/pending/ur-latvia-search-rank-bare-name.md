Follow-up to the ur_latvia name-search fix (Phase 462), from its post-deploy check: production `/search?q=Latvenergo` on 6 Nov 2026 listed AS "Latvenergo" (40003032949) tenth of 33, because the cross-source ranker read only the hit's full name 'Akciju sabiedrība "Latvenergo"' — `same_name`, while the OpenSanctions, OpenAleph and Wikidata "Latvenergo" records were `exact`. `names` strips the legal forms `rigour` knows; Latvian forms written out in full are not among them. `search_rank.bare_names_of_hit` now reads the register's own bare name (`BARE_NAME_FIELDS`: `ur_latvia` → `name_in_quotes`, tidied by `clean_bare_name` of the stray quotes and comma UR leaves on a few), and `best_match` and the similarity tie-break take the better of the full and the bare name. The bare name is the current name, so a match on it is never reported as `matched_former_name` in `opencheck_search`; the candidate's `name` stays the full name. Only a source listed in `BARE_NAME_FIELDS` is read, and a bare name equal to the full name is ignored. The adapter's own page ranking now uses the same `clean_bare_name`. Replaying the 6 Nov production answer, the register's hit moves from tenth to sixth, inside the exact tier and above GLEIF's same-name record (within a tier, ties keep source arrival order). Production check: `/search?q=Latvenergo` shows `ur_latvia` 40003032949 among the exact matches, and `opencheck_search("Latvenergo")` gives it `match: exact` with no `matched_former_name`.
