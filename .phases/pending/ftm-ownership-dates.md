title: OpenSanctions shareholdings keep their start and end dates, so an ownership that ended is drawn as ended.
readme: OpenSanctions shareholdings now carry the start and end dates the source publishes, so ended holdings close instead of reading as current.
tests: backend +7
---
`bods/mappers/ftm.py::_ftm_edge_interest` returned an `Ownership` edge's interest before the step that copies `startDate` / `endDate`, so every FollowTheMoney shareholding — OpenSanctions and OpenAleph — reached BODS undated and therefore current, while directorships, memberships and representations kept their dates. On Rosneft's production OpenSanctions bundle (11 Nov 2026) 71 ownership edges carry a start date and 55 an end date (Russian EGRUL holdings), all dropped. The ownership branch now falls through to the shared date step, and `make_relationship_statement` closes a relationship whose interests all ended (the Phase 317 rule), so the graph draws those holdings faint and integrated ownership lists them as formerly. Dates go through `_ftm_interest_day`: a full ISO day is kept (a datetime is cut to its day, a future end date stays), a partial date ("2019", "2019-05") is left out rather than rounded, since BODS asks for rounding to be annotated and the edge builder has no statement to annotate — none were seen on Rosneft. Found on the "How to deal with data sources where relationships have no start date or end date" ticket (the Rosneft / BP Russian Investments case); the rest of that ticket — folding OpenSanctions' republished Global Energy Ownership Tracker edges into GEM's own read and labelling undated relationships — is a separate change. After deploy, check Rosneft's FullCheck: EGRUL holdings with an end date should draw as ended, and the curated card's signals should be unchanged.
