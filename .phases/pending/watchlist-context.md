Part B of the "Watchlist as pKYC: materiality, context and review record" ticket (Notion) — Moody's "reuse onboarding context". The watchlist re-ran a check and said what moved, but nothing on the list said why the LEI was there or what the watcher believed about it on the day. New module `opencheck/watchlist_context.py` and migration 4 add four optional fields to a watch, set by whoever holds the list token through `PATCH /watch/{token}/items/{lei}` or on the add call: `expected_jurisdiction` (an ISO 3166 code), `expected_parent_lei` (the LEI expected as GLEIF's ultimate parent, by shape and check digits, not resolved), `rationale` (one line, at most 280 characters, refused when it carries an e-mail address, a link other than an OpenCheck saved-report link, or a run of eleven or more digits — the shape of a personal code) and `onboarding_report_id` (a saved report that must exist on this instance, be unexpired and be of the same LEI; only the id is stored). In `_write_rerun` each change is compared with the row's context and the result kept on the change as `expectation: {field, expected, met}`: GLEIF's ultimate parent always, the direct parent only when the expected LEI is on either side, jurisdiction from either diff with a country met by its subdivisions. Stephen's decisions (17 Oct 2026): two expectations and no more; setting context writes no entry, and a baseline that already disagrees is said on the watch row at once, computed on read; a contradicted expectation leaves the materiality tier alone and is said beside the chip; a met one is said too ("as your watch expected"); the entry is the record of the day and is never rewritten when the context changes. Shown on `/watch/{token}` (`context` on the watch with the expected parent's name resolved from the GLEIF mirror on read, the pinned report's date and `baseline_disagrees`; `expectation` on the change), in the Atom feed as a clause folded into the change's sentence and a `<category scheme="https://opencheck.world/watchlist/expectation" term="contradicted">` beside the tier and materiality categories (the title is unchanged), on `/watchlist` as a context block under each watch with an edit form that says "about the company, not a person" and the sentence in the log in the warn or ok tone, and on `/watchstats` as `watches_with_context` and `expectations_contradicted`, never the text. The report page's "Watch for changes" pins the saved report on the page — opened, or just saved from this run — as the onboarding record. No names stored, no accounts, no per-list materiality, no MCP tool. `docs/watchlist.md` gains "What the watcher expected", pinned to the module with the frontend's `EXPECTATION_WORDS` by `test_watchlist_context.py`.
