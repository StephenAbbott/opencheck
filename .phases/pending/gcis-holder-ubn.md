---
title: Taiwan's legal-person directors now carry their own company number, so FullCheck can hop up a Taiwanese chain.
readme: A Taiwanese company's corporate directors are now linked to their own register record by exact name, so FullCheck can follow a Taiwanese ownership chain upwards.
tests: backend +35
---
The GCIS register of directors and supervisors names a legal person holding a seat by name only, so until now the `gcis_taiwan` mapper wrote corporate parents (TSMC in its subsidiaries, 鴻海精密工業 in 亞灣超算) as entities with no identifier and FullCheck stopped there. The adapter now looks each domestic legal-person holder up in the register's keyword search (公司登記關鍵字查詢) and, when exactly one live company carries the exact name, the holder's statement gets that company's `TW-CR` number, the Taiwan jurisdiction and a `transformation` annotation saying the identifier was derived by OpenCheck from a name match and is not published on the directors register. Its `recordId` is unchanged. The keyword API has no exact operator (`like` and `eq` both do a token search), needs a status filter, folds width and case but not 台/臺, breaks on a space and cuts answers at `$top`, so the exact match is OpenCheck's own (`fold_name`). Measured on 4 Nov 2026 over the 503 ISSUED RA000551 LEIs: 563 distinct legal-person holders, 365 resolve to exactly one live company (67% of legal-person seats, on 237 companies; 74 of the parents hold an ISSUED LEI themselves), 0 to several, 42 to none, 156 foreign-prefixed. Stephen's decisions on the Notion ticket: several exact matches are refused silently; a search that did not answer, an answer cut at `$top`, the 15-holder cap or the 12-second phase budget is one `source_read` degradation, so the Taiwan card reads "answered in part"; live companies only; no foreign-prefixed name is looked up; the finding sentence is unchanged. Only names ending 公司 with no whitespace are sent, so ministries, government funds and foundations are never matched or classified by name (Phase 240). Resolutions are cached per name for seven days, a definitive none as a cached absence, and a failure never. The foreign-prefix table also gains 荷商, 英屬安吉拉商 and 英屬蓋曼群島商, spellings the register files that v1 left without a jurisdiction. After deploy, check that FullCheck on 亞灣超算股份有限公司 (984500DDK0015839A106) offers a hop to 鴻海精密工業股份有限公司 (04541302), and on 創意電子股份有限公司 (984500B2D7B5DC1C8985) to 台灣積體電路製造股份有限公司 (22099131).
