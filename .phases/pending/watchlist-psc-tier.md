pKYC stage E, Route 1 (Notion "pKYC stage E, Route 1: bods-stream as a `psc` push tier for GB-COH-anchored watches"; scoping answered 18 Oct 2026). Moody's names a change of owner or controller as the first example of a monitoring trigger, yet a UK PSC filing reached the watchlist only as a side-effect of some other delta naming the company. The PSC graph has been live-fed from Companies House's stream since Phases 184–189; this phase is the hook. `psc_stream.apply_events` now returns what each event did (`Touched`: company number, PSC item id, outcome added / changed / ceased / deleted, kind person / corporate entity / legal person / details withheld / PSC statement — `psc_kind_of` reads `data.kind`, else the resource path, since a deleted event carries no data), and `_flush` hands the committed batch to `watchlist_psc.on_psc_events` on a thread, the `mirror_refresh.apply_delta → on_gleif_delta` shape: never raises, the stream never waits on it. The hook maps company numbers to watched LEIs through GLEIF's `registeredAs` under RA000585 / RA000586 / RA000587 (the mirror facts; else the baseline's `GB-COH` identifier; `normalise_ch_company_number` on both sides, so a filer's `2999029` meets the stream's `02999029`) — an index rebuilt when the watches' count or newest add / baseline moved, or after a minute, and otherwise one set lookup per company number — and queues one re-run per watched LEI on the new `psc` tier. The trigger carries counts and keys only: `events`, `kinds` by outcome, the PSC item ids (the key the graph draws the party on since the item-id fix, so Route 2 can say which row moved), the publication span — never a name, a date of birth or an address (Stephen, Q2: count, don't name). `WatchlistStore.enqueue` gains `fold`: a second filing before the re-run ran is folded into the pending `psc` row with its counts added and ids united, so a company that files three times in an afternoon is re-run once; a pending catch-up still gives way to it. `_write_rerun` writes the entry whether or not the snapshot moved, as the register tier does — the filing is the news — and the entry reads "Companies House recorded 2 changes to this company's people with significant control on 2026-10-19 (company number 02999029)", then "2 persons: 1 added, 1 ceased." (`psc_sentence`, `psc_change_lines`; the feed, the page and the Atom entry share the words, `test_watchlist_psc.py` parses the TypeScript tables). `watchlist_materiality.psc_trigger_tier` grades the trigger **recheck** whatever it counted (Q4: a counts-only trigger cannot say which band moved; the ≥25 % escalate rule waits for Route 2's statement-level diff) — a new row, no existing row moved, so `MATERIALITY_RULES` stays 1. When the consumer resumes live (a long drop, a 416), `on_psc_gap` records the gap (`meta.psc_gaps`) and queues a catch-up re-run of every watched UK company (`reason: register_gap`, `register: companies_house`), once per gap, an entry only where the re-run finds a difference. Every watch row now says what follows its register (Q5, the knowability voice): `register_tier` — "Register changes: followed (Companies House PSC stream, live)" / "(e-Business Register open data, daily)", else "not followed — GLEIF files no register number for this company" / "— OpenCheck follows no change feed for this company's register" — a statement about OpenCheck's feeds, never about the company. The tiers block gains `psc` (stream on, connected, last publication, gap, matched, queued, recent gaps) and the page's honesty line names the stream; `/watchstats` adds `watched_by_registration_authority` (RA codes and counts — the measurement the scoping page could not take) and the hook's counters (`psc_events_seen`, `psc_matched`, `psc_queued`, `psc_coalesced`, `psc_gaps`). `docs/watchlist.md` gains Tier 4 and the trigger's grading. Measured before building: ~15–17k PSC events a day across ~5.5m UK companies, ~4 % of which hold an LEI, so a watched UK company expects about one PSC-triggered re-run a year. The homepage and /features copy were widened to "a followed register" in the phase before this one, so neither names Estonia alone. Not done, on purpose: no statement-level diff (Route 2), no other register (the stream is the only push feed OpenCheck consumes; Estonia keeps its daily diff; nothing is polled), no trigger that names a person.
