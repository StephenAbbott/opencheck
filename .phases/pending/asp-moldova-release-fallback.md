---
title: Moldova's State Register keeps its index across restarts and falls back to a release asset while dataset.gov.md is down.
readme: The Moldova State Register source now keeps its index on the persistent disk and reads a published copy when the national open-data portal is down.
tests: backend +5
---
`asp_moldova` had answered "the weekly export could not be downloaded or built" on every lookup since late September 2026: dataset.gov.md serves its own "Resursa Inexistentă" 404 on every path, the CKAN API included (re-checked 13 Nov 2026), and production kept the index in the temp directory, so the first restart after the outage lost it with nothing to rebuild it from. date.gov.md, the government's new landing portal, is not a replacement — it links back to dataset.gov.md for open data, and its company search is a reCAPTCHA-gated form. Two changes. `render.yaml` sets `ASP_MOLDOVA_DB_FILE=/var/data/asp_moldova.sqlite`, so a built index survives deploys. And `sync_index` gains a fallback, `_from_release`: when the portal cannot be read it takes the `asp-moldova-index` release asset (`ASP_MOLDOVA_DB_URL`), staged beside the index and used only when it carries the current schema, a later snapshot than the index in place and at least half its companies; a `HEAD` against the stamp first, so a portal that stays down costs one request per check. A new outcome, `released`, says the index came from the asset and still carries the portal's error. The portal stays first: an asset never replaces a same-week or later portal build. `refresh-asp-moldova-index.yml` (Tuesdays, plus by hand) builds from the portal and publishes the asset; while the portal is down it warns and publishes nothing, and a hand run with `xlsx_url` publishes a held export. After merge, until the portal returns or someone publishes a held export, production still has no Moldova index: the source stays degraded, now with the reason said.
