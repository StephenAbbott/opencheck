---
title: Estonian party names no longer carry the Nasdaq CSD account prefix ("Omanikukonto:", "Lihtkonto:"); the register's text is kept in an annotation.
readme: Estonian shareholders filed through a Nasdaq CSD account are named without the "Omanikukonto:" / "Lihtkonto:" prefix, on every path and in the corrected 19 October 2026 dataset.
tests: backend +16
---
The e-Business Register files some holders under their Nasdaq CSD securities account and puts the account type before the name ("Omanikukonto: EESTI ENERGIA AS", an owner account; "Lihtkonto: Rahandusministeerium", a simple account). `map_ariregister` copied it as filed, so graph nodes, the "Read as text" tree, BackgroundCheck, the exports and the name screens all carried it, and the 19 Oct 2026 dataset named 1,227 people and 143 companies that way (the July file had stripped "Omanikukonto:" only, in the old sweep). Stephen's decisions (20 Oct 2026, Notion "Strip the Nasdaq CSD account prefix"): strip both prefixes; keep the register's text in a `transformation` annotation only, no line in the interest's details; the account type feeds no signal. `map_ariregister` now runs `_ee_strip_statement_name` on every statement it yields, so one rule covers every list and every tier (index, printable page, REST, SOAP history); the annotation targets `/recordDetails/names` (person) or `/recordDetails/name` (entity), gives the filed text and the account type, and `transformedContent` is the name used. Ids are built before the strip, from the same rows as ever, so nodes, edges, the page-path name merge (`ee_name_key`) and the history joins are unchanged — the tests compare the graph with and without the strip on all four paths. `sweep_estonia.py check` now fails on a name that starts with either prefix. The dataset release `dataset-estonia-2026-10-19` was corrected in place on 20 Oct 2026 by running the same function over its statements (1,370 changed, nothing else; Neo4j and RDF regenerated, byte-identical apart from those names); its "Names as filed" known gap is removed. Check in production on Eesti Energia AS (registry code 10421629): no party on its graph, its "Read as text" tree or its BODS export starts with "Omanikukonto:" or "Lihtkonto:", the ministry that held it is one node, and the annotation is on the statement.
