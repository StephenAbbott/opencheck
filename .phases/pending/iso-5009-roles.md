GLEIF's ISO 5009 list (v1.0.0: 2,127 codes, 32 countries) names each official role in one legal form: "Director" is UDU176 for a UK private limited company (ELF H0PO) and VXSNEB for a PLC (B6ES). It is vendored as `data/oor_codes.json` by `scripts/build_oor_codes.py`, and `bods/oor.py` writes the extension object `officialOrganizationalRole` (`scheme`, `code`, `name`, `legalForm`) on the interest. Stephen's decision (13 Nov 2026): the code goes on the interest, never in the person statement's `identifiers`. A role code is shared by every holder of the role and would merge them as people, `make_person_statement` moves non-document identifiers to annotations, and one person holds different roles at different companies. A code is written only from a reviewed per-source table, keyed on the company's legal form mapped from the register's own type (`CH_TYPE_ELF`, `EE_FORM_ELF`, `NZ_TYPE_ELF`), so a company without an LEI is covered. Coded: Companies House directors (corporate and nominee included), LLP (designated) members, LP general partners and individual PSCs, but not a corporate PSC (a relevant legal entity). Estonia's AS and OÜ: board members, procurists, liquidators, supervisory board members, founders and shareholders. An AS chair is left uncoded because the list's "Esimees" does not say which board. New Zealand limited companies: directors and shareholders. `tests/test_oor_codes.py` checks every table entry against the list's country and ELF. The survey of every source, with match-rate estimates (Norway AS, France INPI, Austria, Canada and New York next), is on the Notion ticket.
