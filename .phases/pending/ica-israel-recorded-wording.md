---
title: The Israel Corporations Authority change log says "recorded", never "filed".
readme: The Israeli change log's rows on History and in the watchlist now say a request was recorded on its day, because that day is the request's latest status update, not its filing day.
---
The first nightly delta of the change log (12 Nov 2026) showed the `ica-changes` day is the request's latest status update: a request whose status moves comes back under the new day and its old key goes (250 rows a night, up to 334 days old; 44 of the 236 new material keys that night were such moves). So "Share transfer filed on <day>" stated a filing day the register does not publish. Every wording now says "recorded": the History rows (`value_new` "… recorded", the `SHARES_FILED` label "Shares request recorded (parties not published)", the enum keeping its name), the watchlist lines ("recorded by the Corporations Authority on <day>", backend and frontend), the History tab's Israel note, the docstrings, `docs/watchlist.md`, `docs/time-machine.md` and CLAUDE.md. Change-log dates are `DateBasis.RECORDED`, so the tab reads them "as entered by the register" rather than "as filed". The index already counted a moved row's old key as withdrawn, so nothing is duplicated; no data or schema changes.
