Prerequisite for pKYC stage E (Notion "Key Companies House PSC persons on the PSC item id, not the etag"), found by measurement 4 on the stage E scoping page. `bods/mappers/companies_house.py` built an individual, corporate and withheld PSC party's `local_id` — and so, through `_stable_id`, its `recordId` — on the item's `etag` (`{company}:psc:{etag}`, `:psc-corp:`, `:anon:`). Companies House reissues an item's etag whenever the item is edited, so a share band moving from 25–50% to 50–75% on the same PSC gave the party a new `recordId`; the relationship, keyed on the party's statement id, moved with it; the graph drew a second node; and a statement-level diff (stage E Route 2) would read one ended + one added where the register recorded one change. The stable key is the PSC item id in `links.self` (`/company/{n}/persons-with-significant-control/individual/{id}`, `corporate-entity`, `legal-person`, `super-secure`) — the register's address for the item. `identifiers.ch_psc_item_id` reads it, `_ch_psc_local_id` keys all three paths on it (`{company}:psc:{item id}` — the company number stays, because an item addresses a filing at one company; grouping across companies is the officer id's job, Phase 193), and `psc_stream.psc_id_of` now reads the same helper, so a stream event and the mapped statement name an item by one key — what lets a `psc` watchlist trigger (Route 1) say which row the graph drew. A record without a self link (fixtures older than the links block; never seen on the live API) keeps the etag key it always had. The id is published the Phase 255 way: on a person as a `GB-COH-PSC` identifier that `make_person_statement` turns into an `identifying` annotation with the item's register URL (so `person_identifiers_from_annotations` and BackgroundCheck group on `GB-COH-PSC:<id>` as they do on an officer id), never in `recordDetails.identifiers`; on a foreign or unnumbered corporate PSC as an `identifying` note in words, its registration number staying the asserted identifier; a UK corporate PSC is keyed on its company number and shared with the related-company pass, as before, and carries no note. Every CH PSC party's `recordId` changes once; saved reports hold their frozen statements, the watchlist snapshot holds no statements, and the golden files are lower bounds and shapes, not ids. `tests/test_ch_psc_item_id.py` pins the stream and the mapper to one rule, the same PSC under two etags to one record, two items with one name to two, the annotation shape, the corporate and withheld paths, and the no-link fallback.
