title: Estonia — board members named because no owner was identified are no longer beneficial owners, and a company with none in the open data reads "none published".
readme: Estonian board and council members listed in place of a beneficial owner are mapped as senior managing officials who are not beneficial owners, and a missing filing now reads "none published".
tests: backend +16
---
Found by checking an article on reporting beneficial owners of an Estonian company (eestifirma.ee, 19 Oct 2026) against what OpenCheck does with Estonian data. Stephen's decisions (20 Oct 2026): (1) rows on the register's beneficial-owner list with manner-of-control code `J` (member of the highest governing body, no owner identified) or `C` (board member(s), no owner identified) keep the interest type `seniorManagingOfficial` but carry `beneficialOwnershipOrControl: false` — a new `kasusaaja_fallback` record kind in the regimes registry (`assert_false`) beside `kasusaaja_bo`. Until now every row on the list carried `true`, so about 104,000 fallback board members (13 Oct 2026 files) were listed by `opencheck_owners` as declared beneficial owners next to real 25% owners. The same rule applies to former entries from the register's history. The finding no longer counts them as beneficial owners — "no owner identified, 2 named" (short because of the 140-character cap) — and the person-companies list labels the role "Named because no beneficial owner was identified — …". (2) "beneficial owners due, none filed" becomes "beneficial owners due, none published": an entry can be restricted for a person at real risk of fraud, kidnapping, blackmail or harassment, or for a minor, and nothing in the open data says so (Estonia has no equivalent of Companies House's protected beneficial-owner type). The longer wording can push the "N ended ownership records" clause past the cap on a long sentence, as the rulings clause already could. The refresh workflow's log line and docs follow the wording; the index's `bo_obliged_none_filed` meta key is unchanged. Production check: an Estonian company whose BO list holds only J/C rows (Eesti Energia AS, as in the test fixture) shows its board members with `beneficialOwnershipOrControl: false` in the BODS export, `opencheck_owners` lists no declared beneficial owner for it, and the source row reads "no owner identified, 2 named".
