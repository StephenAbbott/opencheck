`asp_moldova` was degraded on 21 and 28 Sept 2026 with stub liveness, zero statements and ~45.3 s latency ("the weekly export is still being downloaded and indexed"), then green on 5 Oct with no change. Cause: it is the one bulk store the sweep's warm step did not cover — not a release asset, the adapter builds it from the register's weekly XLSX — so on a cold GitHub runner the probe's `fetch()` raced the 45 s `_BUILD_WAIT_S` against download plus build and lost whenever the route was slow. (The 12 Oct fail was a different cause, the export's shape, fixed in Phase 322.) `scripts/warm_bulk_stores.py` now calls `asp_moldova.warm_index()`, writing to `index_path()` (the temp directory, which both workflow steps share), and `source-health.yml` sets `OPENCHECK_ALLOW_LIVE` on the warm step because `sync_enabled()` reads it; the release-asset warmers do not. Two new tests pin that a warm step without live mode fails loudly naming `asp_moldova` and that a live one goes green on a built file; the path test checks the warm-up hands over `index_path` itself. Measured 19 Oct against that day's export: 119,350 companies indexed in 23 s, Kaufland S.R.L. with 3 administrators and 1 founder, and production's /lookup answering from the 19 Oct snapshot. Check after the next sweep: the warm step's log shows `asp_moldova: built`, and the row is ok/snapshot at well under 45 s.
