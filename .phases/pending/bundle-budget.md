title: CI fails a pull request that makes the homepage's first load bigger than its budget.
readme: A CI check now fails any change that grows what a first-time visitor downloads before the homepage can draw.
tests: vitest +9, vitest_files +1
---
Step 2 of the "Speed up first load of the opencheck.world homepage" ticket, after the lazy-routes change cut the entry chunk from 1,434 KB to 370 KB (495 KB to 97 KB brotli) and production's slow-4G first paint from 3.42 s to 1.50 s (measured 1 Nov 2026 with `frontend/scripts/measure-cold-load.mjs`, median of 5). Nothing had failed or warned while the entry grew: the changelog's 882 KB status table reached it through one static import. `npm run check:bundle` (`frontend/scripts/check-bundle-budget.mjs`, logic in `bundleBudget.mjs`) reads `dist/index.html` for the files a first visit must fetch before paint — the module entry, its modulepreloads and the stylesheet, skipping off-site URLs — brotli-compresses each at the highest quality, and compares the total with `frontend/bundle-budget.json` (121 KB, measured 110 KB). It is a ratchet in the design-lint shape: it fails on growth past the budget and on a budget left more than 25 % above the measurement; `--update` writes the measurement plus 10 % headroom and refuses to raise it without `--allow-increase`. The failure lists every file and its size. It runs in the frontend CI job after the design lint, on the dist the build step wrote. Checked by putting the static changelog import back: the check failed at 375.8 KB, naming the entry chunk.
