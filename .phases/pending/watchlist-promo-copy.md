Copy only. The "New features" band's Watchlist card (`frontend/src/lib/newFeatures.ts`) read "OpenCheck re-runs a check only when GLEIF or OpenSanctions record a change", and the `/features` Watchlist entry (`lib/features.ts`) said the same of GLEIF's daily delta and OpenSanctions' entity delta. Both were true until the pKYC work: the register tier (stage A) re-runs a watched Estonian company when the daily diff of the e-Business Register index names it, stage A graded every entry by materiality, stage B let a watch record what it expected, stage C added the periodic-review line, and stage D (Phase 375) the decision on an entry with a saved report pinned as evidence. The homepage sentence now reads "Re-run only when GLEIF, OpenSanctions or a followed register records a change; see whether it matters; keep your decisions on record." — one sentence, under the band's 140-character cap, no count that can go stale, no real company (`newFeatures.test.ts`). The /features sentence names the register diff beside the two deltas. Nothing else changed; the hardcoded-claims lesson applies — the next tier (the Companies House PSC stream, pKYC stage E Route 1, ticketed) will need the same two edits, which is why the homepage line says "a followed register" rather than naming one.
