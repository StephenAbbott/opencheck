title: A Bulgarian person filed without the register's person key joins the one person of the company it names.
readme: A Bulgarian officer or board seat filed without the register's person key now joins the beneficial owner or officer it names in the same company, so one person is one node.
tests: backend +11
---
Found in the Bulgaria MCP check of 19 Nov 2026: United Bulgarian Bank's BODS drew Petar Grozdev Andronov and Kristin van Reysenghem twice each. The index confirmed the cause: UBB's supervisory-board entries carry no `Indent`, so the builder gives them no company-scoped `person_key` and they could not join the keyed beneficial-owner entries. `bg_join_keyless` (in `bods/mappers/bulgaria.py`, used by `map_bg_tr` and `finding_bg_tr`) gives a keyless person entry the key of the one keyed person of the same company whose name is the same letter case and spacing aside, or, failing that, of which it is a shorter form (same first and last word, every word in the fuller name, at least two words; Stephen, 20 Nov 2026). A rule that fits two keyed people joins nothing; nothing crosses companies, kinds or the reverse direction (a keyless name longer than a keyed one was always a row naming several people). The person is spelled as the keyed entry spells it, and a `commenting` annotation says which entry was joined and why, and for a shorter form quotes the register's spelling and says Bulgaria publishes no birth date to confirm it. Measured on the 17 Nov 2026 index: 731 entries join by case, 2 by shorter form (UBB's van Reysenghem, a liquidator Andrew Blair), 1 left apart as ambiguous. Relationship local ids of the joined entries change. After deploy, check UBB (`5299000PCY1EP8QJFV48`) shows 14 people in its BODS export, not 16.
