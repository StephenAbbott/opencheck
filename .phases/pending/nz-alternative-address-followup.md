title: New Zealand Role Search withholds an alternative address, so the person view reads it from each company's own NZBN record, a match on an alternative address is graded at most medium, and an NZBN outage is no longer cached as an empty company.
readme: The New Zealand register card reads a withheld alternative address from each company's own record and never grades a match on one above medium.
tests: backend +28, vitest +4
---
MBIE fixed the Entity Role Search exposure OpenCheck reported (Case 00094468): from 18 November 2026 (sandbox since 27 October), a role holder with an alternative address comes back as `"physicalAddress": {"addressLines": []}` — no `pafId`, postcode or country, checked in the sandbox on 27 October for both the director and the director-and-shareholder record — and Role Search has no `addressType` and none is planned. MBIE's route to the address is the company's NZBN record. `nz_associations` now treats a block with no address line and no `pafId` as withheld (a postcode alone is never matched on) and, for the BackgroundCheck person view, reads the person's address from each company's full NZBN entity through the `nz_companies` adapter's fetch and cache (`follow_up_withheld`), for at most 25 companies per person in Role Search's order; a director-and-shareholder record carries the NZBN only on its shareholding. Each company row carries `address_source` (`role_search`, `company_record`, `not_checked`, `check_failed`) and the person carries `address_withheld_count`, `withheld_checked`, `withheld_not_checked` and `withheld_check_failed`; the card says how many were checked and how many were counted on the name alone. The company-wide view makes no follow-up reads, and a cached company-wide answer stands in for one person only when nothing was left unchecked. Stephen's decisions (27 Oct 2026): the full entity, not the `/roles` and `/company-details` sub-resources; 25 per person; and option (a) — where either side's address is `ALTERNATIVE` the match is at most medium, worded as a possibly shared service address, which changes the 1 October rule that only relabelled it. Cache keys moved to `nz_associations/v2/` and `person-v2/`. The `nz_companies` adapter raised nothing on an NZBN failure and cached the empty company it built from it: `_resolve_nzbn` and `_get_entity` now raise `NzbnUnavailable` on anything but an answer (a 404 is still an answer), nothing from a failure is cached, the Time Machine still gets `None`, and a person view with a failed company read is not cached. That matters for MBIE's production outage on 17 November 2026, 7pm–midnight NZ time (06:00–11:00 UTC). The fourth sandbox smoke test (Role Search does not serve the alternative address) skipped rather than failed after the fix; it is replaced by three that pin the withheld block, a residential holder's unchanged address, and the NZBN record supplying the address as `ALTERNATIVE`. After deploy: open a New Zealand company's BackgroundCheck card and confirm the rows read as before (no production holder has an alternative address until 18 November); on 18 November re-run the sandbox smoke tests against production keys and check a real alternative-address director.
