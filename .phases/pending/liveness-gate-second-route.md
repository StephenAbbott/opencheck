title: A register-status pair that has never disagreed can now pass the consistency gate, if the register has been seen reading a company as ended.
readme: The record-consistency gate gains a second way in for register status: enough comparisons plus proof that the register can report a dissolved company.

The 18 Nov 2026 `/consistencystats` reading (114,562 lookups, persisted since 1 Oct) found `liveness|ariregister|gleif` at 520 agree / 0 disagree, which fails the Phase 337 gate's "at least one disagreement" rule. Stephen kept the rule (a pair that has never disagreed has not shown it can: before the REST tier of 17 Oct the Estonian index held no deleted company, so every comparison read as agreement) and added a second way in for liveness only. A pair with no disagreement may enter when it has at least 200 decided comparisons and every register side (every side but GLEIF) has read a company as ended at least once in the counted comparisons. `consistencystats.record()` now counts, per sorted pair, how often each side of a liveness comparison read the company as ended, whatever the relation, stored under the reserved field `_liveness_ended` (relation `a`/`b`, no value) and served as `terminal_readings` on each liveness row. `consistency_allowlist.passes_gate` writes the whole rule down for the next reading; the allowlist itself is unchanged (`READING` stays 15 Oct) because the ended readings are counted only from this deploy. `docs/record-consistency.md` describes the second route. After deploy: `/consistencystats` liveness rows carry `terminal_readings`, all zero at first.
