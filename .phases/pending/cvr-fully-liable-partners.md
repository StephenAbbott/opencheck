BackgroundCheck Phase F, task 3 (Notion "BackgroundCheck Phase F — wider person-data source rollout"), the first new person source after the identity-first merge of Phase 393. Confirmed live against Datafordeler on 21 Oct 2026 before building: its CVR model carries exactly one relation to people, `CVR_FuldtAnsvarligDeltagerRelation` (fully liable partners of I/S, K/S and P/S firms); directors, boards, legal owners and beneficial owners are not in it (they are in Erhvervsstyrelsen's System-til-System distribution, a separate access, applied for in parallel). A participant's `CVR_CVREnhed` gives its type (`CVRPerson`; `AndreDeltagerePerson`, a person CVR holds without a CPR number; or `Virksomhed`, whose business key is its CVR number) and `CVR_Navn` its name — no birth date and no CPR number. The adapter now reads both for every participant in two requests per hundred (`in` filter, DAF-GQL-0016 caps it at 100) and the mapper emits a person keyed on the participant number (`DK-CVR-ENHEDSNUMMER`, in an identifying annotation per Phase 255) or a company keyed on its `DK-CVR` number (the same statement id as its own CVR record), each with one `otherInfluenceOrControl` relationship whose interests are CVR's periods — a partner whose every period ended is closed (Phase 317); no share and no beneficial-ownership flag, since CVR states neither. `dk-cvr-enhedsnummer` joins BackgroundCheck's `ONE_PERSON_PER_VALUE_SCHEMES`, because the participant number is the only thing that tells two same-name Danish partners apart: Willis Towers Watson Consultancy Services I/S has two partners named Mikkel Martin Nielsen with different numbers, whom Phase 393's same-source name rule would otherwise have merged into one card. Found on the way: Datafordeler answers 100 rows a page unless asked for more and the adapter never asked, so Willis's 132 partner rows were silently cut at 100, and the same unpaged query shape read names, addresses, industry codes and legal forms; every query now pages (`first`/`after`, up to `MAX_PAGES`, a reached limit raises rather than reading as complete). The current-partner list no longer inherits `_current`'s fall-back to every row, which would have read a firm whose partners had all left as still having them. A failed participant read is a `source_read` degradation with a fixed sentence and the bundle is not cached; the cache key moved to `cvr_denmark:v2:` so pre-change bundles (no participants, unpaged lists) cannot answer. Verified by replaying 24 answers recorded live through the real adapter and mapper: MADEGÅRDEN I/S (CVR 20652047) — two named partners; Willis (31796407) — 132 participants, 130 people and 2 companies, the two companies current and 130 relationships closed, BackgroundCheck listing 130 people with no cluster; Novo Nordisk A/S (24256790) unchanged at one entity statement. After deploy: open BackgroundCheck on a Danish I/S with an LEI (MADEGÅRDEN I/S has one) and check the partners show with the "Fully liable participant" role.
