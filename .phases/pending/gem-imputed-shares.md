title: GEM says which shares it imputed, and OpenCheck now says so too; a share of 0 is no longer published as an exact 0.
readme: A GEM share the tracker marks as an imputed value now carries a commenting annotation, and a GEM share of 0 is published as no stated size rather than share.exact 0.
tests: backend +30, vitest +1
---
GEM's Global Energy Ownership Tracker has carried a `Share Imputed?` column on its `Entity Ownership` and `Asset Ownership` sheets since August 2026, valued `known value, not imputed` or `imputed value`, and OpenCheck published every GEM percentage as `share.exact` whichever it was. Measured on the August 2026 xlsx: 4,465 of 25,206 entity holdings are imputed, overwhelmingly equal splits (100, 50, 33.3, 25, 20 …), and 20 imputed rows carry a share of 0 that means "size not known" (424 in September).

Stephen's decisions on the ticket (27 Oct 2026). **An imputed share keeps its number** and gets a `commenting` annotation on `/recordDetails/interests/0/share` quoting GEM's own label (`GEM_IMPUTED_SHARE_NOTE`), on both the relationships-CSV path and the parent-column path, placeholder owners included; the source card's "as filed" toggle now shows annotations on an interest's `share` as well as its `type`. **Integrated ownership reads an imputed figure unchanged** — pinned by a test; the figure's route does not count imputed edges. **A share of 0 publishes no `share` key at all**, the holder and interest staying exactly as for a blank cell, so the three live `small shareholder(s)` rows no longer say `share.exact: 0.0` of a holder they call a shareholder; the placeholder handling is unchanged, and the ESG card's GEM chips and the Subsidiaries tab's GEM list read 0 as no percentage too. **Both carriers ship now**, since the bucket has not refreshed since 29 July: the adapter reads the column from the relationships CSV under any header whose letters contain "imputed" (GEM's wording or a plain boolean), and `scripts/build_geot_projects.py` writes an `imputed_shares` section (`{subject: {party: share}}`, imputed rows above 0 only) into the artifact at the next rebuild. The two parsers are separate copies pinned equal by a parity test, the status-URL pattern.

One finding changed the shape of the fallback. Against the July CSV production reads, the pairs GEM imputed in August are almost all **blank** in the CSV (1,983 of 1,989; 6 carry the same figure), so the ticket's worry that today's 100s and 50s are imputations is mostly unfounded — the CSV seems to omit the imputed figures. The artifact therefore flags a holding only when the CSV row's own share equals the figure GEM imputed (within 0.05); a CSV row's own flag always wins; with neither, nothing is said. The committed September artifact predates the section, so nothing is annotated in production until the artifact is rebuilt (`python3 backend/scripts/build_geot_projects.py <September xlsx> --release "September 2026"`) or the bucket ships the column. After that: a GEM lookup whose owner GEM imputed shows the note under "Show N fields as filed".
