A French association (an *association déclarée*, which the RNE does not hold) had no history on OpenCheck. DILA publishes every JOAFE notice since 1901 and every deposit of an association's or foundation's annual accounts on its Opendatasoft portal (`jo_associations`, Licence Ouverte, keyless). `sources/joafe.py` reads it live per lookup, cached a day, keyed on the SIREN GLEIF files under any French authority: the association's RNA numbers come from the RNA index (a new `association_siren` index and `rna_index.rnas_for_siren`) for its notices, and the SIREN keys its accounts. `timeline/joafe.py` emits creation and dissolution as status changes, a new name and a seat transfer as Tier 2 identity changes, a change of purpose or statutes and every accounts deposit as administrative rows; a rectification replaces the notice it names and a cancellation removes it and says so. Personal data: a small association's seat is often a member's home ("chez …" in 386,438 notices), so only the commune and postcode of a seat transfer are kept, and no purpose text, street line or founder ever reaches the cache or the response. Foundations and endowment funds reach History through their accounts only (their notices are keyed on an RNF number nothing links to a SIREN), and notices before the RNA (2009) are not reached. A JOAFE that does not answer is said on the tab (`registers_unavailable` on `/history`), never read as "no notices". Stephen's decisions A–F are on the Notion ticket. After deploy, check Transparency International France (`969500AEH12X8M5XEO53`): two seat transfers (2011, 2013), its 2014 change of name and six accounts deposits.
