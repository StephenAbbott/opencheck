The second round of the ISO 5009 roles ticket, on the same rule as the first: a code goes on the interest as `officialOrganizationalRole`, only from a reviewed per-source table `{(ELF, register role): code}`, keyed on the company's legal form as the register states it. Norway's aksjeselskap (`NO_OOR`, ELF YI42): daglig leder, styreleder, styremedlem and varamedlem, person or company holder alike. France's RNE (`FR_OOR`): the SAS (INSEE 5710, ELF 6CHY) président, dirigeant and commissaire aux comptes titulaire, and the SARL "sans autre indication" (5499, V9QP) gérant and commissaire aux comptes; the INPI beneficial-owner skip is unchanged, so a code is only ever added to a row already published. Austria's Firmenbuch (`AT_OOR`): the extract carries no legal form, so `at_company_elf` reads the name's own form words (KG first, so "GmbH & Co KG" is a KG); GmbH managing directors, supervisory board members, liquidators and shareholders, KG general and limited partners, and Privatstiftung board and supervisory board members. Corporations Canada (`CA_OOR`): a director, by the governing federal Act (CBCA, NFP Act, Cooperatives Act, Pension Fund Societies Act). New York DOS (`NY_OOR`): the CEO of a domestic business corporation (PJ10, the list's one subdivision-scoped table). Left uncoded on purpose: SASU and EURL (no ELF of their own), the French SA (the list files its roles under QVPB while INSEE's SA categories have their own ELFs), Austrian chairs and Prokuristen, a CAC suppléant, a brreg nestleder and a foreign New York corporation's CEO. Statement ids of the coded relationships change once, as content-addressing requires.
