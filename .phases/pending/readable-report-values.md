From the 23 Nov 2026 production check (Notion ticket "ISO dates, raw codes and slugs in the Markdown report and BackgroundCheck"). One backend date formatter, `opencheck/ui_dates.py` (`ui_date`, moved out of `og_image.py`, which still exports it), words the "As of" line under "What each source says about owners", the "stood on" lines under integrated ownership and the sanctions 50% test, and the "What can be known" checked and held-as-of dates in the Markdown and PDF/HTML reports; the payloads keep their ISO values. The derived identifier keys get readable names from `opencheck/identifier_labels.py` ("Norwegian organisation number", "OpenCorporates ID" instead of "No orgnr", "Ocid"), and `tests/test_readable_report_values.py` fails when an adapter or the pipeline adds a derived key without a label. The report's signal list labels RECORD_CONSISTENCY "Records compared" (the chip's own label; the share card never draws it, so it has no `og_image.SIGNAL_STYLE` row) and names a signal whose source is `opencheck` "OpenCheck risk engine" instead of the raw id. On the frontend, `lib/readableDate.ts` gives birth dates and role dates on BackgroundCheck at the source's own precision ("25 Feb 1964", "Sept 2026", "1964"), rewords ISO dates inside a source's freshness detail ("RNS cut 18 Sept 2026, IGJ Sept 2026"), and `describeRoleInterest` capitalises the interest type ("Shareholding — 45.2%") and words a one-sided band ("over 25%", "up to 10%") instead of "25–?%". To check after deploy: the Markdown export for EIDE SEAFOOD AS (1595T7RFSMR9TFCTJX53) and the BackgroundCheck tab for GLYN LIMITED (984500E111FEA839D098).
