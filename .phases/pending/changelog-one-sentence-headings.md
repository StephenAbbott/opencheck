The /changelog parser took a row's heading as everything before its first em-dash. That suited the Phase 0–105 rows ("Scaffold — FastAPI + …") and nothing written since: a stamped row (`**Headline.** narrative …`) with an em-dash anywhere in its narrative showed a paragraph in bold, and one without an em-dash showed its headline and no summary, so cards changed shape from phase to phase. `lib/changelog.ts` now takes one sentence as the heading whatever the row's shape — the bold lead on a stamped row (its first sentence, if the lead runs to two), the short title before the em-dash on a legacy row when the dash falls inside the first sentence, otherwise the row's first sentence — and the sentence after it as the summary, with the commit citation removed before either is read. The card puts the phase number on its own line above the heading, marks the newest entry "Latest" and keeps the commit links. The intro no longer says an item appears only when it was "significant enough to earn a phase entry": since the release-steward workflow every merged pull request is stamped as a phase, so the page now says every change is listed, numbered at merge and shown once deployed. The phase label moves from an arbitrary 11px to the `oo-meta` step (design-lint baseline 2 → 1 for the page). Tests: five parser cases over the three row shapes and a jsdom test of the page against the real status.md (one heading per card, no markdown or citation in a heading, one Latest). 71 older rows carry no commit citation and still render without a link; unchanged here.
