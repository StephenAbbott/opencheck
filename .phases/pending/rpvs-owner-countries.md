---
title: Slovak RPVS beneficial owners carry their nationality and country of residence, which no RPVS owner ever had.
readme: Beneficial owners from Slovakia's public-sector partners register now carry the nationality and country of residence the register publishes.
tests: backend +6
---
Found by the mapper sweep on the Companies House country-of-residence ticket. RPVS publishes, per beneficial owner (KUV), `StatnaPrislusnost` (nationality) and `Adresa` → `Stat` (the country of their address), each a country with a Slovak name and an ISO 3166-1 numeric code. The adapter expanded neither, and `map_rpvs_slovakia` read keys RPVS does not have (`Obcanstvo`, `statObcanstva`, a string `Adresa` typed `service`), so **no RPVS owner ever carried a nationality or a residence**. RPVS caps `$expand` at depth 2, so from `Partneri` the address's country is out of reach. The adapter now makes one more read, of the owners collection (`/KonecniUzivateliaVyhod?$filter=Partner/Id eq …&$expand=StatnaPrislusnost,Adresa($expand=Stat)`), which is paged and followed up to ten pages. `kuv_countries_from` reduces each row to its two countries **before anything is cached**: street, number, town and postcode never leave the adapter. The mapper publishes the nationality and a `residence` address (country only), the ISO country where the numeric code resolves, else the register's own name with no code. If the owners read fails, the owners are still listed (they are the register's answer) without their countries, a `source_read` degradation says so, and nothing is cached, so the next lookup reads them again. The partner cache key moved to `partner-v2`. Live check on 3 Nov 2026, partner 7049 (Slovak Telekom): 31 owner rows, 31 country rows; the three current owners map to Austria, Germany and Hungary. The ICIJ jurisdiction gate now has a country for RPVS owners, as for Companies House.
