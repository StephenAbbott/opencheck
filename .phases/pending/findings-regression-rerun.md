The run of 23 Nov 2026 failed three subjects, and every failure was explained by a degradation the run itself recorded: Wikidata did not answer for Eli Lilly (no birth year for Katherine Baicker, so RELATED_PEP fell from high to medium) and Eesti Energia (one LEI confirmation instead of two), and Ørsted's GLEIF anchor was refused and fell back to the Golden Copy snapshot, which carries no OpenCorporates id, so OpenCorporates was not dispatched, no officers were read and both PEP codes, the PEP verdict clause and the card's RELATED_PEP disappeared. All three were back on fresh lookups the next morning; no golden file changed. `findings_regression.run` now looks a subject up once more, after three minutes and at most four times a run, when it failed and its `degraded_sources` holds an entry other than a screen's cap (`truncated`) or a missing key (`not_configured`); the second lookup decides and the first lookup's findings stay in the report (`rerun`, "Looked up again"). `fetch_lookup` also retries a 502 or 504, the edge answer that failed Birtley Investment on 17 Nov. Nothing in the engine or the golden set changed.
