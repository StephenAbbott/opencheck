title: A Companies House PSC relationship names the filing that declared it.
readme: Each Companies House PSC relationship now names the filing that notified it — form, transaction ID, filing date and a link to the document.

From the "Declarant and filing provenance in BODS statements" ticket (Open Ownership's basic disclosure information: who disclosed, and when). For every company in a Companies House bundle that has PSCs, the adapter reads one paged filing-history list (PSC, incorporation and confirmation-statement categories), and `bods/ch_filings.py` matches each PSC to the PSC01/02/03 that notified it and the PSC07 that ceased it, by date and name — never date alone, since Monzo Bank Limited's only PSC02 of 6 April 2016 names a different partnership than the one notified that day. Stephen's decisions (3 Nov 2026): the filing goes in a `commenting` annotation (url = the document, a sentence naming form, transaction and day, plus a `companiesHouseFiling` extension property), not the `source` block, so no statementId moves and the RDF export keeps it; every company in the bundle is read; and a PSC with no notifying filing is said, never signalled — the incorporation filing (NEWINC) is cited for a PSC named at incorporation, and for one notified before 26 June 2017 (when PSC changes still went on the confirmation statement) the first confirmation statement made up to on or after it, otherwise "no filing names this notification", and only from a history read whole. LLPs file the LLPSC forms, and one unique near match (a legal form changed since, a middle name left off) is accepted. Measured on eight companies and their chains: all 51 PSC events resolved — 25 to their own forms, 15 to a confirmation statement, 11 to incorporation. The declarant is always the company: Companies House publishes no presenter. The relationship card gains "Declared on … via PSC02, filed by the company (filing)" and a "Ceased" row. A failed read leaves the lookup whole. The sweep of other registers (Estonia, Latvia, Nigeria, Ukraine, GLEIF) is in `docs/dates.md`; only GLEIF's corroboration level and managing LOU are published and not yet carried. After deploy: check Monzo Bank Limited (09446231) — its Monzo Bank Holding Group PSC carries PSC02 of 30 Oct 2023.
