New adapter `pa_dos` (`sources/pa_dos.py`, `bods/mappers/us_pa.py`, `finding_pa_dos`, `_bh_pa_dos`) over data.pa.gov's Socrata dataset `xvd7-5r2c` (4.2M rows, one per business and listed party, monthly; "Public Domain U.S. Government", attribution requested not required — licence row `pa-open-data`). Measured 12 Nov 2026: 2,153 ISSUED LEIs file under RA000632; GLEIF writes the filing number bare and the dataset pads it to ten digits, so the adapter pads before it queries and the GLEIF mapper strips zeros, both under scheme `US-PA` (in `register_hops._NO_COUNTRY_ALIAS`, not in `RA_BY_COUNTRY` — the DC / NY rule). 1,836 resolve (85%), 1,832 agree on the name; a number off the list or naming another business is a note card, never a record. Decisions taken by the Florida precedent and recorded on the ticket for Stephen to confirm: the dataset states no status (it is the list of active registrations, which the Department says overstates active businesses), so none is stated and a `commenting` note says why; every title is a role, `seniorManagingOfficial` with the title as filed — "Owner", "Member", "Stockholder" and "100% Owner" included, never `beneficialOwnershipOrControl`; Organizer and Incorporator are officer-marked `otherInfluenceOrControl` (Phase 340); a party named with a legal form is an entity with no jurisdiction (Phase 295); only a domestic registration gets a jurisdiction and `foundingDate`, a foreign one's creation date is its Pennsylvania registration, said in a note. No party address is published. Not in this phase: History and the distinct-business view `3urc-uaba`.
