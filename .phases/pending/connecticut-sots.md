`ct_sots` reads three Socrata datasets on data.ct.gov per lookup: Business Master (`n7gp-d28j`, 1.31M rows) by the seven-digit business ID GLEIF files under `RA000600`, then Principals (`ka36-64k6`) and Name Change History (`enwv-52we`) in parallel by the record's id, cached a day only when all three answered. Measured 12 Nov 2026: all 684 ISSUED `RA000600` LEIs resolve (682 on the current name, one on a recorded former name, one a wrong number → note card); 13 of the 2,585 records file the number for a foreign business registered in Connecticut (UBS AG), so the deriver keys on the RA code. Principals are carried by name and designation only — Director → `boardMember`, Officer → `seniorManagingOfficial`, and the 84% with no designation (an LLC's members and managers or a partnership's partners, the register does not say which) `seniorManagingOfficial` worded that way; never ownership, never `beneficialOwnershipOrControl`. A principal's residence and street address, e-mails, phones and the Agents dataset are never asked for, and the schema refuses them. Status: a domestic business that is dissolved, forfeited, revoked, merged, cancelled or converted out is terminal, dated by the register's dissolution date; a foreign one's withdrawal is not terminal and its Connecticut registration date is not a `foundingDate`. Scheme `US-CT` (the DC/NY rule: in `register_hops._NO_COUNTRY_ALIAS`, not in `RA_BY_COUNTRY`). Licence `CT-Open-Data`: Business Master is Public Domain, the other two datasets carry no licence field — stated, not assumed; whether to ask the Secretary of the State to confirm reuse terms is on the Notion ticket.
