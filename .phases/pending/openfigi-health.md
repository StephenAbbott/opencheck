---
title: An OpenFIGI failure is counted and no longer hidden behind its name in the securities panel's sources.
readme: The securities panel names OpenFIGI as a source only when it answered, and /signalstats counts every OpenFIGI request by outcome.
tests: backend +7
---
OpenFIGI wrote on 11 Nov 2026 that its Mapping and Filter APIs move to new IP addresses (69.187.80.97, 69.187.27.102) on 1 December. OpenCheck calls `https://api.openfigi.com/v3/mapping` by domain and Render has no outbound allow list, so nothing had to change to keep the sanctioned securities panel running — but checking it showed that an OpenFIGI outage would have been invisible: `_openfigi_map` logged a warning and moved on, and `sources` listed `openfigi` whenever a key was configured, even when every request failed. Now `_openfigi_map` returns whether OpenFIGI answered, and `sources` lists it only then (an answer of "no identifier found" counts; a key alone does not). Every request is counted in `gleifstats` by a closed outcome vocabulary (`answered`, `http_429`, `http_error`, `unreachable`, `bad_response`) and served at `/signalstats` → `gleif.openfigi` with the last answered and last failed moments; the warning goes through `describe_exception`. The sanctioned list itself comes from OpenSanctions and the ISIN list from GLEIF, so an OpenFIGI failure only leaves rows untyped. After deploy, and again after 1 Dec 2026, open a securities panel (Rosneft) and check `gleif.openfigi.requests.answered` is rising and `last_failed_at` is empty or old.
