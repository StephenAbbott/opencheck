---
title: Botswana is no longer described as an OpenCheck source, after CIPA refused permission to republish its register data.
readme: The knowability table no longer lists Botswana as an OpenCheck source, after CIPA refused permission to republish its register data.
---
On 30 Oct 2026 the Companies and Intellectual Property Authority (CIPA) told Stephen by email that OpenCheck may not republish information from Botswana's register, so the `cipa_botswana` adapter and curated set (PR #335) were closed unmerged and the Notion ticket closed. Nothing of it ever reached `main` except the Botswana row of the "Beneficial ownership access status" table, added the same day for that adapter: it sat in the "OpenCheck source" group, named `cipa_botswana (curated set)` as its OpenCheck source and opened its notes "Added 30 Sept 2026 for the cipa_botswana adapter". The Notion row now sits in "Other", has no OpenCheck source and says why; `jurisdictions.json` is re-synced (only that row moves) and `docs/knowability.md` regenerated. The row's description of Botswana's regime — a public register, 10% threshold, ultimate natural person — stays, because it is a dated statement about what the register publishes, not data from it, and `opencheck_reads` was already empty for BW since no registered adapter has country BW. Standing rule: OpenCheck does not download, harvest, copy or re-host CIPA's BODS v0.3 register data without new written permission. Other mentions of Botswana in the repo are country names in third-party data (the SEC's EDGAR code table, MEIP and GEM subsidiaries) and the historical note in `docs/plan.md`; none relates to CIPA.
