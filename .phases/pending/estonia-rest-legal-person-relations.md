Fourth of the Estonia REST tickets, on the shared client. RIK's `GET /company/companyRelations` with `onlyValid=false` lists every company a legal person has been named on — shareholder, AS shareholder, founder, partner, pledge holder, share registrar — with dates and shares, deleted companies included; the index's reverse holdings know only current holdings in live companies. Eesti Energia AS on 17 Oct 2026: 25 companies, 13 of them deleted, 113 ended relations. New `sources/ariregister_relations.py` makes one call per legal person (Estonian registry code, or a foreign code with its alpha-3 country), reduces the answer to the company, its status and legal form and each relation's role (in the Estonian mapper's words), dates and share — the company's e-mail, phone, web site and addresses are dropped — and caches it a day; a failure is never cached. `GET /legal-person-relations` serves it, and `opencheck_person_positions` takes `EE-ARIREGISTER` and `EE-ARIREGISTER-FOREIGN`. Stephen's decisions (17 Oct 2026): legal persons only; fetched on click from a "Former and deleted holdings (e-Business Register)" control on the Subsidiaries tab and on an Estonian company in the Data drawer, never on every lookup, hidden on a saved report, listed and never drawn; current holdings are shown beside the former and deleted ones, to be tested in production and removed if the repeat of the Subsidiaries tab's fifth list reads wrong. One page of 500; a longer list (Nasdaq CSD SE: 8,562 companies) is cut and says so. A code on no company answers 204 with no body, which the shared client now reads as an answer. Fixtures are the real answers for Eesti Energia AS and the first five of Nasdaq CSD SE, with contact details kept on one row to prove they are dropped; neither carries a personal code. Production check: on Eesti Energia AS's Subsidiaries tab, open the control and confirm 25 companies, 13 deleted, and `/signalstats` → `ariregister_rik.outcomes` counting `company_relations|answered` once, and not again on a second click that day.
