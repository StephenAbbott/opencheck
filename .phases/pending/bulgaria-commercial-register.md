The new `bg_tr` adapter reads a SQLite index built by `scripts/build_bg_tr_index.py`, which replays every daily XML file the Registry Agency publishes on data.egov.bg under CC0 (dataset 2df0c2af-e769-4397-be33-fcbe269806f3) since 1 January 2021, in the header's CreateDate order. Each file is a set of field entries (Add / Erase), and a list entry carries the whole list, so the index keeps the latest state of every field. The 2008–2020 entries are listed on the portal and hold no file, so a field not changed since 2021 is not in the open data: the finding, a note on the company statement and the disclosure status ("None on record", never "Not public") say so, and a company with no entry since 2021 is a note card. data.egov.bg answers Bulgarian addresses only, so the index is rebuilt by hand with `scripts/refresh_bg_tr_index.sh` and shipped as the `bg-tr-index` release asset, downloaded at boot onto the persistent disk. Stephen's decision (18 Nov 2026): beneficial owners keep name, nationality, country of residence and the nature and extent of the control; a person's address, the register's hash of a personal number, a raw birth date and passport fields are dropped by the builder and refused by the bundle schema. One person on several lists of a company is one party through a company-scoped key derived from the hash. GLEIF files the ЕИК under RA000065, RA000730 and RA000731, all now `BG-EIK`; RA000066 (the FSC) files funds' codes and is not dispatched. Beneficial owners map to the register's rights codes, a stated percentage to a shareholding (exact or as a lower bound), a senior manager named for want of an owner to a non-BO senior managing official; partners' percentages only where contributions reconcile to the capital. After merge, build the index from the downloaded files and upload the asset: until it exists the source is never announced.
