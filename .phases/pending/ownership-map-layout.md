Found by the MCP testing of 7 Nov 2026 (Notion "MCP testing ahead of Connected Data London 2026", issues 5 and 6): Rosneft's `opencheck_ownership_map` drew fifteen owners in the 980-unit column `reporting/diagram._render` caps for an A4 PDF page — 65 units a node where a disc, a two-line name and a figure need about 110 — so names overprinted the next disc and the Arabic label sat on the legend; the two edges where Rosneft owns one of its own owners (RAO Rosneftegazstroy, RN-NeftKapitalInvest) were drawn straight from the subject back into the owner column, on top of the owners' own edges and labels; and Eesti Energia's figure sublabel "100% (Estonian e-Business Register (e-Äriregister)); 100% (O…" was cut at 60 characters. `_render` takes `max_h` (default 980, so the PDF figure is unchanged and a test pins its cap); the map passes `None` and the canvas grows: one node's whole drawing per row (`_node_extent`: disc, name lines, sublabel lines) plus a gap, a shorter column centred on the tallest, the legend under the last row. An edge whose owner sits at or right of the party it owns is a quadratic curve bowed below the straight corridor (`_return_curve`, Stephen's choice over leaving it out of the image), with its label on the curve's apex, and the map's summary says how many were drawn so. A node's sublabel holds one line per source's integrated figure (two at most, 56 characters each); `_node_boxes` reads the wrapped name and every sublabel line. Rendered from the production exports of the six homepage examples on 7 Nov 2026: Rosneft 2,826 px at 2×, no overprint.
