The FATF plenary of 28–30 October 2026 (the first under the UK Presidency) removed Bulgaria (BG), Côte d'Ivoire (CI), the Democratic Republic of the Congo (CD) and Monaco (MC) from Jurisdictions under Increased Monitoring after successful on-site visits, and added no one; the High-Risk Jurisdictions subject to a Call for Action are unchanged (DPRK, Iran, Myanmar). Verified against the three fatf-gafi.org pages published on 30 October 2026 (plenary outcomes, increased-monitoring-october-2026, call-for-action-october-2026). `FATF_GREY_LIST_CODES` drops from 22 to 18; the FATF signal summaries now read "grey list, October 2026" and "black list, October 2026"; `docs/risk-signals.md` lists the 18. CI, CD and MC remain on the EU's Article 29 list (Delegated Regulation 2016/1675) until the Commission follows, so `EU_HIGH_RISK_THIRD_COUNTRY` still fires for them — the FATF/EU divergence the separate signals exist for. Tests that used Bulgaria as the "FATF grey, not EU-listed" example now use Kuwait. In production: a company or owner registered in one of the four no longer carries FATF_GREY_LIST. `SIGNAL_RULES` is not bumped (as in June): a delisting is a change in the world, so a watched LEI re-run after deploy reports the signal retired.
