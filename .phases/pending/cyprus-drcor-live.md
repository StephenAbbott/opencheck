title: Cyprus's Registrar of Companies is wired into the lookup as a monthly index of data.gov.cy's open data.
readme: The Cypriot Registrar of Companies joins the lookup as an index of data.gov.cy's monthly CSVs — company, status, registered office and officials for every HE number GLEIF files.
---
Phase 47 built `cyprus_drcor` against a hand-built SQLite file (`extract_cyprus.py`, guessed column names, never wired) and left it in `_DELIBERATELY_UNREGISTERED`. This phase rebuilds it in the `jar_lithuania` shape and registers it: `scripts/build_cyprus_drcor_index.py` scrapes the dataset page for the three current CSV links (`organisations_N`, `registered_office_N`, `organisation_officials_N` — the portal renumbers them each publication), refuses a file whose header or row count is not the register's, and writes a ~235 MB index (62 MB gzipped) keyed on `(type_code, reg_no)` — the whole register: 497,410 companies, 62,406 business names, 10,351 partnerships, 3,727 overseas companies, 1.19M officials on the 29 Sept 2026 files. Unlike JAR and the ЄДР, data.gov.cy answers a datacentre, so `.github/workflows/refresh-cyprus-drcor-index.yml` rebuilds it monthly and replaces the `cyprus-drcor-index` release asset, which `warm_index()` downloads at boot (`CYPRUS_DRCOR_DB_URL`). The adapter reads a shared read-only connection with a schema check, is gated on the index (`covers_lei`), answers a number outside it with a note card (`COVERAGE_NOT_IN_INDEX`) and a SQLite error with a `source_read` degradation; `LookupDeriver` over RA000161 (`cy_he`, digits only — GLEIF files `ΗΕ 449149` in Greek letters, 200 of 200 ISSUED records sampled), `RA_BY_COUNTRY["CY"]` and a CY entry in the picker. The mapper writes the identifier as GLEIF spells it (`ΗΕ 449149`, Greek eta and epsilon) so the two statements meet on `CY-DRCOR`; the register's Greek type and sub-type go to `entityType.details`; the status (`STATUSES`: registered, reminder sent → live; strike-off notice, liquidations, administration → pending; struck off, dissolved → terminal, dated) through `apply_register_status`; officials typed by position — directors `seniorManagingOfficial`, secretaries and authorised persons `otherInfluenceOrControl` marked as officers (Phase 340), partners and a business name's owner `otherInfluenceOrControl` unmarked — a corporate official told by its name (Cypriot secretaries are mostly fiduciary companies) and carrying no jurisdiction (Phase 295). The register publishes no shareholders and no beneficial owners, and the finding says what it counts: "On the register since 2023-07-13, private company, 2 directors on file, 1 secretary." Knowability for CY now reads `cyprus_drcor`. Source counts 52 → 53. After deploy: the first workflow run must land before the source is announced (run it by hand), then a Cypriot LEI (BERN INVESTMENTS, `254900YI1OJO26HFY821`) shows the card with a dated snapshot chip and GLEIF's `CY-DRCOR` identifier corroborated.
