A new registered source, `rues_colombia`, reads Confecámaras' RUES dataset `c82u-588k` on datos.gov.co (9.47M rows, monthly, CC BY-SA 4.0) by NIT: name, legal form, status, first registration, chamber and matrícula, and the legal representative by name and role only — the representative's identity number and natural persons' rows are never asked for, and the bundle schema refuses both. GLEIF files a NIT or a chamber's matrícula under RA000093–RA000150 and RA000848; a NIT with a valid DIAN check digit is written `CO-RUE` `900396759-5` on both sides (value-schemed, the Chile rule) and is a FullCheck register hop; a matrícula is searched across every chamber and name-gated, with a note card when it belongs to another company or to several. Measured on the 583 ISSUED Colombian LEIs: 440 resolve by NIT, 60 by matrícula, 52 file no number and about 31 (funds, patrimonios autónomos, universities, the central bank) are not in RUES. Stephen chose to ship under the dataset's share-alike licence, flagged in the licence matrix and exports (a `cc-by-sa-4.0` row added to `licensing`). After deploy, look up Bancolombia (54930052NWJRPNMP1W58) and Banco de Bogotá (549300LYHE7LWSKDZT53) and check the RUES card, the `CO-RUE` corroboration with GLEIF and the share-alike notice; add a CO row to the knowability table.
