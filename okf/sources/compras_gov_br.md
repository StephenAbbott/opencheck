# Overview

Awards published by Compras.gov.br, the procurement system federal, state and municipal bodies in Brazil use, from the Open Contracting Partnership's Data Registry copy, rebuilt weekly. For a company (all its establishments, by CNPJ root): its latest and largest awards with buyer, sphere of government, value, procedure and legal basis, plus totals since 2021. Awards won, not ownership. Aggregator, cross-border database or ESG source.
