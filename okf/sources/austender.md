# Overview

Contract notices the Australian Government publishes on AusTender (contracts of AU$10,000 or more), from the Open Contracting Partnership's Data Registry copy, rebuilt monthly. For a supplier: its latest and largest contracts with agency, value, period, procedure and a link to each notice, plus totals since 2013. Matched on the ABN, or the ABN a company's ACN implies. Contracts won, not ownership. Aggregator, cross-border database or ESG source.
