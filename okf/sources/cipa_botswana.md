# Overview

Beneficial owners, shareholders and directors from the public register of Botswana's Companies and Intellectual Property Authority (CIPA), whose beneficial ownership register is structured in line with BODS. Curated example set: the companies GLEIF records under CIPA's Register of Companies, harvested from the register's public pages and mapped to BODS v0.4. Not a live feed — CIPA publishes no API or bulk file. Official national company / beneficial-ownership register.
