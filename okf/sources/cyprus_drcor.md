# Overview

Cyprus's register of companies, business names, partnerships and overseas companies, published as open data by the Department of Registrar of Companies and Intellectual Property and read from a monthly index of its three CSV files: name, type and sub-type, registration date, the register's status with its date, the registered office, and the officials — directors, secretaries, partners, authorised persons. No shareholders or beneficial owners are published. Official national company / beneficial-ownership register.
