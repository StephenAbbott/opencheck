# Overview

Israel's registers of companies and partnerships, published daily as open data on data.gov.il by the Israel Corporations Authority and read from an index of the two files: Hebrew and English names, type, status and sub-status, incorporation date, registered address, and for a company the violating-company mark, the government-company flag and the last annual report filed. No directors, shareholders or partners are published. Official national company / beneficial-ownership register.
