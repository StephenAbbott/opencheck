# Overview

Pennsylvania business registrations from the Department of State's list of active registrations on data.pa.gov, looked up by filing number: name, type of registration, creation date, registered address, and the governors and officers listed, by name and title. The list publishes no status and no owners. Official national company / beneficial-ownership register.
