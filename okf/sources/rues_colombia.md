# Overview

Colombian company records from the Registro Único Empresarial y Social (RUES), the joined mercantile registers of Colombia's chambers of commerce, looked up by NIT: name, legal form, status, registration and renewal dates, the chamber and matrícula, and the legal representative named on the record. Official national company / beneficial-ownership register.

- **Source id:** `rues_colombia`
- **Category:** cdd (customer due diligence / compliance)
- **Search kinds:** entity
- **Requires API key:** no
- **National register:** yes
- **Lookup keys (LEI-anchored dispatch):** `co_rues`
