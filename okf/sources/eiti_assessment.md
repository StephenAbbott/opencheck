# Overview

EITI's assessment of its supporting companies against nine expectations, including whether the company discloses its beneficial owners and publishes a list of controlled subsidiaries — plus the declared subsidiary list itself. Records a company's disclosure posture, not its ownership. Snapshot of the EITI global database, resolved to LEIs offline and reviewed by hand. Aggregator, cross-border database or ESG source.
