# Overview

Moldova's State Register of Legal Entities (Registrul de stat al unităților de drept), published every week by the Agenția Servicii Publice as a full open-data snapshot on dataset.gov.md. Company name, IDNO, legal form, registration date, registered address and activity codes, directors with their filed role, and founders with their share of the capital. No beneficial owners; joint-stock companies publish no founders. Liquidated companies, sole traders and peasant farms are not indexed. Official national company / beneficial-ownership register.

- **Source id:** `asp_moldova`
- **Category:** cdd (customer due diligence / compliance)
- **Search kinds:** entity
- **Requires API key:** no
- **National register:** yes
- **Lookup keys (LEI-anchored dispatch):** `md_idno`
