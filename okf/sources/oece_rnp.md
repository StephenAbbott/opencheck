# Overview

The partners and shareholders, legal representatives and governing-body members that each supplier to the Peruvian state declares to the Registro Nacional de Proveedores, kept by OECE (formerly OSCE) and published monthly as open data. A supplier's self-declaration to a procurement regulator, not a company or beneficial-ownership register: no percentages, no supplier name or status, and no document numbers of people (OpenCheck never stores them). Official national company / beneficial-ownership register.

- **Source id:** `oece_rnp`
- **Category:** cdd (customer due diligence / compliance)
- **Search kinds:** entity
- **Requires API key:** no
- **National register:** yes
