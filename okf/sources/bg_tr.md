# Overview

Bulgaria's Commercial Register and Register of Non-Profit Legal Entities, kept by the Registry Agency and published as daily open data: name, legal form, seat, capital, managers, boards, partners, sole owners, procurators, liquidators and beneficial owners. Read from an index of every entry made since 1 January 2021 — a field not changed since then is not in the open data. Official national company / beneficial-ownership register.

- **Source id:** `bg_tr`
- **Category:** cdd (customer due diligence / compliance)
- **Search kinds:** entity
- **Requires API key:** no
